Uma usenze imodeli ku-SketchUp, i-ModelCraft izokuzwakala ojwayelekile: umqondo ofanayo wobuso ne-push/pull, amakhiye amathuluzi afanayo, ibhokisi lenani lapho uthayipha khona ngaphandle kokuchofoza, ukuqagela ngama-akisi abomvu, aluhlaza nabuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, amaqembu, izinto ezingakhomponentiwe, i-Outliner, ulwazi lwento nezigcawu. Leli khasi libhala lokho okudluliselwa ngqo nokubaluleke kakhulu, okungadluliseki.

## Okuhluka okuqamba abantu

### Ukugcina kuwukulanda

Akukho Save ebhala phezu kwefayela owayivulile, akukho ingxoxo ye-Save As ngegama, akukho ukugcina okuzenzakalelayo, nofayela lokusekela. {{ui:Save}} ({{key:Mod+S}}) ilanda ifayela elisha le-`.modelcraft` njalo, ngokuvamile kufolda yakho yokulanda. Gcina kaningi, bese uqamba noma uhambise amafayela ngokwakho. Bheka [Vula, gcina, ngenisa nokukhipha](topic:files).

### Iyangenisa amafayela .skp, kodwa ayiwagcini

{{ui:Import}} ifunda amafayela e-SketchUp ngomfundi waye we-ModelCraft, ekhasini: amaqembu, amakhompoment, imibala, izakhiwo kubuso, amathegi namaphethelo athambile ayangena. Bheka [Amafomethi amafayela ngokuningiliziwe](topic:formats#skp-files). I-ModelCraft ayikwazi ukugcina i-`.skp`: ukubuyela emuva, khipha i-Collada kwi-ModelCraft bese uyingenisa ku-SketchUp.

### Akukho imenyu yokuchofoza kwesokudla

Donsa ngesokudla kuhambisa; ukuchofoza ngesokudla akwenzi lutho. Imiyalo ongayithola emenyu yomongo itholakala ku-{{ui:Entity info}} ({{ui:Reverse face}}, {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:Explode}}, {{ui:Make unique}}, {{ui:Make component}}) nasqenjini le-{{ui:Organize}} ethulusini.

### Amayunithi ahlala ebhaleni lesimo

Akukho Model Info noma ukukhetha isifanekiso. Ikhethi yamayunithi isezansi ngakwesokudla. Imodeli entsha iqala ngamafidi namainshi e-US nasezindaweni ezinye ngamamilimitha, ngokusuka ezilungiselelweni zolimi lwesiphequluli sakho. Amadesimali asebenzisa njalo ichashazi: `2.5m`, hhayi `2,5m`, ngoba ikhoma ihlukanisa amanani njalo.

### Ipeyinti linezinto zalo

{{ui:Paint}} ({{key:B}}) idaya imibala eqinile nezinto ezisemkhakheni {{ui:Materials}}: ibhricks, ungqimba wezindonga, ushingles wophahla, ukhuni, ilitshe nokunye, eyadwetshwa yi-ModelCraft. Awukwazi ukulayisha isithombe sakho njengempahla okwamanje; izindwangu ezifika ngamafayela angeniswe ziyagcinwa. Bheka [Penda ngemibala nempahla](topic:paint).

### Isikali sifana ngokulinganayo, ngaphandle kwamabhokisi okubamba

Ukushintsha usayizi akuboniswa ibhokisi elinezinsimbi zokubamba. Chofoza indawo ehlala ingaguquki, indawo yesikhombo, bese kuba usayizi omusha noma isici esithayiphiwe. Lokho kwenza ngokulinganayo ezindleleni zonke. Ukuze unwebe ngohlangothi olulodwa, sunduza noma hambisa ubuso, noma thayipha kabusha ububanzi, ukujula nokuphakama kweqembu ku-{{ui:Entity info}}, noma [shintsha usayizi ngesilinganiso](topic:dimensions).

### Ukujikisa kukhetha ipuleni lakho kusuka lapho ucindezela khona

Akukho protractor ehlala ebusweni njengoba uhambisa izinkinobho zomcibisholo ukukhetha ipuleni. Chofoza isikhungo ebusweni ukuze ujikise kuleyo puleni, noma emhlabathini ukujikisa ngokuthi kuqonde phezulu. Izinkinobho zomcibisholo zizungeza ukubuka ngaphambi kokuchofoza kokuqala.

### Ukuqagela kusuka emaphoyinteni owadlula kuwo

I-ModelCraft ibambelela kuziqondiso zama-akisi kusuka **kuphoyinti lakho lokuqala**, nakumaphoyinti, emigqeni nasezindaweni ngaphansi kwesikhombi. Ayilandeli iqondiso kusuka kuphoyinti owalidlule nje (ukuqagela okuqhubekayo "from point"). Ukuze uhambisane nekhona elikude, khiya i-akisi ngenkinobho yomcibisholo bese uhlola leliphoyinti: iphoyinti elikhiyiwe liyashelela lihambisane nalo. Noma beka [isiqondisi](topic:guides-measure).

### Okufanayo kusebenzisa umphetho owadlula kuwo ngaphambi kokuqala

Ukuqagela okufanayo nokuthwele kusebenzisa umphetho wokugcina okwadlula kuwo isikhombi **ngaphambi kokuchofoza kokuqala**, hhayi imiphetho oyihlola ngesikhathi udweba. Bheka [Ukuqagela nokubambelela](topic:inference#parallel-and-perpendicular).

### Amakhophi weqembu ayaxhunyiwe

Ukukopisha iqembu nge-{{ui:Move}} kwenza indawo yesibili yeqembu elifanayo, ngakho ukuhlela okukodwa kushintsha kokubili, njengakukhompoment. I-SketchUp yenza amaqembu azimele. Bheka [Ukuxazulula izinkinga](topic:troubleshooting#editing-one-group-changed-another).

### I-Space ayihambisi

{{key:Space}} ikhetha {{ui:Select}}, njengakwi-SketchUp. Ezinye izinhlelo zewebhu ukubamba i-Space kuhambisa; lapha akwenzi lokho. Donsa ngesokudla kuhambisa.

### Ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili kusondeza

Ku-trackpad, setha {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › Scrolling kube yi-Trackpad: iminwe emibili ijikisa ukubuka, {{key:Shift}} neminwe emibili ihambisa, ukukhama kusondeza. Bheka [Ukujikisa, ukushelelisa nokusondeza](topic:orbit-pan-zoom#on-a-trackpad).

Uma izandla zakho zazi izinkinobho ze-Blender kunaseSketchUp, khetha isitayela semenyu ye-{{ui:Modeler}}: {{key:G}}, {{key:R}} kanye {{key:S}} zisebenza ekukhethweni kanyekanye, {{key:E}} iyakhipha (push/pull), {{key:Shift+D}} iyaphinda, nebha yemenyu yekhanda (File, Edit, View, Select, Add, Object, Mesh) ithatha indawo yethuluzi. Uma usuvela ku-SketchUp, isitayela se-{{ui:Push-pull}} sinika ibha yemenyu efana nayo phezu kwamathuluzi afanayo. Bheka [Imenyu, izinkinobho ne-mouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).

### Izinhlanganisela ezigoqayo azihlukani

Push/pull ihlukanisa ubuso obungemuva kwento efanayo, kodwa izinto ezimbili ezihlukene ezisunduzwa zingene kwezinye azihlangani. Azikho i-Intersect Faces nezithuluzi zesisekelo.

### Okungekho

Ukudweba mahhala, i-Trim, i-Split kanye ne-Outer Shell, izinto zakho zesithombe, izandiso, nethuluzi lokwakha amashidi awakho kulokhu okwakhiwe. [Okungakakwakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet) kutshela okufanele wenze esikhundleni salokho.

## Izinkinobho ezigcinwa isiphequluli

I-ModelCraft isebenza kusiphequluli, ngakho izinkinobho ezimbalwa zesiphequluli, hhayi zohlelo. {{key:Mod+N}} ivula iwindi lesiphequluli (sebenzisa {{ui:New}}), futhi {{key:Mod+W}} ivala ithebhu. Uma unezinguquko ezingakagciniwe, isiphequluli siyabuza ngaphambi kokuvala. Bheka [Izinkinobho, izinkinamba kanye ne-trackpad](common:keys#shortcuts-the-browser-keeps).
