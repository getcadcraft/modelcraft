Lokhu kuguquke ku-ModelCraft 0.7, uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.6. Manje i-ModelCraft imodela ama-mesh kanye nejiyomethri ngokusunduza/ukuhudula, irenda ku-graphics card, futhi inendawo yomsebenzi ngayinye: yikhethe ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}} ngakwesobunxele kwe-toolbar.

## Okusha

### Ama-mesh

- **Ama-mesh.** Khetha iqembu noma ingxenye bese uchofoza {{ui:Guqula ibe yimesh}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}: iba yi-mesh, enezinhlangothi zanoma yisiphi isibalo samakhona ezingathwala ama-texture coordinates, ama-normal, imibala nezisindo. {{ui:Guqula ibe yijiyometri}} iyiguqula ibuyele. Ukuhlehlisa ku-mesh kugcina lokho kuphela okushintshwe yisinyathelo ngasinye. Ifayela eliqukethe i-mesh lidinga i-ModelCraft 0.7 ukuze livulwe. Bona [Ama-mesh](topic:meshes).
- **Indawo ye-Mesh.** Chofoza kabili i-mesh, noma khetha {{ui:Imesh}} ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}}, ukuhlela amaphoyinti, imiphetho nobuso: amaloop namawindi, ukuhlelwa ngokulinganayo, ukukhishwa, ukungena, ukuchamfer, ukusika i-loop, ukusika ngommese, ukuhlukanisa, ukujikeleza, ukuhlanganisa, ukuhlanganisa okukhethiwe, ukugcwalisa, ukugcwalisa igridi, ukuxhuma nokudonsa, nezilungiselelo zokugcina ezishintshwa ngemva kwalokho ku-panel. Qala ngendiza, ikhyubhu, ibhola, i-torus nokunye. Izinkinobho zokusunduza/ukuhudula nezinkinobho zomklami zombili ziyasebenza. Bona [Hlela i-mesh ku-Mesh workspace](topic:mesh-edit).
- **Izinguquli.** Bonisa, i-array, indawo yokuhlukaniswa, i-bevel, qinisa, i-boolean, ukuthuthela, ukuwelda, ukuncishisa, bushelelezi, i-lattice, ukuphindisa ijika nokuncinza kwenza kube nokubukeka okuhlukile kwemesh ngaphandle kokuyiguqula. Zibeke ngaphezulu komunye, uvule noma uvale ngalinye ngombono nasezithombeni zokurenda, bese {{ui:Sebenzisa}} enye uma uyifuna ngokuphelele. Bona [Izinguquli ku-mesh](topic:modifiers).
- **Ukuhlukaniswa nama-crease.** {{ui:Ugoqo}} ku-Mesh workspace igcina imiphetho ekhethiwe ibukhali ngaphansi kwendawo yokuhlukaniswa. Ukurenda kungasebenzisa amazinga amaningi kunalawo view, futhi kumesh omkhulu i-view ikhombisa izinga elingaphansi futhi iyakusho. Bona [Indawo yokuhlukaniswa](topic:modifiers#subdivision-surface).
- **Izinto zamajika.** Amajika e-Bézier, NURBS namajika amasegimenti aqondile akha ithubhu, ibhande, ukuncipha noma ipuleti eligcwele, nombhalo njengejika. Hlela amaphoyinti awo ku-workspace ye-{{ui:Ijika}}; {{ui:Ibe yimesh}} lapho ukwakheka sekulungile. Bona [Izinto zamajika](topic:curves).
- **Indawo ye-UV.** Maka imiphetho (noma vumela i-ModelCraft ibamake emiphethweni ebukhali), {{ui:Vula isikhumba}} ne-{{ui:Pakisha}}, noma phrojekthela ngebhokisi, isilinda, ibhola noma ngombono. I-UV editor ecaleni kwe-3D view ihambisa, ijikisa futhi ishintshe usayizi wezingcezu, futhi ichekha nemibala yokwelula ikhombisa umphumela. Bona [Hlela ama-UV e-mesh](topic:uv).
- **Ukubumba.** Indawo ye-{{ui:Ukubumba}} inama-brush okudweba, okubumba, okushelelezayo, okubamba, okuphefumulisayo, ugoqo, ukucindezela, ukulinganisa nemaski, nokubonisa ngaphesheya kwe-X, ne-{{ui:Imininingwane}} engeza amatriyangili lapho ubumba khona. Uxinzelelo lwepeni luqondisa usayizi namandla webrashi. Isiteleka ngasinye sisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bona [Bumba i-mesh](topic:sculpt).
- **Ukwakha kabusha nokuhlelwa kabusha.** {{ui:Yakha imesh kabusha}} yenza i-mesh yonke kabusha ibe ngama-triangle alinganayo, ivale izimbobo futhi ihlanganise izingcezu ezigqumayo. Ukwakha i-mesh ehlanzekile phezu kwemesh enemininingwane, yikhethe ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ukuhlelwa kabusha kwe-topology}}: amaphoyinti oyinyakazisayo afika phezu kwawo. Bona [Yakha kabusha](topic:mesh-edit#remesh).

### Ukudweba

- **Hlukanisa umphetho noma ijika.** Ithuluzi le-{{ui:Hlukanisa}} limaka izikhala njengoba uhamba emphethweni bese lihlukanisa ngokuchofoza noma ngenombolo othayiphile; ngomphetho, ijika noma isiyingi okukhethiwe, thayipha `/5`; {{ui:Ulwazi lwento}} › {{ui:Hlukanisa…}} icela inombolo, ethabuletheni. I-arc noma isiyingi kuhlukaniswa ngama-engeli alinganayo ejika langempela. Bona [Hlukanisa umphetho noma ijika zibe yizingxenye ezilinganayo](topic:divide).
- **Izibalo ku-value box.** Thayipha `2.5/3`, `12'6"+3/4"`, `(24'-0")/3` noma `2*450mm` noma yiliphi ubude, no-`90/2` ngama-engeli. Isibalo esingenakwenzeka sithi yini engalungile nokuthi kuphi. Bona [Izibalo](topic:value-box#calculations).

### Ukurenda

- **Ukulandelela indlela ku-graphics card.** {{ui:Renda isithombe…}} › {{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}} isebenzisa i-path tracer yayo ye-CadCraft ku-graphics card lapho isiphequluli sine-WebGPU; umugqa ongaphansi kwesithombe uthi yiyiphi injini esenze kona. {{ui:Misa}} ugcina isithombe, bese {{ui:Qhubeka}} iqhubeka kusuka lapho. Ngaphandle kwe-WebGPU isithombe silandelelwa nge-WebGL, njengakuqala. Bona [Ikhwalithi ephezulu](topic:render#high-quality).
- **Ukurenda ku-view.** Indawo ye-{{ui:Renda}} ilandelela i-view uqobo futhi iqale kabusha lapho uyizungeza noma uyihlela: renda {{ui:Indawo}}, chofoza ukuze {{ui:Gxilisa}}, setha {{ui:Imininingwane}}. Idinga i-WebGPU. Bona [Ukurenda ku-view](topic:render#render-in-the-view).
- **Izinto, izibani nezibhakabhaka.** Izithombe ezikhwalithi ephezulu zikhanyisa wonke ubuso nge-OpenPBR material model (ama-coat, ubunwebu, amafilimu amancane, ingilazi enemibala, ukukhanya). Nge-WebGPU kukhona nesibhakabhaka sefiziksi ne-{{ui:Umusi}}, {{ui:Isithombe sesibhakabhaka (HDRI)}}, namaprofayili wezibani e-IES wabakhiqizi. Bona [Izibhakabhaka namaprofayili wokukhanya](topic:render#skies-and-light-profiles-on-the-graphics-card).
- **Ikhamera njengeyesithombe.** Ukuchayeka kusuka ku-f-stop, ishutha ne-ISO kuzo zonke izindlela; nge-WebGPU futhi ukujula kwenkundla, ukubona okubili, ukulinganisa kwemibala, ama-panorama angu-360° (nasesiteleo) nama-cube maps. Bona [Ikhamera](topic:render#the-camera).
- **Qeda isithombe ngemva kokurenda.** Yinika izinga ({{ui:ACES filmic}}, {{ui:AgX}}, {{ui:Filmic}} noma {{ui:Ejwayelekile}}, ukuqhathanisa, ukukhanya, ukukhanya okuqhakazayo, i-vignette, ithebula lemibala `.cube`), xuba isibhakabhaka, ilanga nelungu ngalinye lezibani, bese ubheka izinto zokurenda ngayinye, konke ngaphandle kokurenda futhi. Ukuqhuqha okusele kuhlanzwa ngomhlanzi wesithombe kukhompyutha yakho. Bona [Qeda isithombe](topic:render#finish-the-picture) no [Xuba izibani ngemva kokurenda](topic:render#mix-the-lights-after-the-render).
- **Izindlela ezengeziwe zokugcina irenda.** I-PNG engu-16-bit, i-EXR enezingqimba eziningi enazo zonke izinto zokurenda nelungu lezibani, nefayela lokurenda (`.ccrender`) elivulwa futhi nokuxuba kwalo nokuqeda kwalo. Bona [Gcina isithombe](topic:render#save-the-picture).
- **Umsi, intuthu namaca.** Umsi nokufiphala okukhanyiswa yilanga nezibani, nemisebe yokukhanya; ikholomu eyodwa yentuthu noma ifu elilodwa; ama-caustic ngaphansi kwengilazi nezibuko; isampula elizenzakalelayo elimisa ama-pixel asehlanzekile. Kukhadi lezithombe kuphela. Bona [Umsi, intuthu, ukuqhuqha namaca](topic:render#fog-smoke-noise-and-caustics).
- **Uhlu lokurenda ne-MP4.** Engeza izithombe ezikhwalithi ephezulu namagalelo wonke wokunyakaza kuka-{{ui:Uhlu lokurenda}}; ziyarenda ngayinye ngemva kwenye futhi uhlu lusinda ukuvalwa kwekhasi. Idinga i-WebGPU. Amavidiyo angagcinwa njenge-MP4 lapho isiphequluli singayibhala khona. Bona [Uhlu lokurenda](topic:render#the-render-queue) no [Renda ividiyo](topic:animation#render-the-video).

### Ukubuka okuphilayo nokwethulwa

- **Ukubuka okuphilayo.** {{ui:Khipha}} › {{ui:Ukubuka okuphilayo}} ibonisa imodeli ngendlela yesikhathi sangempela ecaleni kombono, ilandela ukuhlela ngakunye nekhamera: izithunzi zelanga, izinto, izibani nokukhanya okubuyela emadongeni. {{ui:Hamba}} kuyo, donsa {{ui:Isikhathi}} no-{{ui:Inyanga}} ukuze uhambise ilanga, noma uyihambise ku-{{ui:Windi entsha}}. Isebenzisa i-WebGPU lapho isiphequluli sinayo, uma kungenjalo i-WebGL 2. Bona [Ukubuka okuphilayo](topic:live-view).
- **Ukuhlakaza.** Hlakaza utshani, izimbali, amatshe, izihlahla nabantu ezindaweni noma ngebhulashi, kusuka kwezinto eziyisikhombisa ezintsha nakwelinye ithala; amasethi alondolozwa efayeleni. Bona [Hlakaza izitshalo, amatshe nabantu](topic:live-view#scatter-plants-rocks-and-people).
- **Yabelana.** I-panorama ye-360°, uhambo lwe-panorama onemicibisho phakathi kwezindawo, isibukeli esimi sodwa (ikhasi elilodwa lewebhu elivulwa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi futhi lingalayishi lutho kusuka ku-inthanethi) nevidiyo yendlela yekhamera, konke ngendlela yesikhathi sangempela. Bona [Yabelana](topic:live-view#share-panoramas-tours-a-viewer-a-video).
- **I-VR ngendlela yesikhathi sangempela.** {{ui:Hlola ku-VR}} ibonisa imodeli ngezinto zayo, isibhakabhaka, isithunzi selanga, izibani, izinto ezihlakazekile nokukhanya okubuyayo. Bona [Kubukeka kanjani](topic:vr-review#what-it-looks-like).

### Izinto

- **Izinto ezenziwe ngama-node.** Indawo ye-{{ui:Ukushadisa}} yakha izinto zefayela njengamagrafu ama-node: izithombe, umsindo namachekha, amaseli eVoronoi, amagagasi, izitsha, amathayili, izinkuni, imabula, amagradient, ukungcola nokuguguka kwemiphetho, ukusebenza kwemibala, izibalo, amamephu okubumba nawokujwayelekile, ibe yi-OpenPBR material model egcwele. Ukubuka kubonisa ushintsho ngalunye ngesikhathi esifanayo. Bona [Yenza izinto ngama-node](topic:shading).
- **Ithala lezinto nezethi zakho zokuthungwa.** Ipaneli ye-Shading ibala izinto zethala: amasethi okuthungwa angu-16 e-CC0, ngalinye linekhredithi. Chofoza okukodwa ukuze uyisebenzise, noma uyidonse ebusweni. {{ui:Izithombe noma i-zip…}} noma {{ui:Ifolda…}} yenza impahla kumamephu akho, ithole ngamagama amafayela awo. Bona [Izinto ezivela ethaleni](topic:shading#materials-from-the-library).
- **MaterialX ngaphakathi nangaphandle.** Khipha impahla njenge-MaterialX (OpenPBR Surface noma Standard Surface, izithungo njenge-PNG), futhi ungenise amafayela e-MaterialX nezithombe zawo. Bona [MaterialX](topic:shading#materialx).
- **Ama-decal nokupeyinta ukuthungwa.** Beka isithombe ebusweni njenge-decal nempahla yayo, bese upeyinta isithombe sempahla ngebhulashi elizungezayo. Bona [Ama-decal](topic:shading#decals) no [Peyinta ukuthungwa](topic:shading#paint-on-the-texture).
- **Ukubhaka ukuthungwa.** Bhaka ukuvinjelwa kokukhanya okuzungezile, ugoqo, ukukhanya nezinormali zento eyinemininingwane ibe yizithombe ezihambisana ne-UV ye-mesh, ngamaprosesa ekhompyutha. Bona [Bhaka ukuthungwa](topic:shading#bake-textures).

### Ukunyakaza

- **Indawo ye-Animate.** Bamba indawo, ukujikeleza nosayizi wento, ukukhanya, ukusakazeka nombala kwelungu ngalinye lezibani, nenguqulo yelensi, ngqo kupanele. Isikhathi sinebuka {{ui:Amajika}} (izibambi ze-Bézier, okuphindwayo) nebuka {{ui:Amaklipu}} ogcina izikhiye njengezenzo ukuze uzibeke futhi uzixube; izikhiye ezingaphezu kwesisodwa zingahanjiswa, zelulwe, zikopishwe futhi zinamathiselwe. Bona [Yenza noma yiyiphi impahla ibe yanyakazayo](topic:animate).
- **Izikhiye zesimo nama-driver.** Imesh iba nezinye izimo zayo, exutshwe ngamaslayida angabanjwa, noma ibekwe ezinanini ezinye ngesisho esincane. Bona [Izikhiye zesimo](topic:animate#shape-keys-morphs) no [Ama-driver](topic:animate#drivers).
- **Ama-armature.** Faka amathambo ku-mesh, vumela {{ui:Izisindo ezizenzakalelayo}} zabelane ngamaphoyinti phakathi kwawo noma upeyinte izisindo, bese umisa futhi ubambe amathambo; ukuqoshwa kwemigqa noma kwe-dual quaternion. Bona [Ama-armature](topic:animate#armatures-bones-that-bend-a-mesh).
- **IK nama-constraint.** Ukufinyelela (IK) nomgoqo, bheka, kopisha ukuguqulwa, imikhawulo, ingane yomzila nokulandela umzila, kumathambo nezinto. Bona [IK nama-constraint](topic:animate#ik-and-constraints).
- **Fiziksi.** Amaqembu nezingxenye ziyawela, ziyasikwa futhi ziyashaya njengamabhokisi noma amabhola, futhi {{ui:Bhaka fiziksi}} igcina umphumela njengezikhiye. Bona [Fiziksi](topic:animate#physics-drop-stack-and-collide).

### Amafayela

- **USD.** {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} ({{key:Mod+Shift+S}}) igcina imodeli njenge-`.usda`, `.usdc` noma `.usdz`, nefayela le-USD eyigcine i-ModelCraft livula futhi lingaphuli lutho. I-USD evela kwezinye izinhlelo ivula noma iyangeniswa njengama-mesh, amaqembu, izingxenye, izibani nezikhamera. {{ui:Londoloza}} ibhala futhi `.modelcraft`. Bona [Gcina, vula nekhiphe i-USD](topic:usd).
- **glTF, ephelele.** Amafayela e-glTF angena njengama-mesh ahlelekayo nama-normal, ama-UV, imibala yamaphoyinti nezithungo, ama-node njengamaqembu, izibani njengezibani nezikhamera njengemiboniso, kubandakanya namafayela e-Draco nemeshopt; i-`.gltf` ivulwa uma yehliswa ndawonye namafayela ayo. Ukukhipha kubhala ama-mesh, izithungo, izibani nezikhamera. Bona [glTF](topic:formats#gltf-glb-gltf).
- **Okungenisiwe kwe-FBX, PLY, STL ne-Alembic.** I-FBX 2011 nezinhlobo ezintsha (ama-mesh, amasethi e-UV, izinto, izithungo ezifakiwe, izibani, izikhamera), i-PLY ne-STL ngemibala yazo, nama-cache e-Alembic ngesampula lawo lokuqala. I-OBJ ingena ne-`.mtl` nezithombe zayo uma uzidonsa ndawonye. Bona [Amafomethi amafayela ngokuningiliziwe](topic:formats).
- **Okukhishwayo okwengeziwe.** I-PLY ngemibala, i-STL ngemibala yobuso obupeyintiwe, ne-OBJ nezithungo zayo njengefayela le-zip. Bona [Amafomethi amafayela ngokuningiliziwe](topic:formats).
- **Amafayela e-STEP.** {{ui:Ngenisa}} ifunda izingxenye nezinhlanganisela ze-STEP (AP203, AP214, AP242) njengama-mesh avaliwe ngosayizi wazo wangempela, yonke ingxenye lapho ifayela liyibeka khona. Bona [STEP](topic:formats#step-step-stp).
- **Amafayela e-.3dm.** {{ui:Ngenisa}} ifunda amamodeli e-`.3dm`: ama-mesh, ama-polysurface nokukhishwa (kusuka kuma-mesh agcinwe efayeleni, noma yenziwe ama-tessellate yi-ModelCraft), amajika njengemiphetho, amabhulokhi njengezingxenye namashidi njengamathegi. Bona [Ngenisa imodeli ye-.3dm](topic:import-3dm-files).
- **Amasolidi e-3D kusuka ku-DWG ne-DXF.** Ukungenisa umdwebo manje kuletha namasolidi wawo e-3D, izifunda nemizimba, ngalinye njengeqembu layo, ezivaliwe njengamasolidi. Bona [Model esitebeleni](topic:site-plan#what-comes-in).

### Ukuprinta nge-3D

- **Amathuluzi okuprinta.** {{ui:Hlola ukuprinta nge-3D (STL noma 3MF)…}} › **Amathuluzi okuprinta** unesikhombisi se-{{ui:Ukubuka kangaphambili kwezisekelo}} (ukuphuphuma, amakholomu noma izisekelo zesihlahla, cishe ukuthi kuzosetshenziswa kangakanani ukuthambo), {{ui:Ukujula kodonga}} (izindonga ezincane kakhulu ukuthi zingaprintwa, ezibhalwe nge-{{ui:Bonisa}}), {{ui:Gobhoza}} (izindonga zobukhulu obunikezwayo, namakhomba okukhipha amanzi) no-{{ui:Lungisa}} (zonke izilungiso zibhalwe; izingxenye ezigqumayo zihlanganisa kuphela uma uzicela). I-Gobhoza nakho i-Lungisa zinikeza iqembu lapho izinhlangothi ezilingene ziyizinhlangothi ezingasile, noma imesh uma umphumela ungavaleki noma unezinhlangothi eziningi kakhulu; umugqa wesimo uthi yiyiphi. Bona [Izisekelo, ukujula kodonga, ukugobhoza nokulungisa](topic:print-tools).
- **Isitayela sabaqalayo.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}} › {{ui:Oqalayo}}: izinkinobho ezinkulu zokuwisa izimo nemingxumo, ukuhambisa, ukuhlanganisa nokuthi {{ui:Iqembu}} ukuze izimbobo zisikwe, bese uhlola imodeli ukuze iprintwe. Bona [Isitayela sabaQalayo](topic:beginner).

Bona [Sebenzisa i-ModelCraft nabasizi be-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).
- **Umshini wakho.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › {{ui:Ukurenda}} › {{ui:Umshini wakho}} ibonisa ukubonakala, amaprosesa nememori esiphequluli esivumela i-app ukuthi isebenzise, nezinga elisetha lapho ukubuka okuphilayo kuqala khona. Bona [Umshini wakho urenda ngani](topic:your-machine).

## Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Amayunithi.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Amayunithi}} unohlu olulodwa ngalinye lobude, indawo, ivolumu, i-engeli nokuthambeka, nosayizi abanikezwa yizichazo zamayunithi (i-US survey foot iyiyunithi yayo). Vala amayunithi ongawasebenzisi nawo ahamba ezinhlwini zamayunithi, bese uwamaka ngenkanyezi ukuze umsebenzi omusha uqale kuwo. Ifayela lihlala linayo iyunithi yalo. Ku-ModelCraft uhlu lwamayunithi ecaleni kwe-value box lulandela iswitshi (iyunithi yemodeli ihlala ikhona), inkanyezi isetha iyunithi yemodeli emisha (uma i-ModelCraft inayo leyo yunithi), futhi ubude obuthayiphwe ngophawu lanoma yiyiphi iyunithi lufundwa ngaleyo yunithi. Bona [Amayunithi, amanani athayiphwayo nezinhlu zamayunithi](common:unit-lists).
- **Ukubonakala okuminyene nezinhlaka ezizicashele.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Ubukhulu besakhiwo}} › {{ui:Okuhlangene}} kulungisa okwengeziwe esikrinini (izikrini zokuthinta nama-fonti abuyisa izinkinobho zazo ezinkulu). Bona [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels#make-the-layout-denser).

## Akukatholakali kule nguqulo okwamanje

- **0.7 ayisayinwanga ngekhodi.** Ukusayina nephakheji ye-Microsoft Store kudlulele ku-0.8 ngoba ukuqinisekiswa kwesitifiketi akukafiki. Hlola i-SHA-256 eseceleni kwefayela ngalinye ku-getcadcraft.com/download. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa umfakeli, khetha {{ui:Ulwazi olwengeziwe}}, bese {{ui:Qhuba noma kunjalo}}. Ku-Mac, okokuqala, chofoza ngokwesokudla ku-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Vula}}. I-CadCraft Studio ayikakabi esitolo sezinhlelo, futhi akukakabi ukwakhiwa kwe-iPad noma i-iPhone.
- Uhlu lwama-engeli nokuthambeka ku-{{ui:Amayunithi}} luyagcinwa, kodwa akukho bhokisi elilufundayo okwamanje.
- Ukurenda ku-graphics card (WebGPU) kuhlolwe lapha nge-software stand-in kuphela, akukahlolwa ku-graphics card yangempela, ngakho renda okuncane kukhompyutha yakho kuqala. Okufanayo kubukeka okuphilayo, nevR isahlolwe nge-emulator ye-headset kuphela.
- Ngaphandle kwe-WebGPU akukho indawo yokurenda noma uhlu lokurenda, futhi izithombe ezikhwalithi ephezulu azinayo ukujula kwenkundla, isibhakabhaka sefiziksi, isithombe sesibhakabhaka, umsi, intuthu, ama-caustic noma ukuxuba izibani.
- {{ui:Renda isithombe…}} ayikadwetshi izinto ezihlakazekile okwamanje; zibonakala ku-live view, kuma-panorama, kubukeli nasevidiyweni.
- Izibani zinyakaza ku-view, kodwa ividiyo eyenziwe isebenzisa izibani njengoba zinjalo lapho iqala.
- Ama-bone nokunyakaza akufundwa noma kubhalwa kumafayela e-glTF, FBX noma USD okwamanje (amamodeli anesikhumba angena esimweni sokuphumula, nezisindo zawo), futhi izinto ze-glTF ne-FBX ngaphezu kombala oyisisekelo noma ithekisha azigcinwa okwamanje.
- Imibala ye-STEP namagama ezingxenye zokuhlanganisa akufundwa okwamanje. Ukungenisa kwe-`.3dm` kuhlolwe ngamafayela abhalwe ngethuluzi lefomethi, akukahlolwa ngamafayela avela ohlelweni lokumodela uqobo; izinto ze-SubD, umbhalo nezilinganiso azifakiwe.
- Ithala lezitshalo elivela ku-CivilCraft likhona ku-ModelCraft, kodwa ukubeka izitshalo kuwo akukakwakhiwa.
- Ukupeyinta ukuthungwa ebusweni bokusunduza/ukuhudula kuphindwa ngethayili yempahla; ukuze upeyinte ubuso obubodwa kuphela, guqula libe yi-mesh bese uyivula isikhumba.
