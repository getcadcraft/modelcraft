Konke ku-ModelCraft kuqala njenge-**face** esicaba: indawo evalwe imiphetho. Ungadweba ubuso ngqo nge-{{ui:Unxande}}, {{ui:Indilinga}} noma {{ui:Ipholigoni}}, noma wenze umbhalo womphetho ngamunye ngesikhathi nge-{{ui:Umugqa}}. Ubuso yilokho [Cindezela/donsa](topic:push-pull) okuguqulwa kube yizinto eziqinile.

## Dweba unxande

1. Cindezela {{key:R}}.
2. Chofoza ikhona lokuqala: phansi, noma kunoma yiluphi ubuso bomodeli.
3. Hambisa isikhombisi. Unxande uhlala phansi (noma ebusweni obuqale kulo), futhi ibhokisi lenani ({{ui:Izilinganiso}}) likhombisa ububanzi nokujula kwawo. Ithiphu lithi "Isikwele" uma izinhlangothi zilingana.

   Ukuze udwebe endizeni enye, nikeza kuyo: ubuso okudlula kulo ikhona lokuqala lwenza unxande ebusweni boba, futhi ukubamba indawo engaphandle kwendiza (iphoyinti, umphetho, i-axis eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka) kumisa unxande ngakho kokubili amakhona. Buyela ekubambeni indiza yokuqala bese ulala phansi futhi. Ukuze ugcine indiza eyodwa, cindezela ukhiye wompengo: {{key:Up}} igcina unxande ulingene (indiza eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka), {{key:Right}} indiza ebomvu, {{key:Left}} indiza eluhlaza. Cindezela ukhiye ofanayo futhi ukuze uvule.
4. Chofoza ikhona elingaphambili, noma thayipha izilinganiso ezimbili ezihlukaniswe ikhoma bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: `4m,3m`, `4000,3000` kumodeli yamamillimitha, noma `12',10'6"` ngamafidi namayintshi.

   Kuvela ubuso obunemiphetho emine ezungezile.

Izilinganiso othayipha ziya ohlangothini obekhomba kulo: uma isikhombisi sisecaleni lesobunxele lekhona lokuqala ngesikhathi uthayipha, unxande ukhula ngakwesobunxele.

:::warning
Ikhoma lihlukanisa izilinganiso ezimbili, ngakho alikwazi ukuba uphawu lwamadesimali. Thayipha `2.5m,3m`, hhayi `2,5m`.
:::

## Dweba indilinga noma ipholigoni

1. Cindezela {{key:C}} ukuze uthole indilinga noma {{key:N}} ukuze uthole ipholigoni.
2. Ukuze ushintshe inani lezinhlangothi, lithayiphe ngaphambi kokuchofoza: `12s` (noma `12` nje) bese {{key:Enter}}. Indilinga iqala ngezinhlangothi ezingu-24 nepholigoni ngezingu-6. Ithiphu likhombisa inani lamanje, futhi i-ModelCraft iligcina endilingeni elandelayo kuze kube yilapho ushintsha inani noma ushintsha amathuluzi.
3. Chofoza isikhungo, phansi noma ebusweni. Ukuze uligxilise ebusweni, nikeza ephakathi kobuso ("Isikhungo sobuso") noma ephakathi komphetho wabo ("Iphakathi") ngesikhombisi siphakathi kalobo buso: indilinga idwetshwa kuleso buso, ngisho nasekugcineni okuncane kwebhokisi elide.
4. Chofoza ukuze usethe umsakazo, noma uwuthayiphe ({{ui:Umsakazo}} kubhokisi lenani) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Indilinga iyipholigoni enezinhlangothi eziningi. Uma udonsa indilinga phezulu nge-Cindezela/donsa, izinhlangothi zayo ziyathambiswa ukuze isilinda sibonakale njengobuso obubodwa obushelezi.

## Dweba umnsalo

1. Cindezela {{key:A}} ({{ui:Umnsalo}}).
2. Chofoza lapho umnsalo uqala khona, bese lapho uphela khona (noma thayipha ubude phakathi kwawo).
3. Hambisa isikhombisi ukuze umnsalo ukhuphuke ngaphandle, bese uchofoza. Ithiphu lithi "Isigamu sendilinga" uma ukukhuphuka kuyisigamu sebanga phakathi kweziphetho. Noma thayipha ukuthi iphakathi limi kanjani: `2` bese {{key:Enter}}.

Ukuze udwebe umnsalo ozungeze iphoyinti, sebenzisa {{ui:Umnsalo osuka embindeni}}: chofoza isikhungo, bese lapho umnsalo uqala khona (noma thayipha umsakazo), bese lapho uphela khona (noma thayipha i-engeli ngamadigri, njengo `90`). Ubamba ngezinyathelo ze-15°.

Umnsalo oqalwe ebusweni udwetshwa kuleso buso; kungenjalo uhlala phansi, noma uma izinhlangothi zawo zimi ngobude obuhlukile umi uqondile. Umnsalo unezigaba eziyi-12; thayipha `24s` ngesikhathi udweba ukuze uthole ezinye. Umnsalo nawo uvala ubuso njengemigqa: umnsalo ophambi kwekhona lesikwele uyihlukanisa.

## Dweba ijika elibushelezi

{{ui:Ijika}} udweba umugqa obushelezi odlula kuwo wonke amaphoyinti ochofoza: intambo, indlela yerelingi, umphetho wensimu. Ingashiya phansi: amaphoyinti abamba emakhoneni, emiphethweni nasezindaweni zobuso noma yikuphi, nezinkinobho zompengo zikhiya okubomvu, okuluhlaza noma okuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka njenge-{{ui:Umugqa}}.

1. Chofoza {{ui:Ijika}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Dweba}}.
2. Chofoza amaphoyinti ijika elidlula kuwo. Thayipha `<0,0,1>` bese {{key:Enter}} ukuze uthole iphoyinti elingu-1 ngaphezulu kwelokugcina, `[x,y,z]` ukuze uthole indawo enembile, noma ubude ukuze uthole iphoyinti elikude kangaka ngasesikhombisini.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze kabili ukuze uqede. Chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala futhi ukuze uvale ijika. {{key:Backspace}} ususa iphoyinti lokugcina.

Ijika likhethwa njengento eyodwa. Ukuzeulishintshe, likhethe bese uchofoza {{ui:Hlela amaphoyinti}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}: amaphoyinti abonakala njengamasikwele. Chofoza eyodwa, bese uchofoza lapho ifuna khona (noma thayipha `<dx,dy,dz>`). {{key:Esc}} ubuyisela iphoyinti, futhi aphelise ukuhlela. Ijika elivaliwe elisicaba lenza ubuso; ijika elibumba ubuso alisakwazi ukuhlelwa.

## Phambanisa: umbheda ngaphakathi noma ngaphandle kobuso

{{ui:Phambanisa}} ikopisha umbhalo wobuso ngobude obuthile ngaphakathi kwawo (noma ngaphandle), kuma-ifreyimu, emibhedeni, emibhedeni yewindi noma kwingilazi yesikrini.

1. Cindezela {{key:F}} bese uchofoza ubuso, noma khetha ubuso kuqala bese ucindezela {{key:F}}.
2. Hambisa isikhombisi ngaphakathi kobuso ukuze uthole umbhalo wangaphakathi, noma ngaphandle kwawo ukuze uthole owangaphandle. Umugqa omusha uyalandela, futhi ithiphu lisho ukuthi kukhashana kangakanani.
3. Chofoza, noma thayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ubuso uhlukaniseka ibe ezimbili: ubuso bangaphakathi nefreyimu eliwuzungezile (noma indandatho entsha ngaphandle). Cindezela/donsa noma yiliphi kulezi ngqo: ngesikrini, dweba ibhokisi, uyicindezele ibe ubukhulu balo, uphambanise ubuso bangaphambili ngama-30 mm ngaphakathi bese ucindezela ubuso bangaphakathi kancane ukuze kube ingilazi. Izimbobo ebusweni nazo ziyaphambanisa, futhi umnsalo noma indilinga ihlala ijika elilodwa (elincane noma elikhulu).

- **Chofoza kabili** ubuso nge-{{ui:Phambanisa}} ukuze uphambanise ngebanga lokugcina futhi.
- **Imiphetho:** khetha ichungechunge lemiphetho exhunyiwe endizeni eyodwa (isibonelo imigqa emibili ye-L), cindezela {{key:F}}, hambela ohlangothini ofuna lona bese uthayipha ibanga: kudwetshwa ikhophi elilingene, namakhona ahlanganiswe ngombandela.
- Uma ibangaliphezu kwalokho ubuso obuvumayo, i-ModelCraft iyasho lokho futhi ayidwetshi lutho.

## Umnsalo, izindilinga namapholigoni ngamajika

Chofoza noma yiliphi isegimenti lomnsalo, indilinga noma ipholigoni bese ijika lonke liyakhethwa. {{ui:Ulwazi lwento}} bese likhombisa umsakazo waso, izigaba zawo, i-engeli (kumanmsalo) nobude bawo. Thayipha umsakazo omusha lapho ukuze uwulungise ngokuzungeza esikhungweni sawo; ubuso obudwetshwe endizeni yawo buyalandela. Uma ubuso bejika sebucindezelwe noma sebudonswe, umsakazo wabo uhlala njengoba unjalo.

## Dweba ngemigqa

1. Cindezela {{key:L}}.
2. Chofoza lapho umugqa uqala khona.
3. Chofoza lapho uphela khona, noma thayipha ubude bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ubude othayiphayo buya ohlangothini okhombisa kulo isikhombisi.
4. Qhubeka uchofoza. Ukuchofoza ngayinye kuqala umugqa olandelayo kusukela ekugcineni komugqa wangaphambilini.
5. Vala umbhalo wangaphandle ngokuchofoza iphoyinti lokuqala futhi. Lapho imigqa ivala ilupu elisicaba, igcwala ngobuso bese ithuluzi limisa ukuxhumanisa. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze umise noma nini.

Imigqa ilandela izinqondo ezibomvu, eziluhlaza nezibhakabhaka uma useduze kwazo; umugqa uphenduka leyo mbala bese ithiphu lithi "Kwi-axis ebomvu". Bheka [Ukuqagela nokubamba](topic:inference) ukuze uthole ukuthi ungakhiya kanjani inqondo nokubamba amaphoyinti.

### Lapho umugqa uya khona uma ungathintani nanoma yini

- Umugqa oqala ebusweni uhlala endizeni yalobo buso.
- Umugqa oqala phansi, noma endaweni engenalutho, uhlala ulingana nephoyinti lokuqala. Uma ubheka imodeli ngaphambili, kunalokho uya endizeni eqondile ebheke kuwe.
- Ukuze udwebe umugqa uye endaweni enembile, thayipha iphoyinti: `[x,y,z]` ephoyintini emodelini, noma `<dx,dy,dz>` ukuze uthole ukuphambuka kusukela ekuqaleni. Bheka [Thayipha izilinganiso ezinembile](topic:value-box).

## Hlukanisa ubuso

Dweba umugqa ngaphesheya kobuso, ukusuka emphethweni uye kwelinye (noma emphethweni uye kwelinye ngemigqa eminye). Ubuso buhlukaniseka ube ezimbili. Ngayinye igcina uhlangothi lwayo nombala wayo. Okufanayo kusebenza nounxande noma indilinga edwetshwe ebusweni: isimo sibe ubuso bayo ngaphakathi kobukhulu, obuthi manje bube nembobo.

Yile ndlela okwakha ngayo amagumbi ebhuleki lesitezi, iwindi edongeni, noma isinyathelo esitendeni: dweba umbhalo ebusweni, bese ucindezela noma udonse ubuso obusha.

## Ubuso obubheke ngendlela engafanele

Ubuso ngabunye bunengaphambili nangemuva. Ingemuva libonakala luhlaza okwesibhakabhaka olunsundu. Ubuso obudwetshwe phansi buhlala buqonde phezulu; ubuso obudwetshwe ebusweni obunye buqonde ngendlela efanayo nalobo.

Uma ubuso bukhombisa ingemuva lapho ulindele ingaphambili, likhethe bese uchofoza {{ui:Phendula ubuso}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}.

## Okubonayo lapho udweba

- Uphawu olunemibala lapho isikhombisi sibamba khona, negama lalo kuthiphu ngakwesobunxele esiphansi.
- Ukubuka kuqala komugqa noma isimo.
- Ubukhulu bangempela kubhokisi lenani. Ungathayipha phezu kwawo noma nini.

## Uma isimo singekho lapho ulindele khona

- Esikrinini sokuthinta, bheka [Ukuxazulula izinkinga](topic:troubleshooting#a-shape-starts-in-the-wrong-place-on-a-touch-screen).
- Uma umugqa ubambe okuthile okude ngemuva, zungeza kancane: amaphoyinti afihlwe ngemuva kobuso awambeki ngakho, kodwa amaphoyinti asemphethweni walo ayambeka.
- Cindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} ukuze uhlehlise isimo sokugcina; ukuhlehlisa kukodwa kususa umugqa owodwa noma isimo esisodwa.
