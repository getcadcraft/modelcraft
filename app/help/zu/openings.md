Ukuvula kudinga udonga oluneziqu ezimbili: ibhokisi, noma udonga owenze ngokuhudula isikhangu esincane nesinde siphakame. Uhlangothi olulodwa oluma lodwa alunalutho okuphusha kulo.

## Yenza udonga lokuzilolonga

1. Cindezela {{key:R}}, chofoza ikhona phansi bese uthayipha `6m,0.3m` (noma `20',12"`), bese {{key:Enter}}.
2. Cindezela {{key:P}}, chofoza isikhangu bese uthayipha `3m` (noma `10'`), bese {{key:Enter}}.

Usunodonga olunobukhulu be-300 mm (12 in).

## Dweba ukuvulwa

1. Zungeza ukuze ubone uhlangothi olude lodonga.
2. Cindezela {{key:R}}.
3. Hambisa isikhombi phezu kobuso bodonga, bese uchofoza lapho ikhona lephansi lewindi liyofika khona. Ithiphu ithi "On face" (Ebusweni).
4. Thayipha ububanzi nobude bewindi, njengo `1.2m,1.5m` (noma `4',5'`), bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

   Iwindi liyinhlangothi yalo ngaphakathi kobuso bodonga, obusanembobo ezungeze lo.

Ukuze ulibeke ibanga elinembile kusuka ekhoneni nasephansi, sebenzisa kuqala [imihlahlandlela](topic:guides-measure): ngo-{{ui:Tape}}, hudula imigqa yomhlahlandlela kusuka ezingxenyeni eziphansi nasemaphethelweni odonga, bese uqala isikwele lapho imihlahlandlela ihlangana khona (ithiphu ithi "On guide" (Emhlahlandlelweni)).

## Phusha kwedlula

1. Cindezela {{key:P}}.
2. Chofoza ubuso bewindi bese uluphusha lungene kudonga.
3. Hambisa uze uyime ebusweni obungemuva, bese uchofoza. Noma thayipha ubukhulu bodonga, `300` (noma `12`), bese {{key:Enter}}.

   I-ModelCraft iyayima ebusweni obungemuva ibe yiyo ivulekile: udonga manje lunembobo eqondile, nezinhlangothi ezine ezingaphansi (izinhlangothi, ikhanda nesibe) ezilimbozayo. {{ui:Entity info}} isabiza udonga ngokuthi yi-solid evaliwe uma lihlanganisiwe.

Uma uphusha ngaphansi kobukhulu obugcwele, uthola isigxobo esingeyona imbobo.

## Umnyango

Dweba isikwele uqale emphethweni ophansi wobuso bodonga (ithiphu ithi "On edge" (Emphethweni) noma "Endpoint" (Ephuzini lokugcina)), ukuze ukuvulwa kufinyelele phansi. Uphushe ngendlela efanayo.

## Amawindi amaningiosayizi ofanayo

1. Yenza ukuvulwa kokuqala.
2. Okulandelayo, dweba izikwele, bese ucindezela kabili ngayinye nge-{{ui:Push/pull}}: ukucindezela kabili kuphinda ibanga lokugcina. Bona [Phusha nokudonsa](topic:push-pull#repeat-the-last-distance).

## Uma kungasikanga

- Isikwele kumele sidweywe **phezu** kobuso bodonga. Uma sidwetshwe emoyeni phambi kwalo, singesiyo ingxenye yalo: hlehlisa bese usidweba kabusha, uqale ebusweni.
- Ukuphusha kuyama ebusweni bokuqala obusemuva kokuvulwa. Uma kukhona okunye okuvimbelayo ngaphakathi kodonga (ubuso bangaphakathi obusele kudwetshwe ngaphambili), kuyoma lapho; susa imiphetho yalobo buso bese uzama futhi.
- Ijiyomethri eseqenjini elihlukile ayisikwa. Vula iqembu elibamba udonga bese udweba ukuvulwa phakathi kwalo.
