## Okuzokwenziwa

Guqula amabhlokhi owamodele nge-push/pull ube yizinketho zobuningi eziqondwa yi-BimCraft, ukuze umqondo uqhubeke njengemodeli yesakhiwo.

## Ngaphambi kokuqala

Yenza ubuningi ngabunye **iqembu** noma **ingxenye** bese uyinika igama. Ngayinye iba inketho eyodwa. Ijiyometri engahlelekile ayithunyelwa.

## Thumela

1. Khetha amaqembu okuzothunyelwa. Uma kungakhethiwe lutho, konke okuyiqembu noma ingxenye yezinga eliphezulu kuyathunyelwa.
2. Khetha {{ui:Export}}, bese {{ui:Massing to BimCraft…}}.
3. Thayipha {{ui:Name of the option set}} kanye no-{{ui:Floor height}}.
4. Hlola uhlu: izitezi zenketho ngayinye, isisekelo, indawo yonke yezakhiwo nevolumu.
5. Khetha **Thumela ku-BimCraft** ukuze uvule i-BimCraft ethebhini elisha ngezinketho, noma {{ui:Save as IFC}} ukulanda ifayela uvule lapho ngokwakho.

Ku-BimCraft izinketho zibhalwe ngaphansi kwe-"Massing options in the model", lapho enye ingenziwa inketho yamanje futhi ichazeke ibe ngodonga nezitezi.

## Indlela isolidi eba ubuningi

- **Izitezi** ziwukuphakama kwesolidi ehlukaniswe ngokuphakama kwesitezi, izungezwe, futhi okungenani ibe eyodwa. Izitezi bese zabelana ngokuphakama kwesolidi ngokulinganayo, ngakho ibhlokhi ye-10 m ngezitezi ezingu-3 m inezitezi ezi-3 ezingu-3.33 m.
- **Indawo yesitezi** iyindawo yesigaba esiqondile sesolidi ngokwayo ezingeni lalesi sitezi. Ibhlokhi eyehla emuva inezitezi ezingaphezulu ezincane, futhi indawo yonke yezakhiwo iyisamba salokho.
- **Isisekelo** yisikhala sesigaba esingaphezulu kwesisekelo sesolidi.
- **Ivolumu** yisolidi ngokwayo.

Ku-IFC ngayinye inketho iyi-building element proxy (isisekelo esiphakanyiswe ekuphakameni kwesolidi) enesikhala esisodwa ngesitezi ngasinye esiphethe indawo yonke yalesi sitezi. Zonke izinketho zabelana ngesakhiwo esisodwa, nezitezi eziningi njengenketho ephakeme kakhulu.

## Izimo ezilula

Ibhlokhi elula emile ithunyelwa njengoba imodelwe. Konke okunye kuyathunyelwa nakho, futhi uhlu luchaza ukuthi kungani imo yaso ilula:

- **Izitezi zayo azilingani konke nesisekelo** (ziyehla emuva noma ziyathambeka): ubuningi ku-BimCraft yisisekelo esiphakanyiswe ekuphakameni okugcwele, kodwa izindawo zezitezi nevolumu kungokwesolidi ngokwayo.
- **Isolidi ayivaliwe**: izitezi ezingeke zivalwe azilinganiswa futhi zibalwa njengento. Vala isolidi ({{ui:Entity info}} ithi "Solid: Yes") ukuze uthole izibalo ezigcwele.
- **Isisekelo sayo sinemigqa engaphezu kwe-outline eyodwa**: eyikhulu isetshenziswa njengesisekelo.

## Okuhle ukukwazi

- {{ui:IFC for BimCraft (.ifc)}} ayiyokukhipha okuhlukile: ithumela izimo namagama kuphela, ngaphandle kwezitezi nezindawo.
- Akukho okulayishwayo. Ifayela linikezwa ithebhu ye-BimCraft kulo mshini.
