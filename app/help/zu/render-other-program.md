Isithombe se-{{ui:Renda isithombe…}} sidwetshwa **yizinjini zethu**: Ngokushesha ne-Path tracer, kusiphequluli nasku-CadCraft Studio. Olunye uhlelo lungadweba kuphela uma i-**add-on yenjini yokurenda** ilethe kuyo: bona [Engeza injini yokurenda nge-add-on](topic:render-engine-addons). Ukuthatha isigcawu kolunye uhlelo ukuze uqhubeke usebenza khona, sebenzisa [Vula isigcawu kolunye uhlelo](topic:render-open-in) noma [Thumela isigcawu kwinjini yemidlalo](topic:render-unreal).

## Dweba isithombe ngenjini ye-add-on

1. Vula i-add-on ku-Izilungiselelo › Okwengeziwe. Injini yayo ivela ohlwini lwe-Injini lwe-{{ui:Renda isithombe…}}.
2. Yikhethe. Izilungiselelo zayo zomqondo ziboniswa ngaphansi kohlu lweNjini.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Renda}}. Ku-Studio, ngokuqala i-add-on iqhuba uhlelo, i-Studio iyakubuza ngengxoxo yayo. Inqubekela phambili ibonisa ngesikhathi edweba; {{ui:Misa}} iyayiphelisa. Isithombe siyavela sibhalwe ngenjini eyidwebile.

Injini ebhalwe nge-JavaScript isebenza nakusiphequluli. Injini eqhuba uhlelo kule khompyutha isebenza kuphela ku-Studio; kusiphequluli iboniswa njengefayela okufanele silondolozwe.

## Okuthuthelwayo

Injini ithola **iphakheji**: imodeli njengefayela le-USD (nefayela le-glTF uma icela lona), nefayela lezilungiselelo elinamanani empahla eqondile, ilanga nesibhakabhaka, wonke amalambu avulekile ngamandla nemiyalo yokukhanya, ikhamera nokuchayeka. Olunye uhlelo ngalunye lukhanyisa, lunciphise futhi lukhethe ngendlela yalo, ngakho izithombe zifana ngekhamera, ilanga, amalambu nokuchayeka, kodwa hhayi ipikseli ngepikseli.

## Izinhlelo i-CadCraft engakaze iziqhube

I-CadCraft ayiqali uhlelo ngaphansi kwelayisense ye-GNU GPL, futhi ayikaze iyibeke njengenjini ohlwini lwe-Injini noma njengesihloso se-Vula ku. I-Blender ingenye yazo, ngakho ayikho ohlwini lwe-Injini. Ifayela le-USD noma le-glTF elilondolozwe kusuka ku-{{ui:Renda isithombe…}} lisavulwa kuyo njengakunoma iyiphi enye inhlelo efunda lawo mafayela.

:::note
Amakhamera asemaphanoramu nasezimbili kanye nesithombe sesibhakabhaka (imibala yesibhakabhaka isetshenziswa esikhundleni) akudlulisi kolunye uhlelo. Akukho okwenziwe kwenye inhlelo okubuyela emodelini.
:::
