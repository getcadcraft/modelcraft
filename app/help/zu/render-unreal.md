Ku-CadCraft Studio uhlu lwe-Engine lungabamba **ixhumanisi** eliya kwiphrojekthi ye-Unreal Engine. Indlela eyodwa kuphela: kusuka ku-CadCraft kuya ku-Unreal.

## Thumela imbonini

1. Khetha inkomba ye-Unreal ohlwini lwe-Engine. Okokuqala, i-Studio iyacela ukuthi ukhethe ifolda yephrojekthi yakho (leyo enefayela le-`.uproject`); iyayikhumbula.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Thumela}}. I-Studio ibhala i-`CadCraft/<file name>/` ngaphakathi kwephrojekthi: imbonini njengefayela le-USD, ifayela lezilungiselelo, amaphrofayili wokukhanya, kanye nezingqimba ezimbili ezincane.
3. Shintsha imodeli, londoloza bese uthumela futhi. Kuphela imbonini nefayela lezilungiselelo ziyabhalwa kabusha.

## Izinguquko zakho ziphephile

Isiteji evulwa yi-Unreal yisigaba esincane esenziwa kanye, sinezingqimba ezimbili ngaphansi kwaso: **eyakho** phezulu (`<name>.user.usda`, ingenalutho ekuqaleni, ayikaze ibhalwe kabusha yi-CadCraft) kanye nembonini ye-CadCraft ngaphansi. Izinguquko ozigcinela ungqimba lwakho zihlala lapho imbonini ithunyelwa futhi. Okukhanyayo nezinye izinkomba ozibeka kumazinga e-Unreal ngaphandle kwesiteji futhi azithintwa nazo.

## Ngaphandle kwe-Studio

Ku-browser, noma kukhompyutha lapho i-Studio ingasithola khona i-Unreal, khetha **Ifolda yephrojekthi ye-Unreal (.zip)** ohlwini lwe-Engine bese uchofoza {{ui:Renda}}. Igcina leyo folda njenge-zip: yikhiphe ngaphakathi kwefolda ye-`CadCraft` yephrojekthi yakho. Shiya **Faka amafayela esiteji** okokuqala; uyicime lapho ulondoloza kamuva, ukuze i-zip iqukathe imbonini nefayela lezilungiselelo kuphela futhi ungqimba lwakho lungabhalwa kabusha.

:::note
I-plug-in ye-Unreal evula isiteji, isebenzise ilanga, amalambu, amakhamera nokuvezwa, ilandele ukuthunyelwa ngakunye futhi irenderise ikhamera **ayikakhishwa**, futhi ayikaze isebenze ku-Unreal. Kuze kube yilapho ikhishwa, Thumela ifaka amafayela ephrojekthini, futhi amathuluzi e-USD e-Unreal angawavula. I-Windows isisetshenziswa sokuqala; i-macOS izolandela.
:::
