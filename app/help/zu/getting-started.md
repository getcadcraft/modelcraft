I-ModelCraft iyi-3D modeler yokuklama kokuqala: izifundo zokwakheka, ukuhlelwa kwamakamelo, izimo ezilula zezakhiwo nezithunzi ezikhishwa yizo. Udweba izimo ezisicaba, uzikhuphule zibe yizinto eziqinile, bese uqhubeka usebenza kuzo ngokudweba ebusweni bazo. Isebenza kusisiphequluli sakho sewebhu, futhi amamodeli akho ahlala esikhompyutheni yakho.

Uma usuvele wasebenzisa i-SketchUp, okuningi ozaziyo kuyasebenza: amanhlamvu afanayo amathuluzi, push/pull, ibhokisi lezilinganiso ezinembile, amaqembu nezakhi. [Abavela kwabanye abamodeli](topic:coming-from) bachaza okuhlukile.

## Vula i-ModelCraft

1. Vula i-ModelCraft kuguqulo yamanje ye-Chrome, Edge, Firefox noma Safari. Bona [Izisiphequluli nezikhompyutha](common:browsers).
2. Linda ukubuka kwe-3D kuvele: indawo ekhanyayo (noma emnyama) maphakathi nesikrini, nekhadi elithi "Qala ukumodela".

Isikrini sinebha yamathuluzi phezulu, {{ui:Uhlu lwezinto}} kanye {{ui:Izakhi}} ngakwesobunxele, ukubuka kwe-3D phakathi, {{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}, {{ui:Isitayela}} kanye {{ui:Imiboniso}} ngakwesokudla, nebha yesimo ngaphansi enebhokisi lezilinganiso namayunithi. [Isikrini se-ModelCraft](topic:workspace) sichaza ingxenye ngayinye.

:::note
I-ModelCraft idinga i-WebGL, isici sokudweba nge-3D sesiphequluli sakho. Uma maphakathi esikrini kuhlala kungenalutho futhi amathuluzi engasebenzi, bheka [Ukuxazulula izinkinga](topic:troubleshooting#the-3d-view-is-blank).
:::

## Hlola amayunithi kuqala

Bheka ekhoneni elingakwesokudla elingezansi. Ukhethi wamayunithi ukhombisa ukuthi amabanga alinganiswa ngani.

- Imodeli entsha iqala ngo-**amafidi nezintshi** uma isiphequluli sakho sisetelwe ku-United States (futhi nase-Liberia nase-Myanmar), futhi ngo-**millimitha** kwezinye zonke izindawo.
- Ukushintsha, khetha enye iyunithi kukhethi: {{ui:ft nama-in}}, {{ui:in}}, {{ui:m}}, {{ui:cm}} noma {{ui:mm}}. Imodeli ngokwayo ayishintshi usayizi; kushintsha kuphela indlela okuboniswa ngayo amabanga nendlela izinombolo ezingenayo iyunithi ezifundwa ngayo.

Inombolo oyithayipha ngaphandle kweyunithi isebenzisa iyunithi yemodeli. Kumodeli yamafidi nezintshi, inombolo engenayo iyunithi iyizintshi, ngakho `120` isho amafidi angu-10.

## Vumela i-ModelCraft ikuqondise kumodeli yakho yokuqala

Uqala ku-3D? Ekhadini elithi {{ui:Qala ukumodela}}, chofoza {{ui:Imodeli yokuqala eqondisiwe}} (itholakala futhi kumenyu {{ui:Ukubuka}}). Ikhadi elincane likuhambisa ngezinyathelo ezine, kusukela kwerektangili esicaba kuya kumodeli elungele iprinta ye-3D:

1. Dweba unxande phansi.
2. Mkhuphule ube yibhokisi.
3. Yenza ibhokisi libe into eyodwa (iqembu).
4. Hlola ukuprinta nge-3D, bese ulanda ifayela le-3MF noma le-STL.

Ikhadi liyawubheka imodeli, hhayi izinkinobho: liyaqhubeka isinyathelo sesiphelile, noma yikuphi okwenze ngakho, futhi liyabuyela emuva isinyathelo uma uhlehlisa. {{ui:Khetha ithuluzi}} likhetha ithuluzi kuwe. Vala noma nini ngo-{{ui:×}}.

## Dweba ibhokisi lakho lokuqala

1. Cindezela {{key:R}} ngethuluzi {{ui:Unxande}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Unxande}} eqenjini {{ui:Dweba}} lebha yamathuluzi.
2. Chofoza kanye esedwaleni eseduze nephakathi lokubuka. Leyo yisikhonkwane sokuqala.
3. Hambisa isikhombi kude. Unxande uyalandela, nebhokisi lezilinganiso (elibhalwe {{ui:Izilinganiso}}) liveza usayizi walo.
4. Thayipha usayizi bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: isibonelo `4m,3m`, noma `16',12'` ngamafidi nezintshi. Akudingeki uchofoze ebhokisini kuqala; ukuthayipha inombolo kuyithumela lapho.

   Uthola unxande osicaba phansi. Ungubuso: indawo enemiphetho ezungezile.

5. Cindezela {{key:P}} ngo-{{ui:Sunduza/hudula}}.
6. Chofoza unxande, hambisa isikhombi phezulu, bese uchofoza futhi. Noma chofoza unxande bese uthayipha ubude, njengo `3m` noma `10'`, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

   Unxande uba yibhokisi elinophezulu, ingaphansi, nezinhlangothi ezine.

7. Cindezela {{key:Space}} ubuyele kuKhetha, bese uchofoza phezulu kwebhokisi. {{ui:Ulwazi lwento}} ngakwesokudla lubonisa indawo yalo.

:::tip
Ungadonsa esikhundleni sokuchofoza kabili: cindezela endaweni yokuqala, donsa, bese ukhulula endaweni yesibili. Zombili izindlela zisebenza kuwo wonke amathuluzi okudweba.
:::

## Bheka indawo

- **Zungeza:** donsa ngenkinobho ephakathi (ivili), noma cindezela {{key:O}} bese udonsa ngenkinobho yesobunxele.
- **Shelelisa:** donsa ngenkinobho yesokudla, noma ngenkinobho ephakathi ngenkathi ubamba {{key:Shift}}.
- **Sondeza:** sebenzisa ivili. Lisondeza kulokho okungaphansi kwesikhombi.
- **Bona konke:** cindezela {{key:Shift+Z}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Sondeza konke}}.

Ku-trackpad noma kuskrini sokuthinta izinyathelo ziyahluka. Bona [Zungeza, shelelisa nesondeza](topic:orbit-pan-zoom).

## Hlehlisa

Cindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} ukuhlehlisa no-{{key:Mod+Y}} ukuphinda wenze, noma sebenzisa {{ui:Hlehlisa}} no-{{ui:Phinda wenze}} kubha yamathuluzi. I-ModelCraft ikhumbula izinyathelo ezingu-200 zokugcina ngenkathi imodeli ivuliwe. Umlando awulondolozwa kwifayela.

## Londoloza imodeli yakho

1. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} eqenjini {{ui:Ifayela}}, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}.
2. Isiphequluli silanda ifayela elibizwa ngegama lemodeli yakho, njengo `Untitled.modelcraft`. Ibha yesimo ikhomba lapho lifike khona: "Ifayela Untitled.modelcraft lilondoloziwe kuzilandiwe zakho."

**Ukulondoloza kuwukulanda.** Ukulondoloza ngakunye kwenza ikhophi entsha kufolda yakho yokulanda; ayibhali phezu kwefayela owalivulayo. Ukuze ugcine ifolda yephrojekthi ihleleke, hambisa noma uqamba kabusha ifayela lapho ngokwakho. [Vula, londoloza, ngenisa nekhiphe](topic:files) ichaza ngokwengeziwe ukuthi kungani nokuthi yini enye oyenzayo.

:::warning
I-ModelCraft ayilondolozi ngokuzenzakalelayo. Uma uvala ithebhu nezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, isiphequluli siyabuza kuqala ("Shiya lesi sayithi?"). Khetha ukuhlala, bese ulondoloza.
:::

## Thola usizo

Cindezela {{key:F1}} noma chofoza {{ui:Usizo}} ebhalweni lohlelo ukuze uvule lo sizo ecaleni kwemodeli. Lusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumana. Cindezela {{key:F1}} futhi, noma {{key:Esc}} ngaphakathi koSizo, ukuze uvale. Bona [Ukusebenzisa lo sizo](common:help).
