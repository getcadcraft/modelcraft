Ikhyubhu lokubuka lihlala ekhoneni elingaphezulu kwesokudla se-3D view. Liphendukela nombono, ngakho ilokhu likhombisa ukuthi ubheka kuphi: {{ui:Phezulu}} iphezulu, {{ui:Phambili}} ibheka eningizimu (ubheka enyakatho, ngomugqa oluhlaza lapho uyibheka), {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}} ibheka empumalanga (i-axis ebomvu). Ihlala ibonakala ngokukhanya kuze kube yilapho uyibheka.

## Bheka kusuka kohlangothi, komphetho noma ikhona

1. Bheka ikhyubhu. Ingxenye ephansi kwe-pointer iyakhanya: uhlangothi oluphakathi, umphetho (ukhanya kokubili izinhlangothi ezihlanganyelwa yiwo) noma ikhona (kuzo zonke izinhlangothi ezintathu).
2. Chofoza. Ukubuka kuyaphenduka kusukela lapho, kugcina iphakathi nezoom efanayo.

Kukhona izindawo ezingu-26 okufanele uzichofoze: izinhlangothi ezi-6 ({{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phansi}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Ngasemuva}}, {{ui:Ngakwesobunxele}}, {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}}), imiphetho eyi-12 nezikhona ezi-8. Ikhona inikeza ukubuka okujwayelekile okungxenye yezintathu; umphetho ubukeka uphakathi kwezinhlangothi ezimbili.

Ukuchofoza {{ui:Phambili}} kufana ne-{{ui:Ukubuka}}, {{ui:Phambili}} ku-toolbar, ngaphandle kokusondeza ukuze kugcwale. {{ui:Ukubuka}} isasondeza imodeli yonke.

## Zungeza ngokudonsa ikhyubhu

Donsa ikhyubhu ukuze uphendule ukubuka, njengokuzungeza ku-view uqobo. Ukudonsa akuthathwa njengokuchofoza, ngakho ungayibamba noma kuphi.

## Indilinga yekhampasi

Indilinga engaphansi kwekhyubhu iphethe N, E, S no-W.

- **Donsa indilinga** ukuze uphendule imodeli ezungeze eceleni eliqondile, njengoba ime esitezeni esijikelezayo. Indilinga ilandela i-pointer.
- **Chofoza uhlamvu** ukuze ubuke kuleyo nhlangothi, ugcine ukuthi ubheka kanjani phansi. Ekubukeni okuvela phezulu ngqo, uhlamvu ochofoza kulo luya ezansi kwesikrini.

## Ukubuka okubheke phambili: utolo

Uma ubheka ngqo esihlangothini, utolo olumbalwa luvela ezungeze ikhyubhu:

- Izinxantathu ezine **zilethe** ubuso baleso sicelo phambili: unxantathu ongaphezulu kwekhyubhu ukhombisa ubuso obabusendaweni ephezulu, njalo njalo.
- Izinkomba ezimbili **ezigobile** ngaphezulu kwesokudla zijikisa isithombe ngengxenye yesine yokujikeleza ngakwesokudla noma ngakwesobunxele, ingazishintshi ukuthi ubheka ubuso bani. Zisebenzise ukuze ujikise ipulani ukuze udonga olude lugijime lunqamule isikrini.

## Ekhaya

Inkinobho yendlu engaphezulu kwesobunxele sekhyubhu ibuyela ekubukeni okungxenye yezintathu {{ui:Iso}} bese igcwalisa imodeli yonke. {{key:Home}} yenza okufanayo lapho ikhyubhu inokugxila kwekhibhodi.

## Ngekhibhodi

{{key:Tab}} ukuya kukhyubhu, bese:

| Ukhiye | Kwenza |
|---|---|
| {{key:left}} / {{key:right}} | khetha uhlangothi, umphetho noma ikhona ezungeze ikhyubhu, ngezinyathelo zengxenye yesi-8 sokujikeleza (abafundi besikrini batshela ukuthi yiliphi) |
| {{key:up}} / {{key:down}} | khetha okuphakeme noma okuphansi, kuze kufike ku-{{ui:Phezulu}} noma kuze kufike ku-{{ui:Phansi}} |
| {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Space}} | buka kusuka kolukhethiwe |
| {{key:Shift+left}} / {{key:Shift+right}} | jikisa ukubuka ngengxenye yesine yokujikeleza ngendiza eqondile |
| {{key:Home}} | ukubuka kwekhaya |
| {{key:Esc}} | phuma ekhyubhini |

Okhiye acindezelwe ekhyubhini bahlala nekhyubhu: abaqali amathuluzi e-ModelCraft.

## Ucezu lwe-axis noma akukho

Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), ithebhu ye-{{ui:ModelCraft}}, bese ubeka **Isikhombi sekhona** ku-{{ui:Ikhyubhu lokubuka}} (okokuqala), {{ui:Isithonjana se-axis}} (uphawu oluncane lwe-X, Y, Z ngaphansi kwesobunxele: X obomvu, Y oluhlaza, Z oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka) noma {{ui:Akukho}}. Lesi siphequluli sikhumbula ukukhetha. Bona [Izilungiselelo ze-ModelCraft](topic:settings#corner-indicator).

:::note
I-ModelCraft ayinayo ukujikeleza okuphambili (roll): ngaphandle kokubuka okuqondile phansi nokuqondile phezulu, phezulu esikrinini kuhlala kuphezulu kumodeli. Ikhyubhu nezinkomba ezigobile zingajikisa ipulani; azikwazi ukuwisa ukubuka okuhlangothini ngaseceleni.
:::
