Ngenkathi ithuluzi lokudweba noma lokuhlela lisebenza, i-ModelCraft ibheka okungaphansi kwesikhombi ibambe into ewusizo kakhulu eseduze. Isimaki sikhombisa okuthole, futhi isiqondiso esingezansi kwesobunxele sichaza.

## Izimaki nezisiqondiso

| Isiqondiso | Isimaki | Ibamba |
|---|---|---|
| Iphoyinti lokuphela | Isiyingi esiluhlaza | Ukuphela komphetho |
| Iphakathi | Isiyingi esiluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka | Iphakathi lomphetho |
| Isikhungo sohlangothi | Isiyingi esizolile | Iphakathi lohlangothi ngaphansi kwesikhombi |
| Ihloko esihloko | Ngebo | Iphoyinti lesihloko eshiywe yi-tape |
| Umsuka | Okhilimu | Lapho ama-axes abomvu, aluhlaza nezolile ahlangana |
| Ku-axis ebomvu / Ku-axis eluhlaza / Ku-axis ezolile | Umugqa waleyo mbala | Umugqa usuka kwiphoyinti lakho lokuqala ngale ndlela |
| Ihambisana nomphetho / Iphambene nomphetho | Umugqa wepinki | Umugqa usuka kwiphoyinti lakho lokuqala uhambisana noma uphambene nomphetho okhunjuziwe (bona ngezansi) |
| Ukhiyiwe | Umugqa wepinki | Ukuqondisa okukhiywe ngekhiye lomcibisholo noma nge-{{key:Shift}} |
| Emphethweni | Isikwele esibomvu | Noma kuphi emaphethweni |
| Ohlangothini lesihloko | Ngebo | Noma kuphi emugqeni wesihloko |
| Ohlangothini | Idayamondi eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka | Noma kuphi ohlangothini |
| Ohlangothini lendiza | Akukho | Indiza oqale kuyo, lapho isikhombi sisesikhaleni esingenalutho |
| Emhlabathini | Akukho | Umhlaba (indiza ebomvu-eluhlaza), lapho kungekho okunye |

Lapho umugqa we-axis usuka kwiphoyinti lakho lokuqala udlula emphethweni noma esihlokweni, i-ModelCraft iyabamba ekuhlanganeni futhi isiqondiso sibiza zombili, njengo "On red axis, on edge" (Ku-axis ebomvu, emphethweni).

## Ukubamba okunqoba

1. **Amaphoyinti** cishe angama-pixels ayi-12: kuqala amaphoyinti okuphela, bese amaphoyinti ezihloko, amaphakathi, isikhungo sohlangothi ngaphansi kwesikhombi nomsuka.
2. **Izinqondo** ukusuka kwiphoyinti lokuqala: ama-axes abomvu, aluhlaza nezolile, nokuhambisana noma ukuphambana nomphetho okhunjuziwe.
3. **Emphethweni** noma emugqeni wesihloko.
4. **Ohlangothini.**
5. **Indiza yokudweba**, noma umhlaba.

Izinto ezifihliwe ngaphansi kohlangothi azikaze zibanjwe. Zungeza ukuze ubone iphoyinti ofuna.

## Ukudweba ohlangothini ukusuka kwiphoyinti

Unxande, isiyingi noma ipholigoni eqaliswe ekhonweni, ephakathini, emphethweni noma esikhungweni sohlangothi kudwetshwa **endizeni yohlangothi okhomba kulo**, futhi iyaluwusa lolo hlangothi. Umphetho ungeyezinhlangothi ezimbili, ngakho uhlangothi womphetho okhomba kuwo uyanquma: ukuze udwebe ekugcineni kwebhokisi elide, qala ephakathini komunye wemiphetho yalo nesikhombi siphakathi kohlangothi lokugcina.

## Ukuhambisana nokuphambana

I-ModelCraft ikhumbula **umphetho wokugcina isikhombi esadlula kuwo ngaphambi kokuchofoza kwakho kokuqala**. Ukuqala ekhonweni noma emphethweni kubalwa njengokudlula kulowo mphetho. Lapho ithuluzi selisebenza, eminye imiphetho ohamba kuyo ayisishintshi.

Ukudweba umugqa ohambisana nomphetho okhona:

1. Cindezela {{key:L}} bese ushayela umphetho ofuna ukuwulingana.
2. Hamba uye kwiphoyinti lokuqala, ngaphandle kokudlula kwezinye imiphetho uma kungenzeka, bese uchofoza.
3. Hamba ngendlela ehambisana nalowo mphetho. Umugqa uba mpinki futhi isiqondiso sithi "Parallel to edge" (Ihambisana nomphetho). Ukuphambana kwenziwa ngendlela efanayo, endizeni oyidwebela kuyo.

Uma kukhunjulwe umphetho ongalungile, cindezela {{key:Esc}}, shayela umphetho olungile, bese uqala kabusha.

## Khiya indlela

Ngemva kokuchofoza kokuqala kwethuluzi, ungagcina iphoyinti elilandelayo emugqeni owodwa:

| Inkinobho | Ikhiya ku- |
|---|---|
| {{key:Right}} | I-axis ebomvu |
| {{key:Left}} | I-axis eluhlaza |
| {{key:Up}} | I-axis ezolile (ngqo phezulu) |
| {{key:Down}} | Ihambisana nomphetho okhunjuziwe (bona ngenhla) |

Cindezela umcibisholo ofanayo futhi ukuze ukhiye. Ibha yesimo ithi "Ikhiywe ku-axis ebomvu. Cindezela umcibisholo ofanayo ukuze uvule."

Lapho ukhiyiwe, iphoyinti liyadonsa lisuka emgqeni lapho sikhomba khona isikhombi, futhi liyahlala lilingana namaphoyinti oshayela kuwo: shayela ikhona lebhokisi elinye ukuze udwebe umugqa ubomvu udlule kuleyo khona ngokuphelele.

**{{key:Shift}}** ikhiya noma yiyiphi indlela ozolile kuyo khona manje (i-axis, noma ukuhambisana), kuze kube sesiphezu kwayo. Yiyeke ukuze uvule.

Okukhiyiwe kusebenza ku-{{ui:Line}}, {{ui:Rectangle}}, {{ui:Circle}}, {{ui:Polygon}}, {{ui:Move}}, {{ui:Scale}} kanye ne-{{ui:Tape}}. Ngaphambi kokuchofoza kokuqala, amakhiye omcibisholo ajikeleza umbono.

## Ngaphakathi kweqembu

Lapho uhlela ngaphakathi kweqembu noma kwesakhi, ama-axes ayama-axes aleqembu (ayahamba nalo, futhi ayaphenduka nalo uma libekwe ngokuphenduka). Ukubamba kusathola amaphoyinti nemiphetho ngaphandle kweqembu, ukuze ukwazi ukuhlanganisa izinto nakwesinye semodeli.
