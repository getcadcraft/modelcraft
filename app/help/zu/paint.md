Imibala isiza ukuhlukanisa izingxenye zemodeli, kanti **izinto zokwakha** (ibhulikhi, isiding, uphahla, iwudi, itshe, ithayili, ukhonkolo, stucco, utshani, ingilazi, insimbi) zenza imodeli ibonakale njengento eyakhiwe ngayo. Zombili zipenda ngendlela efanayo.

## Khetha umbala

Isigaba {{ui:Amabala}} ngakwesokudla sibonisa umugqa wemibala eqinile, {{ui:Okuzenzakalelayo}} (leyo echazwe umugqa oqondile: akukho mbala) kanye nesampula {{ui:+}} yanoma yimuphi omunye umbala. Chofoza enye ukuze uyenze umbala wamanje. Ithuluzi le-Paint liqala ngawo.

## Penda

1. Cindezela {{key:B}} ({{ui:Penda}}), noma, ngaphandle kokuthi kukhethwe okuthile, chofoza umbala. (Uma kukhethiwe okuthile, ukuchofoza umbala kupenda okukhethiwe ngokushesha.)
2. Chofoza ubuso ukuze ububenze.
3. Chofoza iqembu noma isakhi ukuze upende leyo kopi: ubuso bayo obungenawo umbala wabo bathatha lo mbala. Ubuso obupendwe ngaphakathi kwayo bugcina umbala wabo.

Ukuze upende izinto eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, zikhethe kuqala bese uchofoza umbala, noma chofoza noma yiziphi zazo nge-{{ui:Penda}}. Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

| Bamba | Bese uchofoza ubuso | Yini ependa |
|---|---|---|
| — | | Lobo buso |
| {{key:Ctrl}} | | Bonke ubuso obuxhumene nalo obunombala ofanayo |
| {{key:Shift}} | | Bonke ubuso balo mbala weqembu osebenza kulo |
| {{key:Alt}} | | Akukho: uthatha lowo mbala esikhundleni salokho |

Ukuze ubuyisele ubuso ekungabi nalo mbala, khetha {{ui:Okuzenzakalelayo}} bese uchofoza kuwo.

## Penda ngempahla yokwakha

Isigaba {{ui:Impahla yokwakha}}, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Amabala}}, sidwesa ilabhulali yezinto zokwakha ngohlobo: ibhulikhi nebhulokhi, isiding, uphahla, iwudi, itshe, ithayili, ukhonkolo, stucco, umhlaba, ingilazi nensimbi. Hambisa isikhombi phezu kwesampula ukuze ubone igama layo. Chofoza eyodwa, bese upenda ngayo njengombala: {{key:B}}, chofoza ubuso, amaqembu noma izingxenye; noma khetha kuqala bese uchofoza impahla yokwakha.

- Esitayeleni se-**Okushadiwe** ubuso bubonisa ukuthungwa kwempahla. **Umugqa ofihliwe**, i-wireframe ne-x-ray zibonisa umbala wayo.
- Ukuthungwa **kuhamba ngokuqondile kuwo wonke ubuso** nama-**phezulu** kwawo: imigqa yebhulikhi ihlala iqondile kuwo wonke amadonga, izinsimbi zophahla zehla ngokuthambeka kophahla, nezinplanga zesisekelo zilandela i-axis ebomvu. Zinosayizi wangempela (ibhulikhi cishe ingu-215 mm ubude), ngakho zibukeka zilungile noma yikuphi okuhlukile.
- Ku-[umfanekiso owenziwe](topic:render) izinto zokwakha zigcina ukuthungwa kwazo, ukuqina kwazo, futhi, ngengilazi nensimbi, nokukhanya kwazo.
- Ukucindezela {{key:Alt}} uchofoza nge-{{ui:Penda}} nakho kuthatha impahla yobuso.

Ukuthungwa kudwetshwa yi-ModelCraft ngokwayo, ngakho kusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi futhi kukhululekile ukusetshenziswa kunoma yini (CC0).

## Izingxenye nemibala

Umbala opendwe ebusweni **ngaphakathi** kwesingxenye ukhombisa kuyo yonke ikhophi, ngoba amakhophi ahlanganyela inkcazo eyodwa. Umbala opendwe **ekhophini** ngaphandle (ngesikhathi isingxenye sivaliwe) ungowalelo khophi kuphela, ngakho amakhophi amabili engxenye eyodwa angaba nemibala ehlukene. {{ui:Ulwazi lwento}} ibonisa umbala wobuso, weqembu noma wesingxenye okukhethiwe.

## Imibala emafayelini

- Ifayela le-ModelCraft ligcina yonke imibala nazo zonke izinto zokwakha.
- Ezinye izifomethi zithola **umbala wempahla** (ukubukeka kwayo ekude) esikhundleni sokuthungwa: glTF, Collada, OBJ, DWG, DXF nokuhlola kokuprinta nge-3D. Ku-glTF, Collada ne-OBJ impahla igcina igama layo, njengo `Material_brick-red`.
- **glTF (.glb)** ne-**Collada (.dae)**: umbala ngamunye uba yimpahla. Ingxenye ebonakala ngemibala ehlukene ibhalwa kanye ngombala ngamunye. I-glTF ibhala futhi izithombe ezilayishiwe njengokuthungwa.
- **OBJ**: ukukhipha kulanda amafayela amabili, i-`.obj` ne-`.mtl` enemibala. Zigcine ku-folda eyodwa. Uma kukhona ukuthungwa okulayishiwe, i-`.obj`, i-`.mtl` nezithombe ziza njengo-`.zip` owodwa.
- **DWG** igcina imibala ngqo; **DXF** isebenzisa eyona eseduze kakhulu kwemibala eyi-255 ejwayelekile ye-CAD.
- **STL** ne-**PLY** zithwele imibala: i-STL ngesivumelwano se-VisCAM / SolidView (izinhlelo ezingayifundi ziyinaki), i-PLY njengombala kuyo iphoyinti ngalinye. Bona [Amafomethi amafayela ngokuningiwe](topic:formats).

:::tip
Ukuze umodele iskrini, penda uhlaka ngomnyama wamalahle nengilazi ngombala oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka ngemva kokuthi [uphambanise](topic:draw-faces) ubuso bangaphambili.
:::
