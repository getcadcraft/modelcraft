Amathuluzi e-ModelCraft afinyeleleka ngendlela efanayo noma yimuphi umzila. **Isitayela semenyu** sinquma lapho akhona esikrinini, igama lawo lapho, nokhiye abawasebenzisayo. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › {{ui:Menus, keys and mouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi siphequluli siyakukhumbula.

## Izitayela

| Isitayela | Okuzokutholela |
|---|---|
| {{ui:ModelCraft}} | Ibha yamathuluzi ye-ModelCraft (Ifayela, Dweba, Shintsha, Hlela, Hlela, Ikhamera) nokhiye bohlamvu olulodwa: {{key:L}} umugqa, {{key:R}} unxande, {{key:P}} push/pull, {{key:M}} hambisa, {{key:Q}} jikisa, {{key:S}} shintsha usayizi, {{key:E}} isifafazi, {{key:O}} zungeza. Okuzenzakalelayo. |
| {{ui:Push-pull}} | Ibha yemenyu (Ifayela, Hlela, Ukubuka, Ikhamera, Dweba, Amathuluzi, Iwindi) phezu komugqa wamathuluzi amakhulu, nokhiye bohlamvu olulodwa abafanayo. |
| {{ui:Modeler}} | Ibha yemenyu ekhanda (Ifayela, Hlela, Ukubuka, Khetha, Engeza, Into, Imesh) nokhiye bemodeli yemesh: {{key:W}} khetha, {{key:G}} bamba, {{key:R}} jikisa kanye {{key:S}} shintsha usayizi wokukhethwa ngesikhathi sinye ({{key:X}}, {{key:Y}} noma {{key:Z}} ikhiya i-axis lapho kuhanjiswa), {{key:E}} push/pull, {{key:B}} unxande, {{key:X}} susa, {{key:A}} khetha konke, {{key:Alt+A}} akukho, {{key:Shift+D}} phinda, {{key:Mod+G}} iqembu, {{key:H}} fihla. Inkinobho ephakathi izungeza; {{key:Shift}} kanye nenkinobho ephakathi ihambisa, {{key:Mod}} kanye nenkinobho ephakathi isondeza; inkinobho yesokudla ikhansela okwenziwa yithuluzi. |
| {{ui:Beginner}} | Iribhoni enethebhu ethi {{ui:Make}} enezinkinobho ezimbalwa ezinkulu: izimo zokuthi zidonswe, izimbobo, hambisa, jikisa nushintshe usayizi, lingana, {{ui:Group}} (izimbobo ezisikwa ezimumweni) kanye nokuhlola ukuphrinta nge-3D; amanye amathuluzi wonke ethebhini ethi {{ui:More tools}}. Inkinobho yesokudla izungeza, inkinobho ephakathi ihambisa. Bona [Isitayela sesiqalayo](topic:beginner). |

Isitayela sokhiye osenzeka ukuthi usazi kusuka ku-{{ui:Keys}} ngaphansi kwesokudla sokubuka nase-{{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} yisinqumo esifanayo: ukukhetha okhiye bomodeler kukhetha isitayela se-{{ui:Modeler}}, nokukhetha okhiye push-pull kushiya kusitayela se-{{ui:ModelCraft}}. Uma ukhethe okhiye bomodeler kuweminye inguqulo endala, i-ModelCraft ivula ngesitayela se-{{ui:Modeler}} kanye kanye, ngokwayo.

:::tip
Ukhiye ongowe-push-pull kodwa isitayela sakho sinika elinye iyelelo asenzi lutho, nomugqa wesimo utshela lapho iyelelo lalo lihamba khona ("In Modeler, Push/pull is E"). {{key:Delete}}, {{key:Esc}}, {{key:Enter}}, {{key:Tab}}, {{key:Space}} nezimpiko zigcina incazelo yazo njalo, izinombolo zihamba njalo ebhokisini lenani, futhi uma ithuluzi lihambisa into, izinkomba zalo zona zinqoba.
:::

## Yenza eyakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Customize…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Keys and menus}}, thola iyelelo (ibhokisi losesho liyasiza). {{ui:Add a key}} bese ucindezela ukhiye. Chofoza ukhiye ukuwususa.
3. Ngebha yemenyu, {{ui:Where it sits}} ihambisa iyelelo kwenye imenyu, noma kwi-{{ui:Keys only}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Mouse}}, setha isikhulu nokuthi izinkinobho zenzani ku-3D.
5. Yinike igama bese uchofoza {{ui:Save}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela sangaphakathi kwenza ikhophi yakho ("My Modeler"); isitayela sangaphakathi asishintshi. Ikhophi yakho ifunda okhiye ngendlela isitayela esaqala ngayo: eyenziwe kusuka ku-{{ui:Modeler}} igcina okhiye bokubamba, ukujikisa nokushintsha usayizi besebenza ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Imouse

- **Isikhulu semouse**: {{ui:Scroll up zooms in}} (okuzenzakalelayo) noma {{ui:Scroll up zooms out}}, nesivinini sakho. Ukucindezela kwe-trackpad kusondeza ngendlela eminwe yakho enyakaza ngayo.
- **Donsa ku-3D**: {{ui:Okwakho kokubuka ngakunye}} igcina izinkinobho zeModelCraft (inkinobho ephakathi izungeza, inkinobho yesokudla noma {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho ephakathi zihambisa). Noma usethe izinkinobho eziphakathi nezesokudla zibe zungeza, hambisa, sondeza noma azenzi lutho; {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho ishintsha ukuzungeza nokuhambisa. Inkinobho yesobunxele ihlala ithuluzini elisetshenziswayo.
- **Ama-trackpad**: {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › {{ui:Scrolling}} usaqhubeka unquma ukuthi iminwe emibili izungeza (trackpad) noma isondeze (imouse). Bona [Zungeza, hambisa nesondeze](topic:orbit-pan-zoom).

## Yithathe nawe

{{ui:Export}} ilondoloza isitayela esisetshenziswayo njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Import…}} lengeza elilodwa, elivela kwenye ikhompyutha noma kumsebenzi wakho. Ifayela elingeniswe lingakhomba kumyalo we-ModelCraft kuphela: okunye kukho kuyasuswa futhi kubalwe emugqeni wesimo. {{ui:Delete this style}} isusa esakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi siphequluli. {{ui:Use this style in every app}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi wezitayela yenza yonke i-app ivule ngesitayela esihambisanayo: {{ui:Push-pull}} ihambisana nezitayela zemenyu-yebha zezinye izinhlelo. {{ui:Modeler}} anayo ifanekiso kwezinye izinhlelo, ngakho ihlala njengokukhethwa kwe-ModelCraft.
:::
