{{ui:Renda isithombe…}} inokukhetha ngo-**Injini** phezulu kwe-Imodi. Izinjini zethu ezimbili zihlala khona zisebenza noma yiphi indawo; ama-add-on angangeza ezinye, futhi ku-CadCraft Studio uhlu luphinde lube nezinhlelo ezithatha isigcawu.

| Injini | Yenzani | Ikuphi |
|---|---|---|
| Ngaphakathi: Ngokushesha | Isithombe esisheshayo: usuku, izithunzi ezithambile, izinkombandla zengilazi nensimbi, ngemizuzwana | Isiphequluli ne-Studio |
| Ngaphakathi: Umlandeli wendlela | Ukukhanya okugqashuka phakathi kwezindawo, ngekhadi lakho legrafikhi noma iprosesa, umugqa wokurenda nezinye izikhompyutha ezisenethiwekhini lakho | Isiphequluli ne-Studio |
| Injini ye-add-on | Iyadweba bese ibuyisela isithombe: bona [Dweba isithombe ngolunye uhlelo](topic:render-other-program) | Isiphequluli (injini ebhalwe nge-JavaScript) noma i-Studio (injini eqhuba uhlelo) |
| Uhlelo oluthatha isigcawu | I-Studio ibeka isigcawu efolda yayo bese iyigcina ilingana | Studio |
| Vula ku… | Ilondoloza isigcawu bese iyivula kuhlelo; akukho okubuyayo: bona [Vula isigcawu kolunye uhlelo](topic:render-open-in) | Iwebhu ilondoloza ifayela |

## Ukuthi ukukhetha kuziphatha kanjani

- Ukukhetha u-Fast noma i-Path tracer kushintsha imodi nakho, nangokuphambene. Okunye engxoxweni akushintshi kubantu abangakaze basebenzise enye injini.
- Injini engatholakali ihlala ohlwini ngesizathu sayo ("not found", "needs a newer version"); awukwazi ukuyikhetha. Kusiphequluli injini esebenza kuphela ku-Studio ibonisa "saves a file": {{ui:Renda isithombe…}} bese silondolozela isigcawu nezilungiselelo zaso kufayela oliyisa kuleso sihlelo.
- Ukukhetha kugcinwa nezilungiselelo zokurenda zefayela, futhi injini oyikhethe ekugcineni iyinjini yokuqala yamafayela angenayo ukukhetha kwawo.
- Isithombe esivela kolunye uhlelo sithi yiluphi uhlelo oluyidwebile nenguqulo yalo, engxoxweni nasemugqeni wokurenda. Izithembiso zekhasi [Renda](topic:render) zimboza **izinjini zethu** kuphela: olunye uhlelo lukhanyisa, lunciphise kanye nokukhetha ngendlela yalo, ngakho izithombe ezimbili zesigcawu esifanayo zifana ngekhamera, ilanga, amalambu nokuchayeka, kodwa hhayi ipikseli ngepikseli.

## Okunikezwa yonke injini

Isigcawu siya kolunye uhlelo njengephakheji: imodeli njengefayela le-USD (lelo efanayo [Londoloza njenge-USD](topic:usd) elibhala), nefayela lezilungiselelo elibamba okungathenjwa kuwo umlethi we-USD: amanani empahla angokoqobo, amalambu avulekile ngamandla nemiyalo yokukhanya, ilanga, isibhakabhaka, ukuchayeka nekhamera njengoba ingxoxo izodweba. Akukho okulayishwa noma yikuphi.

## Umugqa wokurenda

Isithombe noma i-animation yolunye uhlelo ingalinda kumugqa wokurenda [njengezinye zethu](topic:render#the-render-queue); umsebenzi wayo ukhumbula ukuthi yenjini yini. I-animation iya kuhlelo ngokuqhubeka kanye.
