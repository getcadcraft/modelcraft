{{ui:Hlukanisa}} unqamula umphetho ube yizingxenye ezilinganayo, kunekhona endaweni ngayinye yokusika. Sebenzisa amakhona ukuze uwabambe, uqale imigqa kuwo, noma ukusunduza nokudonsa izingxenye zobuso.

## Hlukanisa umphetho

1. Chofoza {{ui:Hlukanisa}} kumbhalo wamathuluzi, noma khetha umphetho bese uchofoza {{ui:Hlukanisa…}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}.
2. Nikeza umphetho. Amamaki akhombisa lapho izinqamuko zizoba khona: phakathi komphetho kuba izingxenye ezimbili; sondela ekugcineni ukuze uthole izingxenye eziningi.
3. Chofoza ukuze uwuhlukanise ube izingxenye ezikhonjisiwe. Noma thayipha inani, njengo `5` (noma `5s`, noma `/5`), bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: liyahlukana masinyane.

Izingxenye zishiywe zikhethiwe, futhi kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ubuso umphetho ohlobene nawo bugcina isimo sawo futhi buthola amakhona amasha.

## Hlukanisa umnsalo noma indilinga

Nikeza noma yiliphi isegimenti lomnsalo noma lendilinga: iyahlukana njengento eyodwa.

- Umnsalo noma indilinga engenakho amakhona okusetshenziswa yinto enye, elele phansi ebusweni bayo, **iyadwetshwa kabusha ngalezo zigaba** kumnsalo wangempela. Izinhlangothi zayo zihlala lapho zazikhona, futhi isahlala ijika elilodwa, ngakho usakwazi ukuyikhetha ngokuchofoza kanye bese ushintsha umsakazo wayo ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}. Indilinga enezinhlangothi ezingu-24 ehlukaniswe ibe ezingu-8 iba yi-oktagoni.
- Noma yiliphi elinye ijika — umphetho wesilinda esidonsiwe, ijika elidwetshwe nge-{{ui:Ijika}}, umnsalo onemigqa exhunywe emakhoneni ayo — lithola amakhona amasha ngobude obulinganayo kulo, futhi akukho okunyakazayo.

## Ngokhiye bomhleli

Ithuluzi lifana nakukhiye bomhleli (Izilungiselelo › ModelCraft › Okhiye, noma isitayela semenyu yomhleli), lapho likutholakala emenyu ye-{{ui:Imesh}} njenge-{{ui:Hlukanisa imiphetho}}.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Amanani athayiphiwe ahamba kusukela ku-2 kuya ku-1000; ukunyakaza kwesikhombisi kunikeza kuya ku-100.
- Ijika elidwetshwe nge-{{ui:Ijika}} ligcina amaphoyinti alo: ngemva kwe-{{ui:Hlela amaphoyinti}}, liyadwetshwa kabusha kuwo, bese amakhona amasha anyamalala.
