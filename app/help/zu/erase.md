## Sula ngesifafazi

1. Cindezela {{key:E}} ukuze uthole i-{{ui:Isifafazi}}.
2. Chofoza umphetho, iqembu noma izakhi, noma umhlahlandlela. Uyasulwa.
3. Ukuze usule okuningi, cindezela bese udonsa uwadlule. Konke okudlula kukho kuyakhanyiswa, bese konke kuyanyamalala lapho ukhulula.

Isifafazi asikhethi ubuso: ubuso buyanyamalala uma usula imiphetho edingekayo. Ubuso yilokho isifafazi esidlula kuko ukuze sithole imiphetho.

## Yini eyenzeka ebusweni

- **Umphetho phakathi kwezinhlangothi ezimbili endizeni efanayo** (umugqa odwetshwe phesheya kwebhuleki, isibonelo) uyanyamalala bese ubuso obubili **buhlanganiswa** bube bubodwa.
- **Umphetho ubuso edinga ukuze uhlale uvaliwe** (umphetho webhokisi) uthatha naleyo buso kanye nawo. Sula omunye umphetho wangaphezulu webhokisi bese ophezulu nenhlangothi eyodwa kuyanyamalala, kushiye ibhokisi elivulekile.
- **Izinhlangothi ezisele** (amaphoyinti ayekho mphetho esawasebenzisayo) ziyahlanzwa.

Ukusula ikhophi yokugcina yesakhi nakho kususa isakhi kuphaneli {{ui:Izakhi}}.

## Sula ngokhiye we-Delete

1. Khetha okufuna ukususa ngethuluzi {{ui:Khetha}}.
2. Cindezela {{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Backspace}}.

Lokhu kusula nobuso. Ubuso obusulwe ngale ndlela ushiya imiphetho yabo, ngakho ungasula phezulu webhokisi ugcine umbhalo walo, bese udweba okunye lapho.

## Hlanza imigqa elahlekile ebusweni obusicaba

Uma udonga noma ibhuleki likhombisa imigqa ongayifuni (eseleyo kusukela ekudwetsheni), zisule nge-{{ui:Isifafazi}}. Imigqa elele ebusweni iyanyamalala; ubuso buhlala.

## Ubuso obubheke ngendlela engafanele

Ubuso obubonwa ngemuva bunombala oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka olunsundu. Uma ubuso bukhombisa lolo mbala ngaphandle komodeli wakho:

1. Khetha ubuso.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Phendula ubuso}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}.

Indlela ubuso obubheka ngayo ibaluleke kakhulu lapho uthumela: abenzi bezithombe, izinjini zemidlalo nabaprinta be-3D bangafihla noma bafunde kabi ubuso obubheka ngaphakathi.

## Hlehlisa iphutha

{{key:Mod+Z}} uhlehlisa isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi, {{key:Mod+Y}} (noma {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}}) uphinda. Ukudonsa ngesifafazi kuyisinyathelo esisodwa, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi kube nezinto ezingaki. Ibha yesimo ithi okuhlehliswe kukho: "Kuhlehliswe ukususwa."
