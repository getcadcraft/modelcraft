{{ui:Hlola ku-VR}} ikubeka ngaphakathi kwemodeli nge-VR headset, ngosayizi ophelele. Umi lapho kukhona ikhamera yombono, phansi kwayo, ubheke ngendlela efanayo. Akukho okuthunyelwayo: i-headset ibonisa lokho isiphequluli salo siboniselayo kulo khompyutha (noma kwisiphequluli se-headset yalo).

## Okudingekayo

- I-headset isiphequluli singayilawula (WebXR): i-headset emi yodwa nesiphequluli sayo, noma i-Chrome noma i-Edge kukhompyutha enokuxhunywa kwe-headset.
- I-ModelCraft ivulwe ekhasini eliphephile (https), njengoba kunjalo kule site.

Uma kungekho i-headset, i-{{ui:Hlola ku-VR}} iyagreyiwe futhi ichaza isizathu.

## Qala

1. Setha ukubuka lapho ofuna ukuqala khona: ekamelweni, noma ngaphandle ubheka isakhiwo.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Ukubuka}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Ikhamera}}, bese {{ui:Hlola ku-VR}}.
3. Gqoka i-headset.

Imenyu isemuva lesandla sakho sokunxele. Yibheke ngesilawuli sakho sokudla bese udonsa i-trigger ukuze ukhethe inkinobho. (Ngesandla esisodwa kuphela, imenyu ibhoyana phambi kwendawo owaqala kuyo.)

## Kubukeka kanjani

I-headset ibonisa imodeli ngendlela yokubuka okuphilayo ([ukubuka okuphilayo](topic:live-view)): izinto nezinkomba ozinikeze izinhlangothi, isibhakabhaka nelanga kusuka endaweni nosuku lwemodeli yakho nezithunzi zayo, izibani, izitshalo nezitshalo ezisakazekile namatshe, nokukhanya okubuyela emadongeni nasephansi (kuhlolwa kule khompyutha ngenkathi ubheka, futhi kulandela uma uhamba). Ukukhanya kanye nokuguqulwa kombono (ACES noma AgX) kungakwa-[Renda isithombe](topic:render). Okudwetshwayo kuncike kokusesiphequluli se-headset, njengakudeskithophu; ukuvinjelwa kokukhanya okuzungezile (ambient occlusion), izibonakaliso nokuvuthwa kuhlala kudeskithophu.

## Hamba

- **Gxuma:** ku-{{ui:Hambisa}}, khomba phansi bese udonsa i-trigger; uyafika lapho. Noma sunduza induku phambili ukuze uqondise, bese uyikhulula ukuze ugxume. Ungafika kuphela ezindaweni nasemhlabathini (izindawo ezibheke phezulu), hhayi odongeni.
- **Phendukela:** sunduza induku ngakwesobunxele noma ngakwesokudla: uyaphendukela ngama-30° ngesikhathi ngasinye.
- **Hamba:** endaweni yakho yokudlala, hamba nje.

## Linganisa

Khetha {{ui:Linganisa}}, bese udonsa i-trigger kumaphuzu amabili. Ibanga libonakala phakathi kwawo, ngamayunithi emodeli. Iphoyinti eliseduze nekhona libamba ekhoneni.

## Vula ngokusika

Khetha {{ui:Isigaba}} bese udonsa i-trigger ebusweni:

- odongeni (noma nokuthile okungebona umhlabathi): konke okusemaphakathi kwakho nalobo bubuso kuyasikwa, ukuze ubone ngaphakathi. Lokhu kusebenza kangcono ngaphandle, noma ngaphesheya kwegumbi;
- phansi, uma ubheka phansi: ipulani esikwe ngama-1.2 m ngaphezu kwalowo mhlabathi, ukuze izindonga zibonise ingxenye yazo ephansi.

{{ui:Sula}} ususa ukusika nokulinganisa. Uma indiza yesigaba isika imodeli ekubukeni lapho uqala, i-headset iqala nayo.

## Faka inkinga

Khetha {{ui:Inkinga}} bese udonsa i-trigger lapho kukhona inkinga. Iphinikhodi enenombolo iyayimaka, futhi i-ModelCraft igcina inkinga ngesithombe sakho sokuthi ubone nokuthi wamiswa kuphi. Uma uphuma ku-VR, izinkinga zisohlwini {{ui:Izinkinga}} ngakwesokudla:

- inkinobho yeso ibonisa inkinga kusukela lapho yafakwa;
- {{ui:Londoloza njenge-BCF}} idawunilodela zonke njengefayela le-BCF, eliviwa izinhlelo zokubuyekeza i-BIM (ikhamera nesithombe zifakiwe);
- zigcinwa nakufayela le-`.modelcraft` nazo.

Engeza ikhomenti enkingeni ngemva kokuphuma ku-VR, ohlelweni lwe-BCF: ukubhala ku-headset akukakwakhiwa.

## Bona njengemodeli yetafula

{{ui:Phezu kwetafula}} inciphisa imodeli ukuze ilingane netafula phambi kwakho; hamba uyizungeze, noma uyiphendule ngenduku. {{ui:Usayizi ophelele}} ikubuyisela lapho wawukhona khona.

## Nabanye, besikhathi esiphilayo

Ku-[isession ephilayo](topic:work-live), wonke umuntu ku-VR ubona abanye njengekhanda nezandla ezimbili ngombala wabo, negama labo, nedothi lapho ngamunye ebheka khona. Ukuhlela okwenziwa noma ubani kufika ku-headset yakho ngemizuzwana. Abantu edeskini abakabonakali ku-headset okwamanje.

## Phuma

Khetha {{ui:Phuma ku-VR}} (noma susa i-headset uyiqede lapho). Ukubuka okusesikrinini kuhamba lapho ikhanda lakho lalikhona.

:::note
Ihlolwe nge-emulator ye-headset kuphela, akukahlolwa nge-headset yangempela. Imodeli enkulu ingenza i-headset ibe nokugwegwesha: fihla okungakudingekile kuqala ([fihla nehlukanise](topic:hide-isolate)).
:::
