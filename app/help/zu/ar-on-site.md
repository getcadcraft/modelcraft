{{ui:View on site (AR, phone)}} kumenyu ye-{{ui:Views}} ibonisa imodeli ngosayizi wayo wangempela phezu kwekhamera yefoni. Isebenza ku-**ifoni ye-Android nge-Chrome**, ekhasini elivikelekile (https), lapho ifoni isekela i-AR. Ku-iPhone noma i-iPad, ikhompyutha, noma uhlelo lwe-Android oluboniswa ikhasi lewebhu kuphela, into ichaza ukuthi ayikwazi ukuqala; sebenzisa ukubuka kwe-3D lapho, noma vula leli khasi ku-Chrome kwifoni.

**Ifoni yokubuka kuphela.** Ungalinganisi, ungamaki, noma usike lutho kulokho isikrini esikutshengisayo: ifoni iyahamba uma uhamba, futhi iphutha lokukhomba likhula ngokuya ekugcineni kwamaphoyinti amabili ohlanganise kuwo (amasentimitha ambalwa eduze kwawo, okwengeziwe kude). Akukho lutho lapha olungokuhlolwa okunembile.

## Lingana imodeli

1. Vula imodeli kufoni bese ukhetha {{ui:View on site (AR, phone)}}. Vumela ikhamera.
2. Ipaneli esezansi ibiza iphoyinti lokuqala (ikhona lesakhiwo emhlabathini). Ma lapho esakhiweni, khomba isiyingi esiluhlaza emhlabathini, bese uthinta {{ui:Place point}}.
3. Hamba uye kwephoyinti lesibili, okungenani ihhafu yemitha kude (kude kakhulu, kungcono ukulingana), bese wenza okufanayo.
4. Imodeli iyavela ngosayizi ogcwele, ijikisiwe futhi ihanjiswe ukuze ifake lezo zindawo ezimbili. Ipaneli inikeza ukuthi amaphoyinti amabili avumelana kangakanani.

{{ui:Start over}} ihlanza amaphoyinti. Ukulingana kugcinwa kufayela lemodeli, ngakho ngesikhathi esilandelayo uvula imodeli endaweni iqala ilingene.

## Ilensi ye-X-ray

{{ui:X-ray lens}} ibonisa imodeli yonke ngaphakathi kwebhokisi phambi kwefoni (cishe imitha nohhafu ububanzi namamitha amathathu ukujula) futhi ibonise umbono ofiphele kwezinye izindawo, ukuze ubone okusemuva kodonga ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa udonga lwangempela.

## Ukuvakashelwa okulandelayo

Yima ephoyinteni lokuqala uthinte {{ui:Re-check}}, bese {{ui:Place point}}. Uma kukhona umehluko ongaphansi kwamasentimitha angu-5, ukulingana kuyagcinwa. Kuya kuhhafu yemitha, imodeli iyanyakaza ngomehluko. Ngaphezulu kwalokho, ipaneli icela ukuthi ukhombe lezo zindawo ezimbili futhi.

## Phuma

{{ui:Leave AR}} iyaphelisa iseshini. Isithombe sekhamera sihlala ku-browser: akukho lutho oluthunyelwa.
