Indawo yokusebenza ye-Sculpt ibumba imeshi ngamabhulashi, njengodaka: hudula ngaphezu komphezulu bese uphakama, uyehla, ugudiswe noma ulandele isikhombi ngaphansi kwebhulashi elijikelezayo, elinamandla kakhulu emaphakathini nelicimeka ngaphandle emaphethelweni.

## Qala

Khetha imeshi bese ukhetha {{ui:Sculpt}} kuhlu lwe-Workspace. Iqembu noma ikhomponenti kuqala iba yimeshi, njengakwindawo yokusebenza ye-Mesh. Amabhulashi asebenza kumaphoyinti emeshi, ngakho imeshi enamaphoyinti amaningi ithatha izimo ezinhle kakhulu: hlukanisa kuqala (ngo-{{ui:Subdivide}} wendawo yokusebenza ye-Mesh, noma i-Subdivision surface modifier oyi-Apply), noma vula {{ui:Detail}}.

## Amabhulashi

| Ibhulashi | Ukhiye | Lokho okwenzayo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{ui:Draw}} | {{key:D}} | Iphakamisa umphezulu ngaphansi kwebhulashi |
| {{ui:Clay}} | {{key:C}} | Yakha izendlalelo ezilinganayo, igcwalise izidikiza kuqala |
| {{ui:Smooth}} | {{key:S}} | Ilinganisa amagobongo; bamba {{key:Shift}} ukugudisa nanoma yibhulashi |
| {{ui:Grab}} | {{key:G}} | Idonsa umphezulu ngokulandela isikhombi |
| {{ui:Inflate}} | {{key:I}} | Ikhukhumeza umphezulu ngokuqondile kwawo |
| {{ui:Crease}} | {{key:Shift+C}} | Isika umsele obukhali |
| {{ui:Pinch}} | {{key:P}} | Idonsela umphezulu ngasembindini webhulashi |
| {{ui:Flatten}} | {{key:T}} | Icindezela umphezulu ngasendizeni yawo yesilinganiso |
| {{ui:Mask}} | {{key:M}} | Ipenda imaski evikela umphezulu kwamanye amabhulashi |

Bamba {{key:Ctrl}} ngenkathi ushaya ngebhulashi ukusebenza ngendlela enye (yehlisa esikhundleni sokuphakamisa, ugoqo esikhundleni somsele, susa imaski), noma vula {{ui:Take away}}.

## Usayizi namandla

Usayizi webhulashi ungamapikseli esikrinini, ngakho lizwakala lifana noma ukuthi usuhamba kangakanani: {{key:[}} no-{{key:]}} liyancipha futhi likhule, {{key:Shift+[}} no-{{key:Shift+]}} yashintsha amandla alo. Ipaneli ibonisa okubili.

Uma usebenzisa ipeni kwithebulethi, ukucindezela kanzima kwenza ibhulashi likhule futhi libe namandla; ipaneli iyavala kokubili.

## Buka ngu-X, imaski nemininingwane

- {{ui:Mirror X}} ({{key:X}}) bumba izinhlangothi zombili ngokubomvu kanye, kwizinkaba nezibalo ezinomzimba.
- Imaski ibonakala imnyama ngesikhathi ubumba. {{ui:Clear mask}} ({{key:Alt+M}}) iyisusa, {{ui:Invert mask}} ({{key:Ctrl+I}}) imaska okwakungamaskiwe futhi ikhulule okwakumaskiwe. Imaski igcinwa neMeshi.
- {{ui:Detail}} yengeza ama-triangle lapho ubumba: imiphetho ende kunosayizi obekwe kuphaneli (amapikseli esikrinini) ihlukaniswa ngaphansi kwebhulashi. Idinga imeshi yama-triangle, futhi icela ukuthi yenze eyodwa kuqala.

Ushayo ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

:::note
Imininingwane yengeza ama-triangle kuphela; ayisusi lawo asebenze abe mancane kakhulu. I-Remesh (indawo yokusebenza ye-Mesh) ilinganisa imeshi yonke futhi.
:::
