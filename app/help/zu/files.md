## Ifayela le-ModelCraft

Ifayela le-`.modelcraft` ligcina imodeli yonke: ijiyometri, amaqembu, izingxenye, imihlahlandlela, izigcawu, izinyunithi, nekhamera nesitayela owayesebenzisa ngesikhathi olondoloza. Aligcini umlando wokuhlehlisa.

### Vula imodeli

1. Chofoza {{ui:Vula}} ku-qembu {{ui:Ifayela}}, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}.
2. Uma imodeli esikrinini inezinguquko ezingalondolwanga, ModelCraft iyabuza: "Le modeli inezinguquko ezingalondolwanga. Londoloza ikhophi kuqala?" Khetha {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi}}, {{ui:Lahla izinguquko}} noma {{ui:Khansela}}.
3. Khetha ifayela le-`.modelcraft`.

Imodeli ivula ngezinyunithi ayalondolozwa ngazo, nekhamera ayalondolozwa ngayo. Ungaphinda uwehlise ifayela le-`.modelcraft` ku-3D view, noma uchofoze elinye ohlwini lwamafayela amuva ekhadini lokuqala (amabhrawuza angavula kabusha ifayela, nohlelo lwe-desktop).

:::desktop
Ku-desktop app, ukuchofoza kabili ifayela le-`.modelcraft` kulivula ku-ModelCraft, njengoba **Open with** kwenza kwifayela le-Collada, OBJ, glTF, DWG noma DXF (lingeniswa kumodeli entsha).
:::

### Londoloza imodeli

Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}. Ibhrawuza lilanda `<name>.modelcraft`, lapho igama liyifayela owayivula khona, noma "Untitled" kumodeli entsha.

:::desktop
Ku-desktop app, {{ui:Londoloza}} ibhala ifayela owayivulile endaweni yalo. Imodeli entsha ibuza ukuthi ilondolozwe kuphi.
:::

- {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} ({{key:Mod+Shift+S}}) silondoloza ikhophi njengefayela le-`.modelcraft` noma njenge-[USD](topic:usd); lapho ibhrawuza likwazi, liyacela igama nendawo. Uma lingakwazi, qamba kabusha ifayela elilandiwe: ukulondoloza okulandelayo ngemva kokuvula ifayela eliqanjwe kabusha kusebenzisa igama elisha.
- Ukulondoloza ngakunye kuyisidingiliyo esisha. Kuya ngezilungiselelo zebhrawuza, kungena ngqo efolda yokulanda noma kubuze lapho kulondolozwa khona. Uma ifayela ngalelo gama selikhona lapho, amabhrawuza amaningi engeza inombolo, njengo `Untitled (1).modelcraft`.

:::chromium
Ukuze ukhethe ifolda njalo, vula izilungiselelo zebhrawuza, sesha "downloads" bese uvula "Ask where to save each file before downloading".
:::

:::firefox
Ukuze ukhethe ifolda njalo, vula **Settings**, bese **General**, bese ngaphansi kwe-"Downloads" khetha "Always ask you where to save files".
:::

:::safari
Ukuze ushintshe lapho kulanda khona, vula **Safari**, bese **Settings**, bese **General**, bese usetha "File download location".
:::

### Qala imodeli entsha

Chofoza {{ui:Okusha}}. Uma kukhona izinguquko ezingalondolwanga, ModelCraft iyabuza kuqala. Imodeli entsha iqala ingenalutho, ngeyunithi yendawo yakho (ama-feet namainchi e-United States, amamillimitha kwenye indawo), ingenamasiteji.

### Uma ibhrawuza livala ngaphambi kokulondoloza

ModelCraft igcina ikhophi yomsebenzi ongalondolwanga ebhrawuzeni yakho imizuzwana embalwa ngemva kwawo wonke ushintsho. Ikhophi ibhalwa ngemuva, ngakho imodeli enkulu ayikubambezeli lapho igcinwa. Uma ithebhu noma ibhrawuza livala ngaphambi kokulondoloza, ngesikhathi uvula i-ModelCraft ngokulandelayo, ibuza ukuthi "Buyisela umsebenzi wakho?". Ukubuyisela kuvula imodeli njengoba yayinjalo, ingakalondolwa kufayela: yilondoloze ukuze uyigcine. Bona [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover).

### Ku-CadCraft: Vula ku-ModelCraft

Kuhlelo lokudweba lwe-CadCraft, **Ukubuka**, bese **Okwengeziwe**, bese **Open in ModelCraft** kunikeza ukudweba okuvuliwe ku-ModelCraft ithebhu entsha, njenge-DWG. Ifika njengendlela ongayingenisa ngayo i-DWG: imigqa emhlabathini, kweqembu, ilungele ukumodela kuyo. Akukho okulayishwayo; ifayela ludlula ngesitoreji sebhrawuza yakho.

## Ngenisa

Ukungenisa kwengeza ifayela kumodeli oyivulile; akulibhalisi.

1. Chofoza {{ui:Ngenisa}} ku-qembu {{ui:Ifayela}}, noma wehlise ifayela ku-3D view.
2. Khetha ifayela le-SketchUp, Collada, OBJ, glTF, FBX, PLY, STL, Alembic, STEP, `.3dm`, USD, DWG noma DXF.

   Ifayela lifika njengeqembu elilodwa, likhethiwe, nombono usondela ukuze ulibonise. Ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi yini efikile, futhi ibale noma yini engenganginiswanga, njengo "Akungeniswanga: 2 texture".

3. Ukuze usebenze ngejiyometri yalo ngqo, chofoza kabili iqembu ukuze uhlele ngaphakathi kwalo, noma chofoza {{ui:Chitha}}.

Okufanele ukwazi:

- **Usayizi nesiqondiso esiphezulu:** Amafayela e-Collada atshela iyunithi nokuthi yiphi i-axis ephezulu, futhi ModelCraft iyaguqula. I-glTF ihlala ngamamitha. Amafayela e-OBJ awanayo iyunithi: ModelCraft iwafunda njengamamitha, ne-Y ephezulu. Uma i-OBJ ifika ingu-1000 kabili inkulu noma incane kakhulu, sebenzisa [Shintsha usayizi](topic:rotate-scale) ngesilinganiso njengo `0.001` noma `1000`.
- **DWG ne-DXF** ziza njengemigqa (imiphetho) emhlabathini, hhayi njengobuso. Uma idrowing ingatsheli iyunithi yayo, kuvela ingxoxo enesihloko esithi "Izinyunithi zomdwebo" ibuze ukuthi yunithi eyodwa yomdwebo iyini. Bona [Yakha ngaphezu kwepulani yesayithi](topic:site-plan).
- **Imikhawulo:** amafayela angaphezu kuka-300 MB awenqatshelwa ukungeniswa, nalawo angaphezu kuka-200 MB ukuvulwa. Amafayela anamaphoyinti noma ubuso obungaphezu kwezigidi ezi-2 awenqatshelwa njengakakhulu kakhulu. I-OBJ engaphezu kuka-64 MB iyenqatshelwa ngaphambi kokuthi ifundwe. Amafayela okubhala (Collada, OBJ, ASCII STL, USD text) afundwa ngezingxenye, cishe ngu-4 MB ngayinye, ngakho akukho sigaba esisodwa esibamba ifayela lonke; i-OBJ engu-16 MB yasebenzisa cishe ama-0.5 GB enkumbulo ivula ngesikhathi sokuhlolwa kwethu.

## Khipha

1. Chofoza {{ui:Khipha}} ku-qembu {{ui:Ifayela}}.
2. Khetha ifomethi: {{ui:glTF binary (.glb)}}, {{ui:Collada (.dae)}}, {{ui:OBJ (.obj)}}, {{ui:STL yokuprinta (.stl, amamillimitha)}}, {{ui:PLY ngemibala (.ply)}}, {{ui:USD (.usda, .usdc, .usdz)…}}, {{ui:IFC ye-BimCraft (.ifc)}}, {{ui:DWG ye-CAD (.dwg, 2004)}} noma {{ui:DXF ye-CAD (.dxf, R12)}}.

Ibhrawuza lilanda ifayela, liqanjwe njengemodeli. Yonke imodeli iyakhishwa, hhayi ukukhethwa kuphela. Amafayela e-glTF, Collada ne-OBJ abhalwa ngemuva, ngakho ModelCraft iqhubeka isebenza kuyilapho imodeli enkulu ikhishwa; ukulanda kuqala lapho ifayela selilungile. Imodeli engenalutho ayikhishwa: "Imodeli ayinalutho: qala udwebe okuthile." Bona [Khipha imodeli ngezinye izinhlelo](topic:export-model).

## Ku-inthanethi nangaphandle kwe-inthanethi

Konke ku-ModelCraft kusebenza ekhompyutheni yakho, ngakho ukumodela, ukuvula, ukulondoloza, ukungenisa nokukhipha kusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumeka uma uhlelo selulayishiwe.

Ukuvula i-ModelCraft ngaphandle kokuxhumeka, yilondoloze ukuze isetshenziswe ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi uma usuxhumekile:

1. Chofoza {{ui:Save for offline}} ebhareni lohlelo phezulu.
2. Inkinobho ibala likhuphuka ("Saving 40 of 212") ngesikhathi ilondoloza. ModelCraft ilondoloza amafayela ayo (kuhlanganise nomfundi wayo we-DWG), lesi siqondiso nelabhulali ye-3D, ngakho kungathatha isikhathi.
3. Lapho inkinobho ifunda {{ui:Offline ready}}, ModelCraft ivula kulelo kheli ngaphandle kokuxhumeka, nokungenisa kwe-DWG kusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nakho.

Chofoza {{ui:Offline ready}} kamuva ukuze uthole {{ui:Save again (get the newest version)}} noma {{ui:Remove the offline copy}}. Uma inkinobho ifunda {{ui:Could not save}}, amafayela athile awalandwanga: hlola ukuxhumeka bese uchofoza ukuze uzame futhi. {{ui:Offline not available}} isho ukuthi le windi yebhrawuza ayikwazi ukugcina ikhophi (amawindi azimele namakhompyutha alawulwayo athile). Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

Ukugcina ku-cloud akukatholakali okwamanje. Imodeli ngayinye ihlala lapho olilondoloza khona.

:::offline
Awuxhumekile. Ungaqhubeka umodela nokulondoloza. Ngaphandle kokuthi ibha yohlelo ibonise {{ui:Offline ready}}, ungalayishi kabusha le thebhu uze uxhumeke futhi.
:::

## Uhlelo lwe-desktop

:::desktop
Usebenzisa ModelCraft ku-CadCraft Studio, uhlelo lwe-desktop olunawo wonke ama-app ayisishiyagalombili. Amafayela avulwa futhi alondolozwe lapho okhetha khona.
:::

:::web
ModelCraft isebenza ebhrawuzeni ku-Mac, Windows nase-Linux, nasku-CadCraft Studio, uhlelo lwe-desktop olunawo wonke ama-app ayisishiyagalombili kufakwa kunye, kusuka ku-getcadcraft.com/download.
:::
