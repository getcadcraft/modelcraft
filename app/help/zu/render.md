{{ui:Renda isithombe…}} yenza isithombe sombono esifana nokukhanya kwemini kunokuba kube umdwebo: isibhakabhaka, ilanga endaweni nasuku lwemodeli yakho, izithunzi ezithambile, ukukhanya okuvela esibhakabhakeni sonke okufiphaza amakhona namagebe, nemibala engabonakala njengengilazi, insimbi noma upende okukhazimulayo. Imodeli ayishintshwa.

## Renda umbono

1. Lungisa umbono ngendlela ofuna ngayo isithombe: zungeza, sondeza, khetha [imbonini](topic:scenes), fihla okungafanele kubonakale. Isithombe sisebenzisa ikhamera yombono ngokwawo.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Khipha}} eqenjini {{ui:Ifayela}}, bese {{ui:Renda isithombe…}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Imbonini}} uma eyeyekhethwe ingeyiyo leyo ofuna (bona [Izethingi zembonini](topic:render#scene-presets-a-good-picture-in-one-click)), khetha {{ui:Usayizi}}, bese uchofoza {{ui:Renda}}.

   Isithombe siyagcwala ngesikhathi sirenderwa futhi siba bushelelezi ngayo yonke i-pass. {{ui:Misa}} (noma {{key:Esc}}) siyiphelisa ngokushesha futhi siyigcine isithombe sesikhathi esesidlule.

4. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} ukusilanda (i-PNG ngaphandle uma {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} isho okunye).

:::tip
Qala nge-{{ui:Uhlaka}} ngosayizi {{ui:Usayizi wombono}} ukuhlola ikhamera nokubukeka, bese urenda {{ui:Kuhle}} noma {{ui:Kuhle kakhulu}} ngosayizi omkhulu ukuze uthole isithombe sokugcina.
:::

## Izethingi zembonini: isithombe esihle ngokuchofoza kanye

Phezulu kwengxoxo kukhona izinkinobho ezine ze-{{ui:Imbonini}}. Ngayinye iseta isibhakabhaka, ukukhanya, umhlabathi, imigqa yemiphetho, ikhwalithi nelanga ngokuchofoza kanye, ngakho akukho okudingeka ukufunde ngaphambi kwesithombe sokuqala. Chofoza eyodwa, bese {{ui:Renda}}.

| Imbonini | Lokho ekuseta kona | Kusetshenziselwa |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Ngaphandle}} | Isibhakabhaka esicacile, ilanga lemodeli ngokwayo, umhlabathi uvuliwe | Isakhiwo ngaphandle |
| {{ui:Ngaphakathi}} | Isibhakabhaka esimpunga (ukukhanya okulinganayo, akukho amabala elanga anzima), ukukhanya okukhanya kakhulu ngesitobhi esisodwa | Umbono ngaphakathi kwegumbi |
| {{ui:Ukubuka isigaba}} | Istudiyo esimhlophe, akukho mhlabathi, imigqa yemiphetho ekurenderweni okusheshayo | Imodeli esikwe yisigaba |
| {{ui:Ubusuku}} | Ilanga lishonile (phakathi kobusika kuhhafu womhlaba wendawo), likhanya kakhulu ngesitobhi esisodwa | Amalambu akhanyisa imodeli. Ngaphandle kwamalambu isithombe sizoba mnyama |

- {{ui:Khetha ngalo mbono}} ikukhethela: {{ui:Ukubuka isigaba}} lapho kuvulwe ukusika kwesigaba, {{ui:Ngaphakathi}} lapho ikhamera isemodelini, kungenjalo {{ui:Ngaphandle}}. Ngokuvula kokuqala kwengxoxo iqala ngale ndlela yayo.
- Zonke izilungiselelo ezisezansi zisala futhi zingashintshwa. Uma ushintsha olunye lapho imbonini isetha khona, inkinobho yembonini ayisacindezelwa futhi umugqa ongaphansi kwezinkinobho uthi izilungiselelo ziwezakho. Chofoza imbonini futhi ukuze ubuyisele amanani ayo.
- Imbonini ayithinti injini, imodi, usayizi, izinto zokwakha, ikhamera noma amalambu.

:::note
Izimbonini zinika amanani afanayo ku-ModelCraft nakwi-BimCraft. Isithombe esiqediwe sibukeka kanjani kuncike emodelini yakho nasekhadini lakho legrafikhi.
:::

Izilungiselelo zigcinwa kufayela le-`.modelcraft`, ngakho isithombe esilandelayo semodeli efanayo siqala lapho uyeke khona.

## Yenza imibala ibukeke njengengilazi noma insimbi

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izinto zokwakha}}, umbala ngamunye esikrinini unokubukeka: {{ui:Engakhazimuli}}, {{ui:Ukukhanya okuthambile}}, {{ui:Okukhazimulayo}}, {{ui:Insimbi}}, {{ui:Insimbi ecwebezelayo}}, {{ui:Ingilazi}} noma {{ui:Ingilazi eqhwabile}}. {{ui:Izinhlangothi ezingapendiwe}} zinobuso obungenambala wabo.

Ukwenza amawindi abe ingilazi, [pendela](topic:paint) amaphaneli amawindi ngombala owodwa, bese ubeka lowo mbala ku-{{ui:Ingilazi}}. Ingilazi ivumela isibhakabhaka nokungemuva kwayo ukubonakala, icwathwe ngombala wayo, futhi ibonise isibhakabhaka.

:::note
Kwimodi ye-{{ui:Ngokushesha}}, ingilazi nensimbi zibonisa isibhakabhaka, hhayi enye imodeli, futhi ukukhanya akugobeli ngengilazi. Imodi ye-{{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}} ngezansi yenza kokubili.
:::

## Ikhwalithi ephezulu

Khetha {{ui:Imodi}} › {{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}} ukuze uthole isithombe esilandela ukukhanya njengoba ikhamera iyibona:

- ukukhanya kugxumeka phakathi kwezindawo, ngakho igumbi elikhanyiswa yiwindi noma ilambu likhanyiswa zonke izinhlangothi nemibala ichichima kumakhelwane wayo;
- ingilazi iyingilazi yangempela: ukukhanya kuyigoba futhi iyibonisa imodeli;
- insimbi nezindawo ezicwebezelayo zibonisa enye imodeli;
- amaphaneli okukhanya akhanya njengamaphaneli, enezithunzi ezithambile.

Iyahamba kancane kakhulu kunoku-{{ui:Ngokushesha}}: i-pass ngayinye ilandelela ukukhanya kwawo wonke ama-pixel futhi. Isithombe siqala sinamabala bese siyacaca njengoba amasampula engeza, ngakho qala ngosayizi omncane ({{ui:Usayizi wombono}}, {{ui:Uhlaka}}) bese ukhetha {{ui:Umkhawulo wesikhathi}}: uma usuphelile, isithombe sesikhathi esesidlule siyagcinwa. {{ui:Misa}} nayo iyagcina. I-pass yokuqala ilinda kuze kulungiselelwe i-path tracer yesiphequluli.

Lapho isiphequluli sinayo i-WebGPU, i-path tracer ingeyethu ye-CadCraft futhi isebenza ngekhadi legrafikhi; umugqa ongaphansi kwesithombe uthi {{ui:Ilandelelwa indlela ekhadini legrafikhi (WebGPU)}}. Ngemva kwe-{{ui:Misa}} noma isikhathi sesiphelile, i-{{ui:Qhubeka}} iqhubeka nesithombe esifanayo kuze kuphele amasampula ayo; ukushintsha isilungiselelo noma ukucindezela {{ui:Renda}} futhi kuqala kusha. Ngaphandle kwe-WebGPU isithombe silandelelwa nge-WebGL (umugqa uyasho lokho) futhi {{ui:Qhubeka}} ayinikezwa.

:::linux
I-Firefox nezinhlelo eziningi ze-Chrome ku-Linux azinayo i-WebGPU okwamanje, ngakho ikhwalithi ephezulu isebenzisa i-WebGL lapho.
:::

Kule modi imiphetho ayidwetshwa, izindwangu zelabhulali zibonisa umbala nokungabi bushelelezi kwazo kodwa hhayi ama-bump (into [eyenziwe ngamanodi](topic:shading#in-high-quality-pictures) ibonisa i-normal nama-bump ayo), futhi ukusika kwesigaba kwenziwa emodelini ngaphambi kokuqala kwesithombe.

{{ui:Ukunciphisa umsindo (kususa okusele ngemva kwamasampula)}} isebenza ngokuzenzakalelayo: lapho isithombe siqedile, noma simisiwe, umsindo oseleyo uyasuswa unethiwekhi ye-neural eyenzelwe lokhu (i-Intel Open Image Denoise, isebenza kukhompyutha yakho: ekhadini legrafikhi, uma kungenjalo kuprosesa). Isithombe esinamasampula ambalwa siphuma sihlanzekile; {{ui:Bonisa}} › {{ui:Isithombe ngaphambi kokususa umsindo}} sikubonisa njengoba senziwe.

## Renda emoweni

Indawo yokusebenza ye-{{ui:Renda}} isebenzisa i-path tracer embonweni ngokwawo: zungeza, hlela imodeli noma shintsha ilambu bese iqala kusha ngokushesha. {{ui:Indawo}}: hudula isikwele bese kuphela okuyorenderwa; {{ui:Ibonwe lonke}} liyabuyisela. {{ui:Gxilisa}}: chofoza okufanele kube bukhali, bese ukujula kokugxila kugxilisa khona. {{ui:Imininingwane}} isetha ukuthi amaphikseli embonweni angaki ayorenderwa (okuncane kuyashesha). {{ui:Misa}} iyima iqhubeke. Idinga i-WebGPU: ngaphandle kwayo indawo yokusebenza iyakutshela lokho, futhi {{ui:Khipha}} › {{ui:Renda isithombe…}} isasebenza.

## Izinkabi nezinhlobo zokukhanya ezisebenza ekhadini legrafikhi

Ne-WebGPU, ikhwalithi ephezulu inezinkabi ezimbili ezengeziwe:

- {{ui:Isibhakabhaka sefiziksi}} sibala ukukhanya kwemini kusukela ebude belanga: ukuluhlaza okujulile phezulu, ibhendi elibhamayo esibhakabhakeni, nelanga elishisa kakhulu futhi libe mpofu uma lisesikhathini esiphansi. {{ui:Umusi}} uhamba kusuka emini ecacile (2) kuya kwemini enomusi (10).
- {{ui:Isithombe sesibhakabhaka (HDRI)}} sikhanyisa imodeli ngesithombe sepanorama sendawo yangempela. {{ui:Khetha isithombe…}} ithatha ifayela le-`.hdr` noma le-`.exr` le-equirectangular; {{ui:Jikisa}} ilijikelezisa lizungeze imodeli futhi {{ui:Ukukhanya kwesithombe}} kulenza likhanye noma lifiphale. Isithombe sinelanga layso, ngakho ilanga lemodeli alengezwa. Iyagcinwa kuphela ngenkathi ikhasi livuliwe: ngemva kokuvula kabusha, khetha kabusha.

Ilambu ngalinye ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Amalambu}} lingathatha iphrofayili yokukhanya yomenzi: {{ui:Iphrofayili ye-IES…}} ifunda ifayela le-IES (LM-63) bese ilambu likhipha ukukhanya kwalo njengalokho ukulungiswa okunjalo kukwenza (ilambu elimweleko elincane, noma elikhanyisa udonga). Wonke amalambu anegama elifanayo asebenzisa leyo, futhi ilambu ligcina ukukhanya kwalo. Chofoza igama leprofayili ukuyeka ukuyisebenzisa.

Amawindi eyingilazi ecacile asiza ukukhanya kwesibhakabhaka kuthole indlela ingene egumbini, ngakho izithombe zangaphakathi ngaphansi kwesibhakabhaka ziba lula ngokushesha.

Imodi ye-{{ui:Ngokushesha}} nenhlelo ye-WebGL ibonisa {{ui:Isibhakabhaka sefiziksi}} kanye {{ui:Isithombe sesibhakabhaka (HDRI)}} njengesibhakabhaka esicacile futhi ziyawanaka amaphrofayili wokukhanya.

## Ikhamera

{{ui:Ikhamera}} isetha isithombe njengesithombe sokuthwebula:

- {{ui:Ukuvezwa}}: gcina {{ui:Isilayidi sokukhanya}}, noma khetha {{ui:Izilungiselelo zekhamera}} bese usetha {{ui:Ukuvulwa kwembobo (f-stop)}}, {{ui:Isivinini sesibambi}} ne-{{ui:ISO}} njengakukhamera; i-EV eseduze kwazo iyinani lokuvezwa elijwayelekile. I-f/16 ku-1/100 s ne-ISO 100 ihambisana nosuku oluyilanga; isithombe esimnyama sidinga umbobo obanzi, isibambi esiphuza kakhulu noma i-ISO ephezulu. Lokhu kusebenza kuyo yonke imodi.
- {{ui:Ukujula kokugxila}} kubhlurisa okusondele noma okude kakhulu kune-{{ui:Ibanga lokugxila (m)}} njengoba ilensi enale f-stop izokwenza (shiya ingenalutho ukugxila kulokho okusemaphakathi kombono); {{ui:Ukwakheka kwembobo}} kunika okukhanya okungagxilile isimo esijikazayo noma esinezimpaphe.
- {{ui:Ukubonakala kwamaphuzu amabili (imigqa emile ihlala imile)}} ilinganisa ikhamera futhi ihambise isithombe, njengelensi ye-tilt-shift yokwakha, ukuze izindonga zingaphenduki lapho ubheka phezulu noma phansi ngakwesakhiwo.
- {{ui:Ibhalansi yombala}} isetha umbala wokukhanya okubonakala njengemhlophe: phansi (njengo-3000 K) ngokukhanya okufudumele kwamalambu, phezulu ngezinsuku zethunzi nezimpunga.
- {{ui:Ukuprojekta}}: {{ui:Ipanorama ye-360°}} yenza isithombe esi-2:1 sakho konke okuzungeze ikhamera sababukeli bepanorama; {{ui:Ipanorama yesterio ye-360°}} imisa ipanorama yeso lesokunxele neleso lokudla ngaphezulu kwenye ukuze kusetshenziswe ama-headset; {{ui:Imephu yekyubhu (izinhlangothi eziyi-6)}} yenza imibono eyisikwele eyi-6 ngegridi engu-3 × 2. Ububanzi buvela ku-{{ui:Usayizi}}.

Ukuprojekta, ukujula kokugxila, ukubonakala kwamaphuzu amabili nebhalansi yombala kudinga i-path tracer ekhadini legrafikhi (WebGPU), ngakho zibonakala kuphela ku-{{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu}}.

## Qeda isithombe

{{ui:Isithombe}} silinganisa isithombe sekhwalithi ephezulu njengesithombe sokuthwebula, futhi yonke inkinobho iguqula isithombe esiqediwe ngokushesha, ngaphandle kokurenda kusha:

- {{ui:Ukubukeka (ukuguqulwa kokubuka)}}: {{ui:ACES filmic}} (isithombe njengakuqala), {{ui:AgX}} (ithambile, igcina umbala ekukhanyeni okukhanya kakhulu), {{ui:Filmic}} (ijika lefilimu elithambile) noma {{ui:Ejwayelekile}} (kulula, ukukhanya okukhanya kakhulu kuba mhlophe).
- {{ui:Ukuphambana}} kanye {{ui:Ukucindezelwa kokukhanya okukhulu}} (ibuyisela imininingwane ezindaweni ezikhanya kakhulu).
- {{ui:Bloom}} (ukukhanya okuthambile okuzungeze ukukhanya okukhanyayo), {{ui:Glare}} nemigqa yayo {{ui:Imigqa ye-glare}} (imisebe yezinkanyezi emaphuyinteni akhanya kakhulu) kanye ne-{{ui:Vignette}} (amakhona amnyama).
- {{ui:Ithebula lemibala (.cube)…}} isebenzisa ukwenza umbala ngefayela le-`.cube`, nge-{{ui:Amandla ethebula lemibala}} yalo; njengesithombe sesibhakabhaka igcinwa kuphela ngenkathi ikhasi livuliwe.

## Londoloza isithombe

{{ui:Londoloza njenge}} eduze kwe-{{ui:Londoloza}} ikhetha ifayela: {{ui:PNG}}, {{ui:JPEG}} noma {{ui:WebP}} ukwabelana (i-JPEG ne-WebP kuphela lapho isiphequluli sizibhala khona), futhi ngemva kokurenda kwekhwalithi ephezulu ekhadini legrafikhi futhi:

- {{ui:PNG, amabhithi 16}}: isithombe esilungisiwe nezinyathelo ezincane phakathi kwezithunzi, sokuphrinta noma ukuhlela okuqhubekayo.
- {{ui:EXR, zonke izendlalelo (float ye-32-bit)}}: isithombe njengoba sirenderwe (ukukhanya okungqondile, akukho ukulungiswa) nakho konke okuyinkomba ngayinye yokurenda njengengqimba yayo, kumaphrogramu okuhlanganisa.
- {{ui:Ifayela lokurenda (.ccrender, ukuze ulungise futhi kamuva)}}: konke okudingwa yingxoxo ukuze ixube futhi ilungise isithombe futhi. {{ui:Vula ifayela lokurenda…}} iyabuyisa lona, nemixube nokulungiswa kwalo, ngaphandle kokurenda.

## Xuba amalambu ngemva kokurenda

Vula {{ui:Ukuxuba ukukhanya (shintsha umbala nokukhanya kwelambu ngalinye ngemva kokurenda)}} ngaphambi kokucindezela {{ui:Renda}}. Lapho isithombe siqedile (noma simisiwe), {{ui:Xuba isithombe}} ilethe amaqembu ezinhlobo zokukhanya: isibhakabhaka, ilanga namalambu ngegama (wonke amalambu anegama elifanayo ayiqembu elilodwa). Vala iqembu, linike umbala, noma yenze likhanye noma lifiphale, isithombe sishintsha ngokushesha, ngaphandle kokurenda kusha. {{ui:Londoloza ukuxuba}} igcina ukuxuba ngegama ozolikhetha kamuva; {{ui:Njengoba kurenderiwe}} ibuyela esithombeni njengoba senziwa. {{ui:Londoloza}} igcina isithombe esixubile.

{{ui:Izakhi zokurenda (albedo, normals, depth, diffuse, specular, transmission, emission, IDs)}} igcina izingxenye zesithombe nazo. {{ui:Bonisa}} ishintsha umbono ibe enye yazo: imibala yobuso (albedo), ama-normal, ukujula, ukukhanya okusabalele, okuqondile nokudluliswayo (ngakunye ngqo nangokungaqondile), okukhanyayo nesibhakabhaka, nombala ngento noma ngempahla ngayinye ukwenza amaskhi.

Zombili zigcina izendlalelo ezengeziwe ekhadini legrafikhi. Ngosayizi omkhulu ikhadi legrafikhi lingase lingabi nendawo; ingxoxo iyasho lokho, futhi usayizi omncane uyasebenza.

## Uhlu lokurenda

Ngemva kokusetha kwekhwalithi ephezulu, {{ui:Engeza lesi sithombe kuhlu}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Uhlu lokurenda}} igcina isithombe njengoba sisethwe khona manje; ithi {{ui:Engeza le-animation kuhlu}} ewindini levidiyo yenza okufanayo ku-animation yonke, ilandelelwe ngohlaka ngohlaka. {{ui:Qala uhlu}} irenderisa ngayinye ngokulandelana kule kompyutha, futhi {{ui:Londoloza}} eduze kwayo ngayinye eseqediwe iyilanda. Ukuvala ikhasi kugcina uhlu: vula imodeli efanayo bese liqhubeka lapho limise khona. Uhlu udinga i-WebGPU.

## Ukhohlo, intuthu, umsindo nokukhanya okugoqekile

Lezi zikhona ku-{{ui:Ukhohlo, intuthu nomsindo}} futhi zisebenza ku-{{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu}} ekhadini legrafikhi; ezinye izinjini zizishiya.

- {{ui:Ukhohlo nomhwamuko}} ugcwalisa umoya kuze kufike ebude ngomhwamuko okhanyiswa yilanga, isibhakabhaka namalambu. {{ui:Ukubona kukude kangakanani, ngamamitha}} kusetha ukuthi ukhulu kangakanani (cishe amamitha ayi-100 ngumhwamuko okhanyayo, amamitha angama-20 ngukhohlo olukhulu), {{ui:Ukuphakama kokhohlo ngaphezu komhlaba, ngamamitha}} kuwukuthi luphela kuphi, futhi {{ui:Imisebe yokukhanya}} kuwukuthi umhwamuko ukhanya kangakanani uya ekukhanyeni. Ukukhanya kwelanga kushiya ukhohlo engxenyeni yawo ephezulu, ngakho lapho ilanga lingena ewindini noma phakathi kwezakhiwo ulibona njengemisebe yokukhanya.
- {{ui:Intuthu noma ifu}} yengeza ivolumu eyodwa yentuthu noma yefu ekhanyiswa njengokhohlo. {{ui:Uhlobo}} kungu-{{ui:Ifu}}, {{ui:Ikholomu yentuthu}} noma {{ui:Kusuka kufayela levolumu (.ccvol)}}, ifomethi yegridi ye-CadCraft. {{ui:Imininingwane}} kanye no-{{ui:Ukuhlukahluka}} kushintsha ifu noma ikholomu eyenziwe, {{ui:Ubude obude kakhulu ohlangothini olulodwa, ngamamitha (0 = usayizi wemodeli)}}, {{ui:Hambisa X (m)}}, {{ui:Hambisa Y (m)}}, {{ui:Hambisa Z (m)}} kanye {{ui:Jikisa}} kuyilungiselela (iqala emaphakathi emodeli), {{ui:Ukujiya}} kwenza lijiye kakhulu, {{ui:Ukukhanya phambili}} kwenza likhanye kakhulu ngasekukhanyeni, futhi {{ui:Umbala}} wenza umbala walo. Ifayela levolumu liyagcinwa kuphela ngenkathi ikhasi livuliwe, njengesithombe sesibhakabhaka.
- {{ui:Yima ngokushesha lapho isithombe sihlanzekile (ukusampula okuzivumelanisayo)}} akunikezi amasampula amaningi amaphikseli asezinzile, ngakho enye yesithombe iqedwa ngokushesha. {{ui:Umsindo okwamukelekayo}} ukuthi umsindo oseleyo kangakanani lapho ipikseli limisa. Umugqa ongaphansi kwesithombe uthi amasampula angakanani ipikseli ngalinye lithole ngesilinganiso.
