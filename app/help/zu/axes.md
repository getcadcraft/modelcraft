Ama-akisi abomvu, aluhlaza nabuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka aqondisa konke okudwebayo: izinhlangothi zamanxande ziyalingana nawo, imigqa iyakhiya kuwo, futhi umhlaba uyindiza ebomvu-eluhlaza. Uma isakhiwo sihlezi ngesihloko (isakhiwo sesayithi esingeqondile kwenyakatho, ingxenye ejikelwe nge-30°), hambisa ama-akisi kuso, ukuze ukudweba kulandele izindonga zaso. Imodeli ngokwayo ayinyakazi.

Yonke imiyalo emithathu itholakala ku-{{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}}.

## Beka ama-akisi

1. {{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Place axes}}.
2. Chofoza lapho i-origin iyohlala khona, njengekhona lesakhiwo.
3. Chofoza iphoyinti elisemgqeni wama-akisi abomvu amasha, njengokuya phambili kodonga.
4. Chofoza iphoyinti ngasohlangothini lwama-akisi aluhlaza amasha. Aluhlaza yenziwa ilingane nabomvu, nabuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka bume ilingane kukho kokubili.

Ama-akisi adwetshwa endaweni yawo entsha. Unxande odwetshwa manje emhlabathini, noma othayiphwe njengo-`4m,3m`, uhamba ngokubomvu nokuluhlaza okujikisiwe; okhiye bombono bayakhiya kuwo; futhi i-origin snap isendaweni yesi-origin esisha.

## Lingana ama-akisi nobuso

Khetha ubuso obubodwa, bese {{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Align axes to face}}. Obuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka bese bubheka kuphuma kubuso, obubomvu buhamba ngomphetho wabo omude kakhulu, futhi i-origin isekuqaleni kwalowo mphetho. Sebenzisa lokhu ukudweba phezu kophahla olunamathele njengokudwetshwa emhlabathini.

## Setha kabusha ama-akisi

{{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Reset axes}} ibabuyisela lapho imodeli yaqala khona.

## Okunye okufanele ukwazi

- Ngaphakathi kweqembu noma ikhompoment elivulekile, ama-akisi ayo ayasetshenziswa (ajikisa nayo); ama-akisi emodeli asebenza ezingeni eliphezulu.
- Ama-akisi agcinwa kufayela le-ModelCraft, futhi ukushintsha ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- Ukuthumela nokubuka okujwayelekile ({{ui:Top}}, {{ui:Front}}…) kusasebenzisa iziqondiso zemodeli uqobo.
