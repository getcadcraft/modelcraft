## Funda usayizi ngaphandle kokulinganisa

Khetha into bese ubheka i-{{ui:Entity info}}:

- Umphetho ukhombisa **ubude** bawo.
- Uhlangothi lukhombisa **indawo** yalo nokuthi lunezimbobo ezingaki.
- Izinhlangothi eziningi zikhombisa **indawo yazo yonke**.
- Iqembu noma isakhi likhombisa **usayizi** walo (ububanzi × ukujula × ukuphakama kwebhokisi elilizungezile) futhi, uma kungumthamo ovaliwe oqinile, **ivolumu** yalo.
- Uma kungakhethiwe lutho, i-{{ui:Entity info}} ikhombisa usayizi wemodeli yonke.

Ubude nezindawo kusebenzisa iyunithi esikhethini esingezansi kwesokudla. Imodeli yemillimitha ikhombisa izindawo ezingu-0.01 m² nangaphezulu ngamamitha ayisikwele (igumbi elingu-4 × 3 m lifunda "12 m²").

Lapho udweba, ibhokisi lenani likhombisa ubude, usayizi, iradiyasi noma ibanga ngokuphila, ngakho kungadingeki ukulinganisa ngemuva.

Ukuze ushintshe usayizi emva kokudweba, khetha umphetho noma iqembu bese uthayipha kabusha ubude noma usayizi ku-{{ui:Entity info}}. Noma beka [isilinganiso](topic:dimensions) bese uthayipha ubude obusha kuso.

## Linganisa phakathi kwamaphoyinti amabili

1. Cindezela {{key:T}} ukuze uthole ithuluzi le-{{ui:Tape}} lokulinganisa.
2. Chofoza iphoyinti: ikhona, iphoyinti eliphakathi, iphoyinti ohlangothini, noma umhlaba. (Ukuchofoza umphetho kwenza okunye; bona ngezansi.)
3. Hamba uye kweziwumbili iphoyinti. Ithiphu nebhokisi lenani ({{ui:Length}}) kukhombisa ibanga. Liyabamba kumaphoyinti nakuzikhombisi ezibomvu, eziluhlaza nezizolile.
4. Chofoza. Ibha yesimo ithi "Kulinganisiwe 4' 6"." futhi **iphoyinti lesihloko** lishiywa ephoyinteni lesibili.

Ukuze ushiye iphoyinti lesihloko ebangeni elinembile, thayipha lona emva kokuchofoza kokuqala bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: ihamba ngendlela oyibheka ngayo.

## Beka umugqa wesihloko

Umugqa wesihloko ngumugqa oqhekeziwe ongapheli, ohambisana nomphetho, ebangeni okhetha lona. Sebenzisa izihloko ukuhlela ukubuyela emuva, ukuphakama kwesitezi, noma lapho iwindi liqala khona.

1. Cindezela {{key:T}}.
2. Chofoza **umphetho** (hhayi ukugcina kwawo). Ithiphu ithi "On edge" (Emphethweni).
3. Suka emphethweni, ohlangothini lapho isihloko kufanele siya khona. Umugqa oqhekeziwe uyalandela, uhambisana nomphetho, futhi ibhokisi lenani ({{ui:Offset}}) likhombisa ibanga.
4. Chofoza, noma thayipha ukuphambanisa bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: `1.5m`, `5'`.

   Ibha yesimo ithi "Umugqa wesihloko ngu-1.5 m kusuka emphethweni."

Ukuqala emugqeni wesihloko okhona kwenza esinye isihloko sihambisane nawo, ukuze ukwazi ukwenza igridi ngokusuka kwesihloko esisodwa.

Uma uthayipha ukuphambanisa ngaphandle kokuqala ukusuka emphethweni, i-ModelCraft ayikwazi ukutshela ukuthi uhlangothi oluthile: "Suka emphethweni kuqala ukuze ukhombise uhlangothi."

## Isihloko e-engeli: iProthrakhtha

1. Chofoza {{ui:Protractor}} eqenjini {{ui:Modify}}.
2. Chofoza isikhungo: iphoyinti umugqa wesihloko odlula kulo. Ohlangothini, iprothrakhtha ilala phezu kwalo uhlangothi; kwezinye izindawo ilala phansi. Ngemva kwalokhu kuchofoza, {{key:Right}}, {{key:Left}} noma {{key:Up}} ilimisa ibheke ebomvu, eluhlaza noma ezolile (cindezela futhi ukuze uyiguqule).
3. Chofoza iphoyinti elibeka i-0°, isibonelo ngomphetho.
4. Hamba uye ku-engeli ofisa yona bese uchofoza: ibamba ezinyathelweni ze-15°. Noma thayipha i-engeli ngamadigri, njengo-`22.5`, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}; isihloko siya ohlangothini lapho ukhomba khona.

Umugqa wesihloko udlula esikhungweni ngale ngeli. Dweba phambili kwawo nge-{{ui:Line}} ukuze uthole ukuthambeka kophahla, i-chamfer noma udonga oluthambekile.

## Dweba ngokumelene nezihloko

Amathuluzi okudweba abamba imigqa yezihloko ("On guide") namaphoyinti ezihloko ("Guide point"), nalapho umugqa wesihloko udlula khona eqondweni lomugqa wesikhombisi. Qala unxande lapho kuhlangana izihloko ezimbili ukuze ulibeke ngokunembile.

## Susa izihloko

- Isihloko esisodwa: chofoza kuso nge-{{ui:Select}} bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}}, noma chofoza kuso nge-{{ui:Eraser}}.
- Zonke izihloko: lapho kungakhethiwe lutho, chofoza {{ui:Delete all guides}} ku-{{ui:Entity info}}.

Izihloko zigcinwa kwifayela le-`.modelcraft`. Azithunyelwa kwezinye izifomethi.
