Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ngegiya elisebhawini le-app noma nge-{{key:Mod+,}}. Ithebhu yokuqala, {{ui:ModelCraft}}, ibamba izilungiselelo ezingezale le-app; {{ui:General}} ibamba ezabelwana ngazo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft (itimu, ithuluzi, amaphaneli). Izilungiselelo ze-ModelCraft zikhona kuphela kule-app: uzishintsha lapha, futhi leli bhrawuza liyazikhumbula.

## Izilawuli: yiziphi izinkinobho zekhibhodi nezinkinobho zembewu

Izitayela ezimbili zokhiye. Zishintshe lapha, nge-{{ui:Izinkinobho: Sunduza/hudula}} / {{ui:Izinkinobho: Modeler}} ngakwesokudla esiphansi kombono, noma ekugcineni kwemenyu ethi {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} yethuluzi:

- **Isitayela sokusunduza-hudula** (esokuqala; izinhlamvu zabamodeli besunduza-hudula abafana ne-SketchUp): inhlamvu ikhetha ithuluzi — {{key:L}} umugqa, {{key:R}} unxande, {{key:P}} sunduza/hudula, {{key:M}} hambisa, {{key:Q}} jikisa, {{key:S}} shintsha usayizi, {{key:G}} yenza isakhi. Bona [Izinkinobho nezenzo zembewu](topic:keyboard).
- **Isitayela se-Modeler** (izinkinobho zabamodeli bemesh abafana ne-Blender): khetha kuqala, bese wenza isenzo ngokushesha esikhethweni. {{key:G}} iyasihambisa, {{key:R}} iyasijikisa futhi {{key:S}} iyasishintsha usayizi kusuka lapho inkomba ikhona — akudingeki ukuchofoza kuqala. Ngenkathi sihamba, {{key:X}}, {{key:Y}} noma {{key:Z}} ikhiya i-eksisi ebomvu, eluhlaza noma eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, inombolo oyibhalayo isetha ibanga (noma amadigri, noma isilinganiso), bese ukuchofoza noma {{key:Enter}} kuyasibeka phansi; {{key:Esc}} noma ukuchofoza ngesokudla kuyasibuyisela. Bese ubuyela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.

Isitayela sokhiye sifanana nesinqumo semenyu: isitayela se-Modeler yisitayela semenyu ethi {{ui:Modeler}}, futhi ukukhetha okukodwa kukhetha okunye. Bona [Imenyu, izinkinobho nembewu](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse). Uhlu olugcwele lwe-Modeler lusechazwe ku-[Izinkinobho nezenzo zembewu](topic:keyboard#modeler-keys). Amathiphu ethuluzi nekhadi lokuqala alandela isitayela osikhethile.

## Ukuskrola: ivili lembewu noma i-trackpad

- **Yibheke wena** (okokuqala): ModelCraft iyakubona ukuskrola. Ukuskrola okuya ngasohlangothini noma okuncane, njengoba i-trackpad iyithumela, kuzungeza; izinotshi eziphelele zevili zisondeza.
- **Ivili lembewu**: ukuskrola njalo kusondeza ngasenkombeni.
- **Trackpad**: swayipha ngeminwe emibili ukuze uzungeze, bamba {{key:Shift}} uswayipha ukuze ushelelise, cindezela ukuze usondeze. Awudingi izinkinobho ze-{{ui:Zungeza}}, {{ui:Shelelisa}} noma {{ui:Sondeza}} ngalutho.

Ukucindezela kusondeza kuzo zonke izinketho. Uma "Yibheke wena" ingabhula ngembewu noma i-trackpad yakho, khetha leyo onayo.

:::tip
Esitayelweni se-Modeler ungazungeza futhi ngokubamba {{key:Alt}} uhudule, futhi ushelelise nge-{{key:Alt+Shift}} nokuhudula — kuhle kulaphu elingenayo inkinobho ephakathi.
:::

## Ukubambelela

**Bambelela emaphoyintini, emiphethweni nasemahlakaleni** (kuvuliwe ekuqaleni) iyawudonsela umkhombi kuma-endpoint, kumaphakathi, emiphethweni, kumahlahlandlela nasezinxeleni ezibomvu, eziluhlaza nezokwesibhakabhaka — bona [Ukuhlola](topic:inference). Yicima uma ufuna ukubeka okuthile ngokukhululeka eduze kwezinye izinto ngaphandle kokuthi kugxume. Ubuso, umhlabathi nokukhiya kwe-eksisi kusasebenza nalapho ukubambelela kucishiwe.

Inkinobho ethi {{ui:Bambelela}} ngakwesokudla esiphansi kombono yenza okufanayo kulo msebenzi wale seshini (iyagcinwa nayo). Esitayelweni se-Modeler, {{key:Shift+Tab}} iyayishintsha.

## Izinto eziqinile zima lapho zithinta

**Misa izinto eziqinile lapho zithinta** (kuvuliwe ekuqaleni) igcina udonga lungakhukhulwa ludlule kwelinye udonga. Nge-{{ui:Hambisa}} (nange-{{key:G}}, nokunamathisela bese uhambisa nge-{{key:Mod+D}}) ukukhethwa kuyashelela kuze kufike esolidini elinye bese kuyama kuthinta lona; nge-{{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} ubuso buyema lapho buhlangabezana nesolidi elinye. Ukuphumula phezu kwento nokushelela ngayo kuyavumelekile — kumiswa kuphela ukungena ngaphakathi.

Izinto ezivele zivezelana lapho uqala zingakhipha zihlukaniswe.

Ukuze uphushe into ngamabomu, chofoza {{ui:Shayisana}} ngakwesokudla esiphansi (noma sula lesi silungiselelo), yenza ukuhambisa, bese uchofoza futhi.

:::note
Ukushayisana kuhlolwa ngokuhambisa nokusunduza/hudula. {{ui:Jikisa}} no-{{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} awakahlolwa okwamanje, futhi ijiyomethri eyindilinga ethintayo ihlangana njengakuso njalo: gcina izinto ezihlukene ku-[amaqembu](topic:groups-components) ukuze zihlale zihlukene.
:::

## Ibhedi yomshicileli ye-3D

Usayizi webhedi yomshicileli wakho: {{ui:Akukasethwa}} (okokuqala), 180 × 180 × 180, 220 × 220 × 250, 256 × 256 × 256 noma 300 × 300 × 400 mm. Khetha usayizi oseduze kakhulu kwevolumu yokwakha yomshicileli wakho. [Hlola ukushicilela nge-3D](topic:print-3d) bese isho ukuthi imodeli inkulu kunebhedi; nge-{{ui:Akukasethwa}}, usayizi awuhlolwa.

## Ukuzungeza nokushelelisa

**Zungeza ngokuzungeze lokho okungaphansi kwekhesa** (kuvuliwe ekuqaleni): ukuzungeza kujikeleza engxenyeni yemodeli engaphansi kwenkomba, nokushelelisa kugcina leliphoyinti ngaphansi kwenkomba; endaweni engenalutho kokubili kusebenzisa indawo ephakathi kombono. Vala ukuze uzungeze njalo indawo ephakathi kombono. **Isivinini sokuzungeza** nesokushelelisa **Isivinini sokushelelisa** siyahamba kusuka ku-0.25× kuya ku-3× (1× iyisimo sokuqala) futhi sisebenza embewini, ku-trackpad, ekuthinteni nakumicibisho yekhibhodi. Bona [Ukuzungeza, ukushelelisa nokusondeza](topic:orbit-pan-zoom).

## Isikhombisi sekhona

Okubonakala ekhoneni le-3D view: **Ikhyubu yombono** (okokuqala; phezulu ngakwesokudla — chofoza ubuso, umphetho noma ikhona ukuze ubuke kusuka lapho, hudula ukuze uzungeze, hudula indilinga yekhampasi ukuze ujikise imodeli), **Isithonjana se-eksisi** (uphawu oluncane lwe-X, Y, Z ngakwesobunxele esiphansi oluguquka ngombono: X kubomvu, Y kuluhlaza, Z kuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka) noma **Akukho**. Bona [Ikhyubu yombono](topic:view-cube).
