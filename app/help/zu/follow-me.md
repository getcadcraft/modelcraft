{{ui:Ngilandele}} ihambisa ubuso ngendlela futhi yakhe into eqinile edlula kuyo. Ubuso ugcina isimo sawo futhi uhlala uqondile endleleni, futhi emakhoneni endlela izingxenye zihlangana ngombandela ohlanzekile.

## Yenza intambo

1. Dweba indlela: {{ui:Ijika}} odlula kumaphoyinti intambo edlula kuwo (ingakhuphuka iphinde yehle), noma {{ui:Umugqa}} ne-{{ui:Umnsalo}} exhunyiwe ekugcineni nekugcineni.
2. Ekuqaleni kwendlela, dweba isigaba sentambo: {{ui:Indilinga}} encane ephesheya kwendlela. Qala indilinga kuphuzu lokugcina lendlela bese uyiphendulela ukuze ibheke endleleni; noma yidwetshe ebusweni obuwela indlela lapho.
3. Khetha indlela (ukuchofoza kanye kukhetha ijika lonke noma umnsalo; {{key:Ctrl}}+chofoza kwengeza imiphetho eminingi).
4. Chofoza {{ui:Ngilandele}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Guqula}}, bese uchofoza indilinga.

Ithubhu iyinto eqinile evaliwe, enezinhlangothi ezibushelezi (ezithambile).

## Ngaphandle kokukhetha indlela kuqala

Chofoza {{ui:Ngilandele}}, chofoza ubuso, bese uchofoza noma yiliphi umphetho wendlela. Indlela iyichungechunge lemiphetho edlula kuleyo mphetho, kuze kube lapho imiphetho emithathu noma ngaphezulu ihlangana khona.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- **Izindlela ezivaliwe** (indilinga, ijika elivaliwe, umbhalo wounxande) zenza indandatho: ubuso buyasetshenziswa, indandatho izivala yona. Dweba indilinga phansi njengendlela nendilinga encane emi ephesheya kwayo ukuze uthole idonathi.
- **Ngaphandle kwendlela:** ubuso akudingeki uthinte indlela. Iphrofayili edwetshwe eceleni kwendlela ihamba eceleni kwayo, njengombumbo ngaphezulu kodonga.
- **Indlela akufanele ihlukane**, kufanele ibe yichungechunge elilodwa elixhunyiwe. I-ModelCraft iyasho uma kungenjalo.
- **Ubuso oluncamele indlela** (endizeni efanayo nomphetho wokuqala wendlela) alukwazi ukuhanjiswa: luphendule ukuze luwele indlela.
- Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
