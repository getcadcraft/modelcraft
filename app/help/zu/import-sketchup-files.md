ModelCraft ifunda amafayela e-SketchUp (`.skp`) ngomfundi wayo, ekhasini. Ayikho into elayishwa, futhi isebenza ngaphandle kuxhumo uma i-ModelCraft isebenza. Amafayela kusukela ku-SketchUp 3 kuya ku-2026 ahlolwe.

## Ngenisa ifayela

1. Chofoza {{ui:Import}} eqenjini {{ui:File}} (noma ushiye ifayela le-`.skp` ku-3D view).
2. Khetha ifayela.

   Ibha yesimo ithi "Ifunda house.skp…" ngenkathi lifunda, bese kuthi okufike, isibonelo "Ingenisiwe house.skp (SketchUp 25.0.575): izinhlangothi ezingu-1240, izakhi ezingu-6, eqenjini elilodwa."

Imodeli yonke ifika njengeqembu elilodwa elibizwa ngegama lefayela, likhethiwe, futhi umbono uyasondela kulo. Ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kuyakususa konke futhi, kubandakanya namathegi.

:::tip
Hlukanisa iqembu lokungenisa ({{ui:Entity info}} › {{ui:Explode}}) uma ufuna okuqukethwe kwalo kube kukhululekile emodelini yakho kunokuba kube esikhwameni esisodwa.
:::

## Okufika

- **Izinhlangothi** nezimbobo zazo, kanye **nemiphetho** engeyona ingxenye yehlangothi.
- **Amaqembu** afika njengamaqembu, nezakhi njengezakhi: yonke ikhophi yesakhi iyindawo yencazelo eyodwa, ngakho ukuhlela ikhophi eyodwa kushintsha zonke, njengakuSketchUp. Amagama ayafika nawo.
- **Imibala.** Impahla yehlangothi ngayinye inika umbala wayo. Impahla ependwe eqenjini noma esakhini ibe yipende lalowo mkhiqizo, ngakho izinhlangothi ezingaphakathi kwawo ezingenambala wazo ziyithatha, njengakuSketchUp. Isakhi ephendwe obomvu endaweni eyodwa nobuluhlaza endaweni enye sihlala siyisakhi esisodwa.
- **Izithungo (textures).** Uhlangothi oluphendwe ngempahla enesithungo lufika nesithungo sawo, sibekwe ehlangothini njengakufayela, futhi isimo se-{{ui:Shaded}} siyasibonisa. Isithombe sigcinwa kufayela le-ModelCraft lapho uligcina, futhi [isithombe esirendiwe](topic:render) sibonisa naso. Impahla yethuluzi yethuluzi eqenjini noma esakhini yesonke inika leyokhophi umbala wempahla kuphela.
- **Amathegi.** Iqembu noma isakhi esikuthegi le-SketchUp sithola ithegi le-ModelCraft egama elifanayo, ngakho ulibona ku-{{ui:Tags}}, libonakale noma lifihlwe njengoba lenjenjalo ku-SketchUp. Uma imodeli yakho isinalo ithegi elinegama elifanayo, leyo thegi iyasetshenziswa.
- **Izinto ezifihliwe zifika zifihliwe:** izinhlangothi, imiphetho, amaqembu nezakhi, nezinhlangothi nemiphetho ethegini elifihliwe. Ubona okubonisiwe yi-SketchUp, futhi akukho okulahlekayo: {{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Unhide all}} iyazibonisa. Bona [Fihla, hlukanisa futhi veza](topic:hide-isolate).
- **Imiphetho ethambile nebushelelezi** igcina ukuthamba, ngakho izindawo ezigobile zisabonakala zibushelelezi.
- **Usayizi** ufika ulungile kunoma iyiphi iyunithi: amafayela e-SketchUp angaphakathi ayingishi, futhi i-ModelCraft iyaguqula.

## Okungafiki

Ibha yesimo ithi yini ebiphuphe, kanye nezinani.

- **Ezinye izithungo** zigcina umbala wempahla yazo kuphela: isithungo esingekho efayelini, isithombe esingumumo ongawubonisi izingxenye zewebhu (njengefayela le-Photoshop), noma isithungo esingenaso usayizi. Izinhlangothi ezinesithungo esiprojected zifika nesithungo sibekwe cishe.
- **Amathegi ezinhlangothi nemiphetho ekhululekile.** I-ModelCraft ithegisa amaqembu nezakhi kuphela, ngakho uhlangothi noma umphetho onethegi yawo ufika ungenathegi, futhi ibha yesimo iyawabala. Yenza izinhlangothi ezinjalo ibe iqembu ({{key:Shift+G}}) bese uthegisa iqembu.
- **Izithombe ezibekwe emodelini**, umbhalo, izilinganiso, izihloko, izindiza zezigaba, izigcawu nezitayela.

## Izinkinga

- "Lokhu akulona ifayela le-SketchUp." noma "Leli fayela le-SketchUp lonakele: …": ifayela liqunjiwe noma kuyinto enye enegama elithi `.skp`. Lilondoloze futhi kusukela ku-SketchUp, noma cela ikhophi entsha.
- Ifayela lenguqulo ye-SketchUp esha kuneyeModelCraft kwaziwa ligashwa ngenguqulo elitholakale ngayo.
- Imodeli eyizithombe, umbhalo noma izilinganiso kuphela ithi ayinalutho olukhombisayo.

## Thatha imodeli

I-ModelCraft ayikwazi ukugcina amafayela e-`.skp`. Thumela ngaphandle njenge-Collada ({{ui:Export}} › `.dae`) bese uyingenisa ku-SketchUp: amaqembu, izakhi nemibala ziyadlulela; izithungo zidlulela njengombala wempahla yazo. Bona [Thumela imodeli yakho](topic:export-model).
