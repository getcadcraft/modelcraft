Izibane zikhanyisa [izithombe ezirenderiwe](topic:render). Azishintshi ukubuka kwe-3D, lapho isibane ngasinye sibonakala njengophawu oluncane ngombala waso.

## Engeza isibane

1. Esigabeni esithi {{ui:Lights}} ngakwesokudla, chofoza {{ui:Lamp}}, {{ui:Spotlight}} noma {{ui:Light panel}}.
2. Chofoza lapho okuzofakwa khona. Ukubamba kusebenza njengakuhlanganiswe ekudweni.
   - Esilingeni noma eludongeni **isibane** silenga ngama-25 cm ngaphandle kwendawo, ohlangothini olubukwa kulo; **iphaneli lokukhanya** lilele endaweni futhi likhanyela kude nayo.
   - **Isibane esigxilile** sithatha ukuchofoza kabili: kuqala lapho sikhona, bese kuthi lapho sibheka khona.

Isibane esisha sikhethwa esigabeni esithi {{ui:Lights}}, silungele ukushintshwa.

## Shintsha isibane

Chofoza igama lesibane esigabeni esithi {{ui:Lights}}, noma indawo yaso ekubukeni ngo-{{ui:Select}}. Bese:

| Izilungiselelo | Okwenzayo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Brightness (lumens)}} | Ukukhanya okunikezayo. Ibhalbhu yasekhaya cishe ingu-800 lumens; iphaneli elikhanyayo lesilingi lingu-3000 |
| {{ui:Color}} | {{ui:Warm}} (ibhalbhu yasekhaya), {{ui:Neutral}} noma {{ui:Daylight}}, noma noma yimuphi umbala |
| {{ui:Beam (degrees)}} | Ukhonkolo lwesibane esigxilile, kusuka emaphethelweni kuya emaphethelweni |
| {{ui:Bulb size}}, {{ui:Width}}, {{ui:Depth}} | Amabhalbhu namaphaneli amakhulu anikeza izithunzi ezithambe kakhulu |
| {{ui:Red (x)}}, {{ui:Green (y)}}, {{ui:Height}} | Lapho isibane sikhona |

Uthikhi oseceleni kwegama lesibane luvula noma luvala. {{ui:Delete the light}} liyasisusa. Ushintsho ngalunye yisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Ctrl+Z}} silihlehlisa. Izibane zilondolozwa efayeleni `.modelcraft`.

## Izithombe ebusuku

Ku-{{ui:Render a picture…}}, thikha {{ui:Use another date and time for this picture}} bese ukhetha ihora lakusihlwa. Uma ilanga selishonile, isibhakabhaka siba mnyama, futhi izibane kuphela zikhanyisa imodeli. Phakamisa {{ui:Brightness}} kulolu xoxo uma isithombe simnyama kakhulu, noma engeza izibane.

:::tip
Ekamelweni elibonwa ngaphakathi, beka ikhamera ngaphakathi ekamelweni (zungeza futhi usondeze, noma [isigcawu](topic:scenes)), engeza isibane esilingeni bese urenda ebusuku. Kumodi ethi {{ui:Fast}} izindawo ezikude nesibane ziba mnyama kunasesithombeni; [imodi yekhwalithi ephezulu](topic:render#high-quality) ivumela ukukhanya ukuthi kugxume udonga.
:::
