Ungahambisa ikhamera noma nini, ngisho nasesikhathini sokudweba umugqa noma ukusunduza uhlangothi: ithuluzi lilinda lapho lalifike khona.

## Ngembewu enenkinobho ezintathu

| Ukwenza | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Zungeza | Hudula ngenkinobho ephakathi (cindezela isondo) |
| Shelelisa | Hudula ngenkinobho yesokudla, noma bamba {{key:Shift}} bese uhudula ngenkinobho ephakathi. Ukucindezela noma ukukhulula {{key:Shift}} ngesikhathi sokuhudula kushintsha phakathi kokuzungeza nokushelelisa |
| Sondeza | Phendula isondo. Lisondeza ngokuya ebusweni obungaphansi kwesikhombi, noma endaweni ephansi kwaso ngokujula kwesikhungo sokubuka |
| Bona imodeli yonke | {{key:Shift+Z}} noma {{ui:Extents}} |

Zungeza ijikeleza ukubuka ngokuzungeze ingxenye yemodeli ephansi kwesikhombi, ngakho leyo ndawo ihlala indawo yayo kuyilapho konke kujikeleza kuyo. Endaweni engenalutho ijikeleza ngokuzungeze isikhungo sokubuka. Shelelisa igcina iphoyinti obamba kulo ngaphansi kwesikhombi.

Ukuze uzungeze njalo isikhungo sokubuka, noma wenze ukuzungeza nokushelelisa kusheshe noma kuhambe kancane, vula {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), ithebhu ye-{{ui:ModelCraft}}, **Orbit and pan**. Izinga lisebenza ku-mouse, ku-trackpad, ekuthintweni nakumakhiyi omcibisholo.

Ukuchofoza ngenkinobho yesokudla ngaphandle kokuhudula akwenzi lutho; i-ModelCraft ayinayo imenyu yokuchofoza ngesokudla.

## Ngaphandle kwenkinobho ephakathi

Sebenzisa amathuluzi ekhamera kubha yamathuluzi, noma ikhibhodi:

1. Cindezela {{key:O}} (noma chofoza {{ui:Orbit}}) bese uhudula ngenkinobho yesobunxele ukuze uzungeze. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ngesikhathi sokuhudula ukuze ushelelise.
2. Cindezela {{key:H}} (noma chofoza {{ui:Pan}}) bese uhudula ukuze ushelelise.
3. Cindezela {{key:Z}} (noma chofoza {{ui:Zoom}}) bese uhudula phezulu ukuze usondele, uhudule phansi ukuze wehlise.
4. Cindezela {{key:Space}} ukubuyela ku-{{ui:Select}}, noma cindezela ukhiye welithuluzi ofuna elilandelayo.

Amathuluzi ekhamera athatha indawo yethuluzi osebenzisa lona. Ukuze ubuke ngaphakathi phakathi kokusebenza ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa yilo, sebenzisa ikhibhodi:

- Okhiye bomcibisholo bazungeza.
- {{key:Shift}} nokhiye bomcibisholo kushelelisa.

Uma ithuluzi lokudweba lisesiphakathini sokusebenza (ngemva kochofozo lwalo lokuqala), okhiye bomcibisholo bakhiya izinqondo esikhundleni sokuzungeza. Bona [Ukukhomba nokubamba](topic:inference#lock-a-direction).

## Ku-trackpad

Indlela elula: vula {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), ithebhu ye-{{ui:ModelCraft}}, bese usetha i-Scrolling ibe **Trackpad** (noma uyishiye ku-"Work it out"). Bese uswayiphela ngeminwe emibili ukuzungeza, bamba {{key:Shift}} uswayiphe ukushelelisa, bese uphinda ngeminwe ukusondeza — azikho izinkinobho zekhamera. Bona [izilungiselelo ze-ModelCraft](topic:settings#scrolling-mouse-wheel-or-trackpad).

Uma i-Scrolling isethelwe ku-**Mouse wheel**:

:::mac
- **Ukusondeza:** skrola ngeminwe emibili, noma phinda ngeminwe. Kokubili kusondeza ngokuya esikhombini.
- **Ukushelelisa:** cindezela ngeminwe emibili (ukuchofoza kwesibili) bese ugcina iminwe iphansi ngesikhathi uhudula. Vula "Secondary click" ku-**System Settings**, bese ku-**Trackpad**, uma ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili kungenzi lutho.
- **Ukuzungeza:** cindezela {{key:O}} bese uhudula ngomunwe owodwa, noma sebenzisa okhiye bomcibisholo.
- Ukubamba {{key:Ctrl}} ngesikhathi uchofoza kungenzeka kungabalwa njengochofozo lwesokudla ku-ModelCraft, kuye ngesiphequluli: kungasebenza njengochofozo olubanjwe ne-{{key:Ctrl}}. Sebenzisa ukucindezela ngeminwe emibili ukushelelisa.
:::

:::windows linux
- **Ukusondeza:** skrola ngeminwe emibili. Ukuphinda ngeminwe nakho kuyasondeza lapho idrayivu ye-touchpad iyithumela njengokuskrola nge-{{key:Ctrl}}.
- **Ukushelelisa:** hudula ngenkinobho yesokudla (kuma-touchpad amaningi, cindezela ikhona eliphansi kwesokudla bese uhudula), noma cindezela {{key:H}} bese uhudula.
- **Ukuzungeza:** cindezela {{key:O}} bese uhudula, noma sebenzisa okhiye bomcibisholo.
:::

Ngembewu, ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili kusondeza; akushelelisi. Lokho kuphambene nakwamanye amakhasi ewebhu, futhi yindlela imbewu yesikrolo esiziphatha ngayo kuma-3D modeler amaningi.

:::tip
{{key:Space}} ikhetha ithuluzi le-{{ui:Select}} ku-ModelCraft. Ukubamba {{key:Space}} nokuhudula akushelelisi, njengakwamanye ama-app.
:::

## Esikrinini sokuthinta

:::touch
- **Umunwe owodwa** usebenzisa ithuluzi lamanje: **thepha** ukuchofoza, **cindezela bese uhudula** ukudweba noma ukuhudula.
- Lapho umunwe wethuluzi lokudweba uphansi, i-**loupe** phezu kwawo ibonisa okungaphansi kwawo, ukuze ubone iphoyinti lokuphela, iphakathi noma umphetho okubambisa kuwo ngaphambi kokuphakamisa.
- **Mamisa** isikhashana isigamu sesekhondi ukuqeda lokho ithuluzi elikwenzayo, njengalokho ukucindezela {{key:Enter}} kwenza: ijika, uchungechunge lomugqa. Uma kungekho lutho olwenzekayo, ukumisa bese uhudula lo munwe ofanayo **kuzungeza** kuze kuphakanyiswe.
- **Iminwe emibili** ehudulwa ndawonye **ishelelisa**; **phinda** ukusondeza. Zombili zisebenza ngokunyakaza kunye.
- **Iminwe emithathu** ehudulwa ndawonye **izungeza**.
- Ukuzishintsha (iminwe emibili izungeze, emithathu ishelelise), vula {{ui:Settings}}, ithebhu ye-{{ui:ModelCraft}}, Touch screen.
- Ukubeka umunwe wesibili phansi **kukhansela** lokho umunwe wokuqala owenzayo (umugqa wesikwele osuqalile, ukusunduza okuqhubekayo). Phakamisa iminwe bese uqala futhi.
- Ibha encane ngaphansi kwembono inoku-**Done** ({{key:Enter}}), **Cancel** ({{key:Esc}}) ne-**Undo**, lapho kungekho ikhibhodi.
- Ukubamba nokukhetha kufika kude kancane kunenkinobho, nezinkinobho zikhulu, ngakho umunwe ungazifinyelela.
:::

:::mouse
Esikrinini sokuthinta, umunwe owodwa usebenzisa ithuluzi lamanje (thepha ukuchofoza, mamisa ukuqeda, noma mamisa bese uhudula ukuzungeza), iminwe emibili ishelelisa iphinde isondeze, iminwe emithathu izungeza, nebha encane inikeza Done, Cancel ne-Undo.
:::

## Imibono evamile

1. Chofoza {{ui:Views}} ekugcineni kwesokudla kwebha yamathuluzi.
2. Khetha {{ui:Iso}}, {{ui:Top}}, {{ui:Front}}, {{ui:Right}}, {{ui:Back}}, {{ui:Left}} noma {{ui:Bottom}}.

Ukubuka kuphendukela kuleyo ndlela futhi kulingane nemodeli yonke. {{ui:Front}} ibheka enyakatho, ngomugqa oluhlaza; {{ui:Top}} ibheka ngqo phansi nenyakatho iphezulu.

I-view cube ekhoneni eliphezulu lesokudla lokubuka yenza okufanayo ngochofozo olulodwa, kanye nemiphetho namakhona, impompo yenkomfa yokuphendula imodeli, nenkinobho yasekhaya. Bona [I-view cube](topic:view-cube).

## Ukubona ngokujula nokuprojekthwa okulinganayo

Khetha {{ui:Views}}, bese {{ui:Perspective}}. Uphawu lokuhlola lusho ukuthi ukubona ngokujula kuvulekile. Lapho kuvaliwe, ukubuka kuba ukuprojekthwa okulinganayo (orthographic): imigqa elinganayo kwimodeli ihlala ilinganayo esikrinini, okuyilokho ofuna ngezinhlangothi ezivela ku-{{ui:Front}} noma {{ui:Right}}. Ibha yesimo ithi "Perspective." noma "Parallel projection."

Isigameko ngasinye sikhumbula ukuthi sasinokubuka ngokujula yini. Bona [Gcina izibuko njengezigameko](topic:scenes).
