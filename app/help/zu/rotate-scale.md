## Jikisa

{{ui:Jikisa}} ({{key:Q}}) ijikisa okukhethiwe ngokuzungeza indawo ephakathi. Idinga ukuchofoza kathathu: indawo ephakathi, isiqondiso sokuqala, nesiqondiso sokugcina.

1. Khetha lokho ofuna ukukujikisa. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, Jikisa uthatha okuchofozwa kuqala.
2. Cindezela {{key:Q}}. Indandatho ilandela isikhombi, ikhombisa indiza ozojikisa kuyo.
3. Chofoza indawo ephakathi yokujikisa.
   - Emhlabathini noma endaweni engenalutho, indandatho ilele phansi (eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka): ukujikisa kuzungeza i-axis eqondile, njengokujikisa isakhiwo ngesayithi yaso.
   - Ebusweni, indandatho ilele endizeni yalobo buso: ukujikisa kuzungeza isiqondiso salobo buso. Chofoza odongeni ukuze ujikise okuthile ngokuzungeza i-axis evundlile.
4. Chofoza indawo ebeka lapho i-engeli iqala khona, isibonelo ngasemphethweni wento ojikisayo.
5. Hambisa isikhombi uzungeze indawo ephakathi. I-engeli iboniswa kwi-tip nasebhokisini lenani ({{ui:I-engeli}}). Ibamba ezinyathelweni ezingu-15° uma useduze kwenye.
6. Chofoza ukuqeda, noma thayipha i-engeli ngamadigri bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: `90`, `45`, `-30`.

   Ama-engeli amahle ajikisa ngokuphambene nokuthambekela kwewashi, ebukwa ohlangothini indandatho ebheke kulo.

Cindezela {{key:Ctrl}} (noma {{key:Alt}}) ngesikhathi ujikisa ukuze ujikise **ikhophi** bese ushiya okokuqala lapho kunjalo, njengo-Hambisa; i-tip ikhombisa ukuthi ikhophi livuliwe yini, nokucindezela ukhiye futhi kuyalivala. Thayipha `3x` ngemva kwekhophi elijikisiwe ukuze uthole amakhophi amaningi ezungeze indawo efanayo.

Njengo-Hambisa, ukujikisa ingxenye yejiyometri exhunyiwe kuphendula nobuso obuxhunyiwe kuyo. Hlanganisa izinto kuqala ukuze uzijikise ngokuphelele.

:::note
I-tip ithi izinkinobho zomcibisholo zikhetha i-axis yokujikisa, kodwa kule build zizungeza umbono ngaphambi kochofoza wokuqala. Ukuze ukhethe i-axis, chofoza indawo ephakathi ebusweni obubheke ngendlela ofuna. Bona [Akukakwakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).
:::

## Shintsha usayizi

{{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} ({{key:S}}) ishintsha usayizi wokukhethiwe. Khetha lokho ofuna ukushintsha usayizi wakho kuqala, bese ucindezela {{key:S}}: amasikwele amancane (izibambi) avela emakhoneni, emiphetheni nasemaphakathini ezinhlangothi zebhokisi yawo.

### Elula noma shintsha usayizi ngezibambi

- Isibambi se-**khona** sishintsha usayizi ngokulinganayo kuyo yonke indlela, ngaphezu kwekhona elibhekene nalo.
- Isibambi esiphakathi kwe-**ngohlangothi** solulwa ngalona lodwa, ngaphezu kohlangothi olubhekene nalo: yenza ibhokisi lide ngaphandle kokuyenza iphakame.
- Isibambi esiphakathi kwe-**mphetho webhokisi** solulwa ngokuqondile kokubili okuphambene nakho.
- Bamba {{key:Ctrl}} uma uchofoza isibambi ukuze ushintshe usayizi ngasemaphakathini webhokisi.

Chofoza isibambi, hambisa isikhombi, bese uchofoza futhi; noma chofoza isibambi bese uthayipha isilinganiso (`2` iphindaphinda kabili, `0.5` ihlukanisa ngamabili) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ukuze unike ngayinye yezinqondiso isilinganiso sayo, thayipha okuthathu, okubomvu, okuluhlaza nokuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka: `2,1,0.5`. Ngaphakathi kweqembu elivulekile, izinqondiso ziyi-axis yeqembu lalo.

### Shintsha usayizi ngokuzungeza noma yiliphi iphoyinti

Chofoza noma yiphi indawo ngaphandle kwesibambi ukushintsha usayizi ngokulinganayo ngokuzungeza iphoyinti lakho. Idinga ukuchofoza kathathu:

1. Khetha lokho ofuna ukushintsha usayizi wakho. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, Shintsha usayizi uthatha okuchofozwa kuqala.
2. Cindezela {{key:S}}.
3. Chofoza iphoyinti elihlala lilapho (hhayi esibambini), isibonelo ikhona elingezansi.
4. Chofoza iphoyinti lesithombe. Ibanga layo kusuka kwiphoyinti lokuqala libalwa njenge-1×.
5. Hambisa isikhombi usondele noma ukhashele iphoyinti lokuqala. Isilinganiso sibonakala kwi-tip nasebhokisini lenani ({{ui:Shintsha usayizi}}).
6. Chofoza ukuqeda, noma thayipha isilinganiso bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: `2` iphindaphinda usayizi kabili, `0.5` uwuhlukanisa ngamabili.

### Shintsha usayizi kusayizi oqondile

Ukuze wenze into ibe nobude oqondile, thola isilinganiso: ubude obusha kuhlukaniswe ubude bamanje. Kumodi yamaphoyinti amathathu ibhokisi lenani liyasebenza, ngakho thayipha `4.2/3.6` ukwenza udonga olunamamitha angu-3.6 libe ude ngamamitha angu-4.2. Ibhokisi lenani lesibambi lamukela izinombolo ezilula kuphela: sebenzisa isilinganiso kuqala (4.2 ahlukaniswe ngo-3.6 cishe ngu-1.1667). Khetha umphetho kuqala ukuze ufunde ubude bawo bamanje ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}.

### Lungisa ukungeniswa okufike ngosayizi ongalungile

Amafayela e-OBJ awanayo iyunithi, ngakho imodeli edwetshwe ngamamillimitha ifika ingu-1000 kabili inkulu. Khetha iqembu elingeniswe, cindezela {{key:S}}, chofoza isibambi sekhona, bese uthayipha `0.001` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ngemodeli edwetshwe ngamainchi, thayipha `0.0254`.

## Izinto okufanele uzazi

- Ukushintsha usayizi ngokuzungeza iphoyinti ngamachofozo amathathu kulinganayo. Ukuze unwebe ngokuqondile ngohlangothi lunye kuphela, sebenzisa isibambi sohlangothi noma somphetho (bona ngenhla).
- Ukushintsha usayizi weqembu noma isingxenye kushintsha ikhophi oyishintshile kuphela; amanye amakhophi esingxenye agcina usayizi wawo.
- Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ngaphambi kochofo wokugcina ukuze ukhansele.

## Phendula

{{ui:Hlela}} › {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Phendula ngomugqa obomvu}}, {{ui:Phendula ngoluhlaza}} noma {{ui:Phendula ngoluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka}} ibonisa okukhethiwe endaweni yakho, ngasemaphakathini ebhokisi lawo: isitebhisi sesandla sokhohlo siba sesandla sokudla. Ubuso bugcina buhlangana ngaphandle, futhi iqembu noma isingxenye ibonisiwe ngokuphelele (izingaphakathi zayo azibhalwa kabusha).

{{ui:Ikhophi ephenduliwe ngomugqa obomvu}} (nangokuthi ngoluhlaza, ngoluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka) ishiya okokuqala lapho kunjalo bese ibeka ikhophi elibonisiwe eduze kwawo, ngaphesheya kohlangothi olude lwebhokisi lokukhethiwe: omunye umnyango wepheya, enye ingalo yesakhiwo. Ijiyometri engabotshiwe ethinta lapho ijoyina okokuqala, njengoba ijiyometri ethintanayo njalo ikwenza; yenza okokuqala kuqala kube iqembu ukuze zihlukane.

Ngaphakathi kweqembu elivulekile, i-red, i-green ne-blue ngama-axis aleqembu lalo.
