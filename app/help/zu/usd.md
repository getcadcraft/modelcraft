I-USD iwuhlobo lwesimo olwabelwana izinhlelo eziningi ze-3D, zokurenda nezokwakha. I-ModelCraft iyabhala futhi iyafunda. `.modelcraft` isahlala uhlobo {{ui:Londoloza}} alubhalayo; i-USD iyinketho ku-{{ui:Londoloza njenge}}.

## Londoloza njenge-USD

1. Khetha {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} eqenjini {{ui:Ifayela}} (noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+S}}).
2. Khetha ifomethi: {{ui:Umbhalo (.usda)}} (uyafundeka, uhle ngokulawula inguqulo), {{ui:Obhayinari (.usdc)}} (uncane futhi usheshe kakhulu) noma {{ui:Iphakheji (.usdz)}} (ifayela elilodwa elinezithombe ngaphakathi).
3. Ifayela liyalandwa (ohlelweni lwedeskithophu uyakhetha lapho liya khona).

Okukhethwayo okufanayo kukhona ku-{{ui:Khipha}} › {{ui:USD (.usda, .usdc, .usdz)…}}.

## Akukho okulahlekayo

Vula ifayela le-USD eyilondoloze i-ModelCraft uthole imodeli ngokuphelele: ubuso namakhala abo, imiphetho ethambile nefihliwe, amajika, amaqembu nezakhi, amathegi, imiboniso, izilinganiso, imihlahlandlela, amapulani okusika, amalambu, izithombe ezingeniswe nokubuka. Ezinye izinhlelo zibona imodeli efanayo njengama-mesh, amaqembu, izifanekiso zezakhi, impahla yokwakha, amalambu namakhamera.

:::note
Uma olunye uhlelo lushintsha ijiyomethri yeqembu noma yesakhi, i-ModelCraft ivula leyo ngxenye njenge-mesh nezinguquko zigcinwe, futhi itshele ukuthi zingaki izingxenye ezifundwe ngalendlela. Indawo ehanjiswe noma esulwe kwenye inhlelo ihlala ihanjisiwe noma isuliwe.
:::

## Vula noma ngenisa i-USD yomunye umuntu

- {{ui:Vula}} ifayela le-`.usd`, `.usda`, `.usdc` noma `.usdz` ukuqala imodeli kuyo.
- {{ui:Ngenisa}} elilodwa ukulengeza emodelini evulekile. Donsa namafayela alibhekisela kuwo (ezinye izingqimba, izithombe) kanye nalo.

Ama-mesh angena nama-normal, izixhumanisi ze-UV nemibala, ijiyomethri eyabelwanayo njengezakhi, izinto zokwakha njengemibala yokupenda, amalambu njengamalambu namakhamera njengemiboniso, ngamamitha ne-Z iphezulu, nanoma iyiphi iyunithi yefayela nei-akisi yalo ephezulu.

## Akikekho okwamanje

Ukunyakaza (animation), amathambo (skeletons) namagrafu wenodi ye-MaterialX akafundwa noma abhalwe njenge-USD okwamanje. Ukunyakaza nezinto zamanodi ze-ModelCraft kugcinwa efayeleni le-USD eyilondoloze, ukuze i-ModelCraft iyivule futhi, kodwa ezinye izinhlelo aziziboni. Izingqimba ifayela elibhekisela kuzo ezingafundwa yi-ModelCraft (isibonelo idokhumenti ye-MaterialX) ziyashiywa ngesaziso.
