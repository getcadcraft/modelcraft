Kusuka phezulu kuya phansi: ibha yohlelo, ibha yamathuluzi, izinsika ezintathu (amaphaneli angakwesobunxele, ukubuka kwe-3D, amaphaneli angakwesokudla) nebha yesimo.

## Ibha yohlelo

Ibha ecwebile ngaphezulu kakhulu iyeyazo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft. Inegama lohlelo nenguqulo, {{ui:Mayelana ne-ModelCraft}}, inkinobho yemodi yesikrini kanye noshintshi {{ui:Iphepha}} / {{ui:Imodeli}}.

- {{ui:Iphepha}} yitimu ekhanyayo, {{ui:Imodeli}} eyimnyama. Ukubuka kwe-3D kushintsha imibala yakho ukuze kufane; imodeli yakho ayishintshi.
- Inkinobho yemodi yesikrini (ifunda {{ui:Isikrini esigcwele}} ekuqaleni) ne-{{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} zijikeleza phakathi kwemodi ezintathu: ejwayelekile, isikrini esigcwele, nesikrini esigcwele namabha afihliwe. Uma amabha efihliwe, bheka emphethweni ophezulu wesikrini (noma uthinte kuwo) ukuze uwabuyisele. {{key:Esc}} iphuma kwisikrini esigcwele.

:::note
{{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} isebenzisa inkinobho Control kuwo wonke amakhompyutha, nase-Mac. Ku-iPhone, lapho izisiphequluli zingenayo isikrini esigcwele, inkinobho ifihla futhi ibonise amabha kuphela.
:::

## Ibha yamathuluzi

Ibha yamathuluzi ihlukaniswe ngamaqembu anamagama. Phakamisa inkinobho ukuze ubone igama layo nokhiye bayo.

Ngakwesobunxele kwayo, uhlu lwe-{{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}} lushintsha ukuthi ibha yamathuluzi nephaneli yesokudla yenzelwe ukwenzani: {{ui:Imodeli}} (ukumodela ngokusunduza/hudula, uhambo oluphansi), {{ui:Imesh}} ([hlela i-mesh](topic:mesh-edit)), {{ui:Ijika}} ([izinto zamajika](topic:curves)), {{ui:UV}} ([hlela ama-UV](topic:uv)), {{ui:Ukubumba}} ([bumba](topic:sculpt)), {{ui:Ukushadisa}} ([izinto ezenziwe ngamanodi](topic:shading)), {{ui:Yenza i-animation}} ([yenza i-animation](topic:animate)) kanye {{ui:Renda}} ([renda ekubukeni](topic:render#render-in-the-view)).

| Iqembu | Izinkinobho |
|---|---|
| Ifayela | {{ui:Okusha}}, {{ui:Vula}}, {{ui:Londoloza}}, {{ui:Ngenisa}}, {{ui:Khipha}} (imenyu yamafomethi) |
| Dweba | {{ui:Umugqa}} ({{key:L}}), {{ui:Unxande}} ({{key:R}}), {{ui:Indilinga}} ({{key:C}}), {{ui:Ipholigoni}} ({{key:N}}) |
| Guqula | {{ui:Khetha}} ({{key:Space}}), {{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} ({{key:P}}), {{ui:Hambisa}} ({{key:M}}), {{ui:Jikisa}} ({{key:Q}}), {{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} ({{key:S}}), {{ui:Isifafazi}} ({{key:E}}), {{ui:Ithephu}} ({{key:T}}) |
| Lungisa | {{ui:Isakhi}} ({{key:G}}), {{ui:Iqembu}} ({{key:Shift+G}}), {{ui:Chitha}} |
| Hlela | {{ui:Hlehlisa}} ({{key:Mod+Z}}), {{ui:Phinda wenze}} ({{key:Mod+Y}}), {{ui:Kopisha}} ({{key:Mod+C}}), {{ui:Namathisela}} ({{key:Mod+V}}), {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} (imenyu: sika, kopisha, namathisela, namathisela endaweni efanayo {{key:Mod+Shift+V}}, phindeza {{key:Mod+D}}, susa, khetha konke, khetha lutho, nesitayela sokhiye) |

Okhiye abakhonjiswa ngukhiye wokusunduza/hudula; uma imodi ye-Modeler ikhethiwe kwi-[Izilungiselelo ze-ModelCraft](topic:settings), ama-tooltip akhombisa lawo makhiye. Ithuluzi osebenzisa lona libonakala licindezelwe. Esikrinini esincane ibha yamathuluzi iyashelelisa.

Imenyu {{ui:Ukubuka}} inawo {{ui:Iso}}, {{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}}, {{ui:Ngasemuva}}, {{ui:Ngakwesobunxele}} no-{{ui:Phansi}}, ngayinye ilingene kumodeli, kanye no-{{ui:Ukubuka ngokujula}}, okushintsha phakathi kokubuka ngokujula nokubuka okufanayo.

## Ukubuka kwe-3D

Maphakathi esikrini. Imigqa ebomvu, eluhlaza nebhlu iyizinsika: ebomvu neluhlaza zilele phansi, obhlu uyaphezulu, futhi **enyakatho iyinsika eluhlaza**. Lapho zihlangana kungumsuka.

- Uma uqala, ikhadi elithi "Qala ukumodela" libhala ezinye izinkinobho zokuqala. Liyanyamalala uma ukhetha ithuluzi lokudweba noma uchofoza ekubukeni.
- Ngenkathi ithuluzi lisebenza, ukubuka kukhombisa **izimpawu zokubamba**: izimo ezincane ezinemibala lapho isikhombi sibamba khona (isiyingi esiluhlaza esiphethweni, isiyingi esiluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka esiphakathi, isikwele esibomvu emphethweni, idayimani eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka ebusweni). Bona [Ukubamba nokubambelela](topic:inference).
- Izinto ezikhethiwe ziba luhlaza okwesibhakabhaka. Ubuso obubukwa emuva bubonakala luhlaza okwesibhakabhaka oluyimpunga.
- Uma uvula iqembu ukuze uhlele, konke okungaphandle kwalo kuyanyamalala, nezinsika zihamba ziye ezinsikeni zeqembu lalo.
- Uma imodeli inemiboniso, umugqa wamathebhu emiboniso uvela phezulu kwesikrini.
- Donsa ifayela uliphonsele noma yikuphi kukubuka ukuze uliyivule noma uliyisise.

## Amaphaneli angakwesobunxele

**{{ui:Uhlu lwezinto}}** lubhala amaqembu nezakhi kumodeli, zihlelwe ngendlela ezihlelwe ngayo kumodeli. Isakhi esisetshenziswe ngaphezu kwesisodwa sikhombisa izikhathi eziningi, njengo "Iwindi (4)".

- Chofoza igama ukuze ulikhethe. Chofoza kabili ukuze uvule ukuhlela.
- **Thola ngegama** (kubonakala uma kunamaqembu amabili noma ngaphezulu) ibonisa kuphela imigqa elingana negama, namaqembu ahlala kuwo. {{key:Esc}} iyasusa.
- Ihlo ekugcineni kwemugqa lifihla noma libonise leqembu noma leskhakhi. Ukuze ukwenze ngale ndlela: {{ui:Hlela}} › {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Fihla}}. Isithonjana sethegi sisho ukuthi sifihlwe yithegi yaso [ithegi](topic:tags).
- **Qamba kabusha ngaphakathi:** chofoza umugqa, bese ucindezela {{key:F2}}, uthayiphe, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ({{key:Esc}} igcina igama elidala).
- **Donsa umugqa** uwuphonsele ephakathi komugqa weqembu elinye ukuze uwungenise kulo, phezulu noma phansi komphetho womugqa ukuze uwubeke ngaphambi noma ngemva kwaleso mugqa, noma ekhanda le-{{ui:Uhlu lwezinto}} ukuze uwuhambise ezingeni eliphezulu. Uhlala lapho ekhona kumodeli, futhi ugcina umbala wawo, ithegi negama. Iqembu alikwazi ukungena kuyo ngokwalo. Ukuhamba ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bona [Hlela kabusha ku-Outliner](topic:groups-components#rearrange-in-the-outliner).
- Uma uhlela ngaphakathi kweqembu, umugqa othi "Ukuhlela: Imodeli › …" uyavela, nenkinobho {{ui:Vala iqembu}} iyavela. Ivala zonke izingqimba ezivuliwe ngesikhathi esisodwa.

**{{ui:Izakhi}}** libhala zonke izichazo zezakhi ezikhona nokuthi zibekwe kangaki ("3×"), nenkinobho {{ui:Beka}} engeza enye ikhophi lapho ochofoza khona.

## Amaphaneli angakwesokudla

**{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}** lichaza okukhethiwe. Ikhanda lalo liyashintsha liveze ukuthi yini:

| Ikhanda | Ikhombisa | Izinkinobho |
|---|---|---|
| Imodeli (akukho okukhethiwe) | Ubuso, imiphetho, amaqembu, izakhi nokuthi zingaki ezibekiwe, izihloko, usayizi jikelele | {{ui:Susa zonke izihloko}} (uma kunezihloko) |
| Ubuso | Indawo, inani lemigodi | {{ui:Phendula ubuso}} |
| Umphetho | Ubude | awekho |
| Iqembu noma Isakhi | Igama (thayipha ukuze uqamba kabusha), ukuthi zibekwe kangaki, uma kuyisisombululo esivaliwe, ivolumu, usayizi | {{ui:Hlela}}, {{ui:Chitha}}, {{ui:Yenza ibe yodwa}} (isakhi esibekwe kaningi), {{ui:Yenza ingxenye}} (iqembu) |
| Indawo yesikhombi noma umugqa wesihloko | awekho | {{ui:Susa}} |
| Izinto eziningi | Ubuso, imiphetho, amaqembu nezihloko, indawo yonke yobuso | {{ui:Yenza isakhi (G)}}, {{ui:Yenza iqembu (Shift+G)}} |

**{{ui:Isitayela}}** sibeka indlela imodeli edwetshwe ngayo: {{ui:Ubuso}} ({{ui:Oshadiwe}}, {{ui:Umugqa ofihliwe}}, {{ui:Uhlaka lwewaya}}, {{ui:X-ray}}), {{ui:Bonisa imiphetho}}, kanye no-{{ui:Izithunzi zelanga}} nosuku, isikhathi nendawo yelanga. Bona [Izitayela nezithunzi zelanga](topic:styles-shadows).

**{{ui:Imiboniso}}** igcina amabuko ebonakala: {{ui:Engeza umboniso}}, uhlu lwemiboniso, kanye no-{{ui:Okwangaphambili}}, {{ui:Dlala}} no-{{ui:Okulandelayo}}. Bona [Londoloza ukubuka njengemiboniso](topic:scenes).

## Ibha yesimo

Ngaphansi, kusuka ngakwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla:

1. **Ithiphu**: okufunwa yithuluzi elilandelayo, noma okubanjwa yisikhombi ("Endpoint" = "Ekugcineni", "On red axis" = "Kusinsika ebomvu").
2. **Imilayezo**: okwenzeke ngokushesha ("Kuhlehliswe ukusunduza/hudula.", "Kungenisiwe site.dxf: imiphetho engu-1697 phansi, kweqembu.") noma ukuthi kungani okuthile kungasebenzi.
3. **Ibhokisi lezilinganiso**: ilebula layo lishintsha ngethuluzi ({{ui:Ubude}}, {{ui:Izilinganiso}}, {{ui:Umsakazo}}, {{ui:Izinhlangothi}}, {{ui:Ibanga}}, {{ui:I-engeli}}, {{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} noma {{ui:Phambanisa}}). Libonisa usayizi ophilayo lapho usebenza, futhi lamukela lokho othayipha. Bona [Thayipha izilinganiso ezinembile ebhokisini lezilinganiso](topic:value-box).
4. **Isitayela sokhiye**: {{ui:Izinkinobho: Sunduza/hudula}} noma {{ui:Izinkinobho: Modeler}} zishintsha izinkinobho zekhibhodi nezinkinobho ze-mouse ngesikhathi esisodwa (kufana nakwi-[Izilungiselelo ze-ModelCraft](topic:settings)).
5. **{{ui:Bambelela}} no-{{ui:Shayisana}}**: ukubambelela kumaphoyinti, emiphetheni nasezinsikeni, nezinto eziqinile ezima lapho zithintana. Egwaziwe = kuvaliwe. Chofoza ukuze ushintshe. Bona [Izilungiselelo ze-ModelCraft](topic:settings).
6. **Ibhokisi lamayunithi**: iyunithi amabanga aboniswa ngayo. Bona [Qala: hlola amayunithi kuqala](topic:getting-started#check-the-units-first).

## Esikrinini esincane

Ngaphansi kwamapikseli angu-760 ububanzi (ifoni, noma iwindi elincane), ukubuka kwe-3D kuthatha isikrini sonke. Ibha yamathuluzi ingumugqa oyodwa oshelelisayo, namaphaneli avulwa njengamashidi kusuka ebhalweni elisezansi lesikrini.
