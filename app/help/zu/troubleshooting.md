## Ukubuka kwe-3D kungenalutho

**Isimpawu:** maphakathi nesikrini kuhlala kungenalutho, noma kubonisa umbala wangemuva kuphela, nokuchofoza amathuluzi akwenzi lutho.

**Imbangela:** I-ModelCraft idwetsha nge-WebGL 2, ukuzwakala kwe-3D kwesiphequluli. Ayikho uma isiphequluli sidala, uma ukusheshisa kwehadiwe kuvaliwe, noma uma umshayeli wegrafikhi uvinjiwe.

**Isixazululo:**

:::chromium
1. Vula izilungiselelo zesiphequluli bese useza "ukusheshisa kwegrafikhi".
2. Vula "Sebenzisa ukusheshisa kwegrafikhi uma kutholakala" bese uqalisa kabusha isiphequluli.
3. Uma sesivulekile, thayipha `chrome://gpu` ebhalweni lekheli (`edge://gpu` ku-Edge) bese ufuna "WebGL2" ohlwini. Uma ithi ivaliwe, buyekeza umshayeli wegrafikhi wakho.
:::

:::firefox
1. Vula **Izilungiselelo**, bese **Okujwayelekile**, bese uskrola ufike ku-"Ukusebenza".
2. Susa ukukhetha "Sebenzisa izilungiselelo zokusebenza ezincomekayo", bese ukhetha "Sebenzisa ukusheshisa kwehadiwe uma kutholakala". Qalisa kabusha i-Firefox.
3. Uma kuhlulekile, thayipha `about:support` ebhalweni lekheli bese ubheka isigaba esithi "Graphics" ukuze uthole WebGL 2.
:::

:::safari
Vuselela macOS ne-Safari. Izinguqulo ezintsha ze-Safari zihlala zivulile i-WebGL 2. Uma uguqule izici ezizama ngaphansi kwe-**Safari**, bese **Izilungiselelo**, bese **Okuthuthukisiwe** (noma **Feature Flags**), zisete kabusha.
:::

Ekhompyutheni yomsebenzi, ku-remote desktop noma emshinini obonakalayo, izinto zokubonisa ze-3D zingavinjwa ngomthetho. Zama enye ikhompyutha. Bheka [Iziphequluli nekhompyutha](common:browsers).

## Izinkinobho azenzi lutho, noma zibhala ebhokisini

**Imbangela:** ukugxila kwekhibhodi kusensimini yephaneli: uhlu {{ui:Ubuso}} ku-{{ui:Isitayela}}, isilungiselelo selanga, insimu {{ui:Igama}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}, noma isikhethi sezinyunithi. Izinhlamvu nezinkinobho zomcibisholo zibe zisiya kuleyo nsimu.

**Isixazululo:** chofoza ebukweni le-3D, bese ucindezela inkinobho futhi. Izinkinobho zingasebenzi nalapho kuvulekile ingxoxo.

## Ukuthayipha inombolo akwenzi lutho

- **Akukakho qondo.** Ubude obuthayipha buhamba ngendlela isikhombi esikhomba ngayo kusukela ekuchofozeni kokuqala. Hambisa isikhombi kancane endleleni ofuna ngayo, bese uthayipha.
- **Isikhathi esingalungile.** Amathuluzi amaningi amukela inani elithayiphiwe kuphela ngemva kokuchofoza kokuqala: chofoza ikhona yokuqala, ubuso noma iphoyinti okuzohanjiswa kulo kuqala. {{ui:Indilinga}} ne-{{ui:Ipholigoni}} athatha inani lezinhlangothi ngaphambi kokuchofoza kokuqala.
- **"Thayipha ubude njengo-3m, 10'6" noma 4m,3m."** I-ModelCraft ayiyifundanga. Hlola ukuthi kukhona yini ikhoma lamadesimali (`2,5`): sebenzisa ichashazi.

## Isimo siqala endaweni engalungile esikrinini sokuthinta

**Isimpawu:** ngomunwe, unxande wokuqala uhle, kodwa unxande, indilinga noma umugqa olandelayo uqala lapho ukuthinta kwakho kwangaphambilini kuphela khona, hhayi lapho uthinte khona.

**Imbangela:** iphutha kulolu hlobo lwalolu hlelo: esikrinini sokuthinta ithuluzi likhumbula iphoyinti lokubambelela lokugcina kusukela esenzweni sangaphambilini.

**Isixazululo:** thinta inkinobho yethuluzi ebhalweni lamathuluzi futhi ngaphambi kwesimo ngasinye esisha. Lokho kuqalisa ithuluzi kabusha, futhi iphoyinti lokuqala lifika lapho uthinta khona.

## Ukuhlela iqembu elilodwa kushintshe elinye

**Isimpawu:** ukopishe iqembu nge-{{ui:Hambisa}} kanye {{key:Ctrl}}, wavula ikhophi eyodwa wayihlela, bese nenye ikhophi nayo yashintsha. I-{{ui:Uhlu lwezinto}} ibonisa zombili ngegama elifanayo.

**Imbangela:** kulolu hlelo, ukukopisha iqembu kwenza ukubekwa kwesibili kweqembu elifanayo, njengakwesakhi. Inkinobho ethi {{ui:Yenza ibe yodwa}} inikezwa izakhi kuphela.

**Isixazululo:** khetha ikhophi ofuna ukuyishintsha, chofoza {{ui:Yenza ingxenye}} ku-{{ui:Ulwazi lwento}}, bese {{ui:Yenza ibe yodwa}}. Leyo kophi manje inezinto zayo. Noma {{ui:Chitha}} yona.

## {{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} akwenzi lokho engilindele

- **"{{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} isebenza ebusweni. Chofoza ubuso."** Uchofoze umphetho noma indawo engenalutho. Hambisa isikhombi phezu kuze kugqanyiswe ubuso, bese uchofoza.
- **Ubuso bungaphakathi eqenjini.** Chofoza kabili iqembu ukuze uliviule kuqala.
- **Iwindi alisikanga udonga.** Unxande kufanele udwetshwe ebusweni bodonga, hhayi phambi kwalo, futhi udonga kufanele lube nobuso bangemuva. Bheka [Sika ukuvuleka udongeni](topic:openings#if-it-did-not-cut-through).
- **Amabhokisi amabili aphindana, kodwa awahlanganisi.** Izinto eziqinile ezisunduzelwe ngaphakathi kwenye azisiki noma zingahlanganisi kulolu hlelo.

## Umugqa noma isimo akenzanga ubuso

Ubuso ugcwala kuphela uma imiphetho ivala ilupu esicaba. Hlola ukuthi iphoyinti lokugcina libambelele kolokuqala ("Isiphetho"), nokuthi yonke imiphetho ilele endizeni eyodwa. Ilupu elingaphandle kwendiza kancane lihlala lingumugqa. Dweba omunye wemiphetho yalo futhi, ubambelele emakhonweni.

## Angikwazi ukukhetha noma ukususa ubuso

- Nge-{{ui:Khetha}}, ukuchofoza eduze komphetho kukhetha umphetho. Chofoza eduze nesiphakathi sobuso.
- I-{{ui:Isifafazi}} ayikhethi ubuso. Khetha ubuso bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}}, noma usule omunye wemiphetho yalo.
- Uma sekuyiqembu noma isakhi, ukuchofoza kukhetha yonke into. Chofoza kabili ukuze uyivule.

## "Khetha okuthile ukuze uhambise kuqala, noma uchofoze kukho."

{{ui:Hambisa}}, {{ui:Jikisa}} kanye {{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} zidinga okuthile okuzosebenza kukho: khetha kuqala, noma uqale ithuluzi ngokuchofoza into ngokwayo (hhayi endaweni engenalutho noma esikhombisini).

## "Khetha ijiyomethri kuqala (donsa iwindi, noma chofoza kathathu ukukhetha konke okuhlangene)."

{{key:G}} kanye {{key:Shift+G}} zidinga ukukhethwa. Imikhombandlela ayikwazi ukuhlanganiswa.

## Izinkinobho zomcibisholo zijikeleza esikhundleni sokukhiya

Ukukhiya kusebenza kuphela ngemva kokuchofoza kokuqala kwethuluzi lokudweba noma lokuhlela. {{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} alinakho ukukhiya, futhi ku-{{ui:Jikisa}} azishintshi ukujika. Ngezikhathi ezinye izinkinobho zomcibisholo zijikeleza umbono.

## {{key:Esc}} esikrinini esigcwele

Esikrinini esigcwele, isiphequluli singase sisebenzise {{key:Esc}} yokuqala ukuphuma esikrinini esigcwele. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} futhi ukuze ukhansele ithuluzi.

## Akukho inkinobho ephakathi

Cindezela {{key:O}} ukuze {{ui:Zungeza}} bese udonsa, noma sebenzisa izinkinobho zomcibisholo. Bheka [Zungeza, shelelisa nosondeze](topic:orbit-pan-zoom#without-a-middle-button).

### Ukungena kungenza ngosayizi ongalungile noma kulele ecaleni

- **OBJ:** i-OBJ ayinayo iyunithi; i-ModelCraft ifunda amamitha. Imodeli yamamilimitha ingena ngokukhulu izikhathi ezingu-1000: [shintsha usayizi](topic:rotate-scale#fix-an-import-that-came-in-the-wrong-size) wayo ngo-`0.001`; imodeli yamainshi ngo-`0.0254`.
- **OBJ ilele ecaleni:** i-ModelCraft ilindele amafayela e-OBJ anoY uphezulu. Ifayela elibhalwe no-Z uphezulu lingena lilele. Jikisa ngo-90° ngokuya ku-akisi ebomvu: chofoza isikhungo se-{{ui:Jikisa}} ebusweni obubheka ngebomvu, noma vula ifayela ohlelweni lwalo bese ukhipha no-Y uphezulu.
- **DWG noma DXF:** ukhethe iyunithi engalungile kwingxoxo ethi "Iyunithi zomdwebo". Hlehlisa bese ungenisa futhi.

## Ngilahlekelwe umsebenzi

I-ModelCraft ayilondolozi ifayela ngokwayo. Uma uvala noma uvuselela ithebhu enezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, isiphequluli siyabuza kuqala (ukushiya isayithi noma ukulayisha kabusha); khetha ukuhlala bese ulondoloza. Okuthi {{ui:Entsha}} no-{{ui:Vula}} nakho kubuza ngezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga. Uma isiphequluli noma ikhompyutha iwohloka, vula i-ModelCraft futhi kwisiphequluli esifanayo: iyakunikeza umsebenzi ongalondolozwanga ("Buyisa umsebenzi wakho?"; bheka [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover)). Londoloza kaningi nge-{{key:Mod+S}}.

## Ifolda yami yokulanda igcwala ngamakhophi

Ukulondoloza ngakunye kuba ukulanda okusha, kuqanjwe njenge `Untitled (1).modelcraft`, `Untitled (2).modelcraft` njalo njalo. Susa ezindala, noma setha isiphequluli ukuze sibuze ukuthi ufuna ukulondoloza kuphi ngalowo ngamunye futhi ubhale phezu kwefayela elidala ngokwakho. Bheka [Vula, londoloza, ngenisa nokukhipha](topic:files#save-a-model).

## Kuhamba kancane

- Isinyathelo ngasinye sokuhlehlisa silondoloza ikhophi yemodeli ([i-mesh](topic:meshes) ilondoloza okushintshile kuphela), ngakho ekungeniseni okukhulu kwejiyomethri yokusunduza/ukuhudula, ukuhlela ngakunye kuthatha isikhathi eside. Chitha noma susa okungadingeki, londoloza, bese uvula kabusha: ukuvula kabusha kusula nomlando wokuhlehlisa.
- Izithunzi zelanga zibiza kakhulu. Susa ukukhetha {{ui:Izithunzi zelanga}} ngenkathi umisa imodeli.

## Azikho izithunzi

{{ui:Izithunzi zelanga}} ivaliwe, ilanga lishonile ngesikhathi osikhethile ("Ilanga lishonile ngalesi sikhathi."), noma isitayela siyi-{{ui:Uhlaka lwewaya}}, olungadwetshi izithunzi.

## "Lesi siphequluli asivumelanga ukugcwalisa isikrini."

Ezinye iziphequluli nezinkombandlela ezifakiwe zenqaba ukugcwalisa isikrini. Sebenzisa isikrini esigcwele sesiphequluli kunalokho ({{pick:mac=Control-Command-F|windows linux=F11}}).

## I-ModelCraft ayivuli ngaphandle kokuxhumana

I-ModelCraft ivula ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuphela uma yayilondolozelwe ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kulesi siphequluli: inkinobho yebha yohlelo kumele ifunde {{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}. Vula uma ukhona ku-inthanethi bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}. Uma inkinobho ifunda {{ui:Akukwazanga ukulondoloza}}, yichofoze futhi ngokuxhumana okungcono; {{ui:Ayikho ingaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} kusho ukuthi lo windi (iwindi eliyimfihlo, noma ikhompyutha elawulwayo) alikwazi ukugcina ikhophi. Bheka [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).
