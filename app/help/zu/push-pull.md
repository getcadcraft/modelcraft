{{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} ({{key:P}}) ihambisa indawo ngokuqondile kwayo: phezulu nphansi kwesitezi, ngaphakathi nangaphandle kodonga. Lokho okwenzekayo kuncike endaweni.

## Yenza i-solid kusuka endaweni

1. Dweba indawo, isibonelo unxantathu wesikwele phansi ([Dweba izindawo](topic:draw-faces)).
2. Cindezela {{key:P}}.
3. Hlanganisa indawo. Iyakhanya.
4. Chofoza indawo, bese uhambisa isikhombi. Indawo iyaphakama (noma icwile) futhi izinhlangothi zikhula emaphethelweni ayo. Ibhokisi lenani ({{ui:Ibanga}}) nethiphu zikhombisa ibanga.
5. Chofoza ukuqeda, noma uthayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: `3m`, `10'`, `2700` kumodeli yemilimitha.

   Manje unesolid evaliwe. Yikhethe yonke bese uyiqembisa (bona [Amaqembu namakhompoyinti](topic:groups-components)): {{ui:Ulwazi lwento}} bese ikhombisa "Solid: Yes" nevolumu yayo.

Indawo eyodwa ingahamba noma yiluphi uhlangothi: yidonse phezulu ukuze ibe yisisindo ngaphezu komhlaba, yicindezele phansi ukuze ibe umgodi noma isisekelo.

:::tip
Esikhundleni sokuchofoza kabili, cindezela indawo, hudula, bese ukhipha. Esikrinini esithintekayo lokho yindlela ejwayelekile: cindezela, hudula, phakamisa.
:::

## Donsa kuze kufike ebude bento esele

Lapho usadonsa, hlanganisa ikhona, umaphethelo, isiphakathi, umphetho noma umugqa wesiqondisi wenye ingxenye yemodeli. Indawo ilingana nalelo phuzu, futhi umaka uyaliboniswa. Sebenzisa lokho ukwenza amabhlogo amabili abe ubude obufanayo, noma ukuma kumugqa wesiqondisi owubekile nge-[tape measure](topic:guides-measure).

Ungahlanganisa futhi indawo ehambisanayo nendawo oyihambisayo, njengophezulu bebhokisi elinye noma ophezulu bebhokisi empini ekuma phezu kwalo. Ithiphu ithi "On face", bese indawo oyisunduzayo iphela ilingana nayo. Isibonelo, cindezela indawo engaphakathi yepayipi phansi kuze kulingane nebhokisi elingaphansi kwepayipi.

Indawo ethambekele endaweni oyisunduzayo ayisethi ulingano. Ibanga lilandela isikhombi. Lokho kuyenzeka nakwindawo emuva kwendawo oyisunduzayo.

Lapho indawo esundulwayo iphela ilingana nezindawo ezizungezile futhi, izinhlangothi zayo ziyanyamalala. Azikho izinhlangothi zobude obungu-zero ezishiyekile, futhi isakhiwo owasidweba sihlala endaweni, ngakho ungayisunduza futhi kamuva. Ukuze ibuye ibe yindawo eyodwa, susa imiphetho yesakhiwo ngesi-{{ui:Isifafazi}}: izindawo ezimbili ziyahlangana.

## Yenza ibhokisi libe phezulu noma libe phansi

Sunduza/hudula phezulu kwebhokisi. Ngoba izinhlangothi sezikhuphuka ngqo kusuka kulo, phezulu kuyanyakazela nje: ibhokisi liba phezulu noma liba phansi, futhi azikho imiphetho emisha edalwayo.

- Ukucindezela phezulu kuze kufike phansi kuqothule ibhokisi. I-ModelCraft iyayeka ebudeni obungu-zero; ayiyiguquli ngaphakathi ngaphandle.
- Okufanayo kunoma iyiphi indawo ezinhlangothi zayo zonke zihamba ngasendleleni yayo, njengesiphetho sodonga.

## Engeza ibhe noma isikhala

1. Dweba unxantathu ohlangothini lwebhokisi (uqale endaweni; uhlala ndizeni yendawo).
2. Cindezela {{key:P}} bese udonsa unxantathu omusha **ngaphandle**. Wengeza impahla: ibhe, isimini, isimbozo.
3. Noma uyicindezele **ngaphakathi**. Inquma isikhala: iphothi, isikhala esincane, indawo yokungena esilethwe ngemuva.

Lapho izinhlangothi zengxenye entsha zihlanganisa nezindawo ezikhona, ziyahlangana zibe indawo eyodwa, ngakho awuthole imigqa elahlekile odongeni oluncane.

## Cindezela uphume ngapha

Ukucindezela indawo ngaphakathi kwesolid kuyama endaweni yokuqala ngemuva kwayo. Lapho ifinyelela leyo ndawo, iqeqesha ivulo. Yiyo indlela yokwenza amawindi namasuku odongeni olunobukhulu. Bona [Sika ivulo odongeni](topic:openings).

## Thayipha ibanga

Ngemva kokuchofoza indawo, thayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

- Ibanga elithayiphiwe lihamba ngendlela oyihambayo. Uma ungakahambi, lihamba ngasembhoksini yendawo (ngaphandle kwesolid, phezulu ngaphansi).
- Thayipha inombolo engemihle, njengo-`-500`, ukuya ngendlela ephambene.
- Uma ushintsha umqondo wakho ngaphambi kokuchofoza, cindezela {{key:Esc}}. Indawo ibuyela endaweni yayo.

## Phinda ibanga lokugcina

Ngemva kokusunduza/hudula kanye, chofoza kabili enye indawo ngo-{{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} isakhethiwe. Ihamba ibanga elifanayo. Yiyo indlela esheshayo yokunika izindonga noma izitezi eziningi ukuthi zibe nobukhulu noma ubude obufanayo.

I-ModelCraft ikhumbula ibanga kuze kushintshe ithuluzi.

## Okufanele wazi

- Sunduza/hudula isebenza endaweni eyodwa ngesikhathi. Uma uchofoza into engeyona indawo, ibha yesimo ithi "Push/pull works on a face. Click a face."
- Ukuze usunduze/hudule indawo ngaphakathi kweqembu noma kukhomponenti, vula kuqala (chofoza kabili kuyo).
- Ngo-**Hlangana** evuliwe, ukusunduza okuhlangana nesolid esinye kuyama lapho kuthinta khona, futhi ithiphu ithi yini ethintwayo (igama lento, noma "loose geometry in this group") futhi ikhanyisa izindawo ezihlangene nazo. Umgodi osunduza ngaphakathi kwesolid yawo (isibonelo indawo ehlukile esunduzwa phansi) uyingxenye yaleyo solid futhi awuyeki ngezindonga zawo.
- Izolid ezimbili ezisunduzwa ngaphakathi kwenye azisiki noma zihlanganise: izindawo zazo ziyadlula nje. Bona [Akukakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).
