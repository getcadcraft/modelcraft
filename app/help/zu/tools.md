Isiluleko sebha yesimo sihlale sisho ukuthi ithuluzi lamanje lifuna ini okulandelayo. {{key:Esc}} ikhansela lokho ithuluzi eliphakathi kokwenza.

## Amathuluzi okudweba

| Ithuluzi | Ukhiye | Ukuchofoza | Thayipha ebhokisini lenani |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Umugqa}} | {{key:L}} | Okokuqala, bese ichofoza amaphoyinti alandelayo. Iyama lapho ubuso buvala, noma nge-{{key:Esc}} | Ubude oluya kusikhombi; `[x,y,z]` iphoyinti; `<dx,dy,dz>` ukuguqulwa ({{ui:Ubude}}) |
| {{ui:Unxande}} | {{key:R}} | Ikhona yokuqala (phansi noma ubuso), ikhona ephambene | Ububanzi nobujulile: `4m,3m` ({{ui:Izilinganiso}}) |
| {{ui:Indilinga}} | {{key:C}} | Isikhungo (phansi noma ubuso), iphoyinti emaphethelweni | Ngaphambi kokuchofoza kokuqala: izinhlangothi, `24s` noma `24` ({{ui:Izinhlangothi}}). Ngemva kwalokho: umsakazo ({{ui:Umsakazo}}) |
| {{ui:Ipholigoni}} | {{key:N}} | Isikhungo, ikhona | Njengakuthi {{ui:Indilinga}}; iqala ngezinhlangothi ezi-6 |
| {{ui:Umnsalo}} | {{key:A}} | Okokuqala, okokugcina, bese ugoba | Ubude besitimela ngemva kokuqala ({{ui:Ubude}}); ukugoba ngemva kokugcina ({{ui:Ukugoba}}); `12s` kumasegmenti |
| {{ui:Iprothrakhtha}} | — | Isikhungo, iphoyinti le-0°, bese i-engeli | I-engeli ngamadigri ({{ui:I-engeli}}) |
| {{ui:Ngilandele}} | — | Khetha indlela, bese uchofoza ubuso (noma chofoza ubuso, bese umphetho wendlela) | — |
| {{ui:Penda}} | {{key:B}} | Ubuso, iqembu noma isakhi | — ({{key:Ctrl}} ohlangene, {{key:Shift}} wonke umbala ofanayo, {{key:Alt}} thatha umbala) |
| {{ui:Phambanisa}} | {{key:F}} | Ubuso (noma khetha ubuso noma imiphetho kuqala), bese uhlangothi nebanga | Ibanga ({{ui:Ibanga}}); ngaphakathi kobuso kuyinhlangothi yangaphakathi |
| {{ui:Ijika}} | — | Amaphoyinti ijika edlula kuwo; {{key:Enter}} noma ukuchofoza kabili kuqeda | Ubude oluya ngasekhomponi, `[x,y,z]` noma `<dx,dy,dz>` ephoyintini elilandelayo |
| {{ui:Umnsalo osuka embindeni}} | — | Isikhungo, okokuqala, bese okokugcina | Umsakazo ngemva kwesikhungo; i-engeli ngamadigri ngemva kokuqala; `12s` kumasegmenti |

## Amathuluzi okuhlela

| Ithuluzi | Ukhiye | Ukuchofoza | Thayipha ebhokisini lenani |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Khetha}} | {{key:Space}} | Chofoza, chofoza kabili, chofoza kathathu, donsa ibhokisi. Bheka [Khetha izinto](topic:select) | Nesilinganiso esisodwa noma umphetho okhethiwe: ubude obusha, obushintsha usayizi wemodeli |
| {{ui:Sunduza/hudula}} | {{key:P}} | Ubuso, bese ibanga. Chofoza kabili ubuso ukuze uphinde ibanga lokugcina | Ibanga; ibanga elibi liya ngakolunye uhlangothi ({{ui:Ibanga}}) |
| {{ui:Hambisa}} | {{key:M}} | Kusuka kuphoyinti, kuya kuphoyinti. Bamba {{key:Ctrl}} noma {{key:Alt}} ukukopisha | Ibanga oliya kusikhombi, `<dx,dy,dz>` noma `[x,y,z]` ({{ui:Ibanga}}). Ngemva kokukopisha: `3x` noma `/3` |
| {{ui:Jikisa}} | {{key:Q}} | Isikhungo (kuso ubuso ukuze ujikise endizeni yalo), iqondo lokuqala, iqondo lokugcina | I-engeli ngamadigri ({{ui:I-engeli}}) |
| {{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} | {{key:S}} | Iphoyinti elihlala, iphoyinti lesethulo, usayizi omusha | Isici: `2`, `0.5` ({{ui:Shintsha usayizi}}) |
| {{ui:Isifafazi}} | {{key:E}} | Chofoza noma donsa phezu kwemiphetho, amaqembu, imihlahlandlela nezilinganiso | Lutho |
| {{ui:Ithephu}} | {{key:T}} | Kumphetho: umugqa wokuhlola ofanayo. Kuphoyinti: ilinganisa bese ishiya iphoyinti lesiqondisi | Ukuphambanisa ({{ui:Phambanisa}}) noma ibanga ({{ui:Ubude}}) |
| {{ui:Isilinganiso}} | {{key:D}} | Amakhona amabili (noma umphetho owodwa), bese kuthi lapho umugqa wesilinganiso uya khona. Bheka [Isilinganiso nokushintsha usayizi](topic:dimensions) | Ukuphambanisa ({{ui:Phambanisa}}). Ngemva nje: ubude obusha, obushintsha usayizi wemodeli |

{{ui:Hambisa}}, {{ui:Jikisa}} kanye {{ui:Shintsha usayizi}} zisebenza ngokukhethiwe. Uma kungakhethiwe lutho, zithatha lokho ochofoza kukho kuqala (ubuso buletha imiphetho yalo).

## Amathuluzi ekhamera

| Ithuluzi | Ukhiye | Donsa ukuze |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Zungeza}} | {{key:O}} | Jikela ngaphezulu kwengxenye yemodeli ephansi kwesikhombi ({{key:Shift}} shelelisa) |
| {{ui:Shelelisa}} | {{key:H}} | Shelelisa umbono |
| {{ui:Sondeza}} | {{key:Z}} | Sondeza: phezulu kuyasondela, phansi kuyakude |
| {{ui:Sondeza konke}} | {{key:Shift+Z}} | (inkinobho, hhayi ithuluzi) Lwanela imodeli yonke |

Akudingeki kakhulu ukusebenzisa lezi nge-mouse: donsa phakathi ukuzungeza, donsa kwesokudla ukushelelisa, bese isikhombisi sisondeza nganoma yiliphi ithuluzi. Bheka [Zungeza, shelelisa nosondeze](topic:orbit-pan-zoom).

## Hlela

|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Isakhi}} | {{key:G}} | Yenza ukukhethwa kube yisakhi |

## Beka isakhi

Akukho kwibha yamathuluzi: chofoza {{ui:Beka}} eduze kwesakhi ephaneli {{ui:Izakhi}}. Chofoza kanye lapho kuya khona umsuka wesakhi; ithuluzi bese libuyela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.
