Khetha inkinobho ethi **Vula ku…** ohlwini lwe-Injini lwe-{{ui:Renda isithombe…}} ukuthatha isigcawu kolunye uhlelo. Akukho okubuyela emodelini.

## Ifayela kunoma yiluphi uhlelo

- **Ifayela le-USD** lilondoloza isigcawu njenge-`.usdc` (noma `.usdz` uma imodeli inezithombe): [lelo fayela elifanayo njenge-Londoloza njenge-USD](topic:usd), nezinto zempahla, amalambu namakhamera. Igcina ukuphindwa kwezinto nezinani eliqondile lezinto zempahla. Imiklamo eminingi, izinjini zemidlalo nababoni bafunda i-USD.
- **Ifayela le-glTF (.glb)** lilondoloza ifayela elilula le-glTF 2.0 nempahla, izinhlobo nezibani; bona [Khipha imodeli yezinye izinhlelo](topic:export-model) ngamafomethi aseqinisweni ModelCraft ayabhala.
- **Ifolda yephrojekthi ye-Unreal (.zip)** ilondoloza ifolda [Thumela isigcawu kwinjini yemidlalo](topic:render-unreal) eyibhalayo, yephrojekthi noma yikuphi okuyikhompyutha.

Kokuthathu kusebenza kusiphequluli nasku-Studio.

## Ku-Studio: vula kuhlelo

I-Studio ibala izinhlelo ezitholakala kule khompyutha ezingavula isigcawu: i-usdview uma isendleleni yakho yokusesha, nohlelo lwekhompyutha ngohlobo lwefayela. Khetha olunye, khetha i-USD noma i-glTF lapho olufunda kokubili, bese uchofoza {{ui:Renda}}. I-Studio ibhala iphakheji efolda entsha ngaphansi kwe-Documents (`CadCraft hand-off`; akukho okubhalwa phezu, ukuthumela ngakunye kunefolda yakho) bese iqala uhlelo kuyo, ibe isitshela lapho amafayela akhona.

| Uhlelo | Lufunda kangcono |
|---|---|
| usdview | USD |
| Uhlelo lwekhompyutha | Noma yini ikhompyutha enayo ngohlobo lwefayela |

I-Studio iqala izinhlelo ohlwini oluthile kuphela; ayiqhubezi lutho okucelwe ikhasi ngegama, futhi ayiqali uhlelo ngaphansi kwelayisense ye-GNU GPL (bona [Dweba isithombe ngolunye uhlelo](topic:render-other-program#programs-cadcraft-never-runs)). Noma yiluphi uhlelo olufunda i-USD noma i-glTF luvula ifayela elisuka ku-**Ifayela kunoma yiluphi uhlelo** lapha ngenhla.

:::note
I-Twinmotion, i-Enscape nezinye izinhlelo zokubonisa azikho ohlwini: i-CadCraft ayikahlolwa nazo futhi ayikhulumi ngazo. Ifayela le-glTF noma le-USD elivela lapha yindlela yokungena.
:::
