Ipulani lesigaba lisika imodeli ivuleke, ukuze ubone futhi usebenze ngaphakathi kwayo: uhlelo lwendlu, ingaphakathi likabhodwe. Imodeli ayishintshwa; ukusika kuwumbono kuphela.

## Beka elilodwa

1. Chofoza {{ui:Section}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Camera}}.
2. Chofoza ubuso. Ipulani libekwa phezu kwalobo buso, ngamacembu amane aphethe kuwe.

   Chofoza kude nanoma yibuphi ubuso ukuze uthole ipulani esikwele endleleni obheka kuyo kakhulu. Cindezela inkinobho yomcibisholo kuqala ukuze umise ngesikwele kubomvu ({{key:Right}}), koluhlaza ({{key:Left}}) noma koluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka ({{key:Up}}).

Uhlangothi ogxilwe yimicibisholo lusikwa **lususwe**. Ipulani ebekwe ebusweni ayisiki lutho okwamanje: liyidonse ngaphakathi kwemodeli.

## Yidonse, uyiphendulele, uyicime

Chofoza umugqa wepulani ukuze uyikhethe. Ku-{{ui:Entity info}}:

- {{ui:Along its arrows}} ibonisa lapho ihlala khona. Bhala ibanga elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuyidonsela ngaphakathi kwemodeli; ukubhala `-1m` kuphlanyelwe ebusweni bangaphambili kuyihambisela ngaphakathi kwemodeli ngu-1 m.
- {{ui:Reverse}} ijikisa imicibisholo, ukuze olunye uhlangothi lusikwe.
- {{ui:Active (cuts the model)}}: ipulani eyodwa isika ngesikhathi. Tika ibhokisi lenye ipulani, noma beka enye, bese leyo isika esikhundleni.
- Yiqamba kabusha ku-{{ui:Name}}; uyisule ngokucindezela {{key:Delete}} noma nge-{{ui:Eraser}}.

Lapho ipulani idlula isolidi evaliwe, ukusika kugcwaliswa ngombala omnyama. Ijiyomethri engavaliwe (ubuso obubodwa, ibhokisi elivulekile) iyasikwa kodwa ayigcwaliswa.

## Bonisa noma fihla

Ku-{{ui:Views}}:

- {{ui:Show section planes}} ifihla imigqa yamapulani nemicibisholo (ukusika kuhlala).
- {{ui:Show section cuts}} icisha ukusika futhi ibonise imodeli yonke, ngaphandle kokususa amapulani.

[Umbono wokwethula](topic:styles-shadows#presentation-view) nawo ufihla imigqa.

## Okufanele wazi

- Lokho okusikwe kususwa ekuchofoleni noma ekuboneni, ngakho ungadweba phezu kwalokho ukusika kukubonisa.
- Amapulani esigaba, eyodwa esebenzayo nezinkinobho ezimbili zigcinwa kufayela le-ModelCraft. Ushintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- Ukuthunyelwa kuhlala kuqukethe imodeli yonke: isigaba ngumbono, hhayi ukuhlela.
