I-{{ui:Umtapo wokuqala}} inikeza izinto zansuku zonke ngosayizi wangempela, ukuze imodeli ifundeke ngokushesha: umnyango ukukhombisa usayizi wegumbi, imoto endleleni, abantu nezihlahla eduze kwesakhiwo.

## Beka into

1. Ohlwini {{ui:Izakhi}} ngakwesobunxele, chofoza {{ui:Umtapo wokuqala}}.
2. Khetha into, njengo-{{ui:Isihlalo}}.
3. Chofoza lapho iyokuya khona.

Phansi, etafuleni noma emhlabathini, imi phezu kwayo. **Iminyango namawindi** yakhiwe ukuze ingene ezindongeni: chofoza udonga bese zimi kulo, zibheke ngaphandle. (Zihlala phezu kobuso bodonga; azisiki ukuvulwa. Ukuze wenze enye, dweba unxande odongeni lapho kuzofakwa iwindi bese uyisunduza ngaphakathi.)

Into ngayinye ingu-**isakhi**: ukukhetha into efanayo futhi kubeka ikhophi enye, futhi zonke izikhophi ziyashintsha uma uhlela eyodwa. Iphinde ibonakale ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Izakhi}}, ne-{{ui:Beka}} ukuze wengeze eminingi.

## Yini okukuyo

| Iqembu | Izinto |
|---|---|
| Iminyango namawindi | Umnyango, umnyango owabelana ngobubili, iwindi, iwindi elibanzi, umnyango wegalaji |
| Izinsiza | Itafula lokudlela, itafula elijikelezayo, isihlalo, isihlalo sebha, isofa, isihlalo esinezingalo, ibhedi ebanzi nencane, idesiki, ishelefu yezincwadi, ikhabethe lezingubo |
| Ikhishi nendlu yokugezela | Ikhabethe elisezansi nelisezulwini, ikhawunta yekhishi, ifriji, isitofu, iyunithi yesinki, ithoyilethi, ibhavu, ishawa, isinki lokugeza |
| Isakhiwo | Isitebhisi esiqondile (ama-riser angu-15 ayi-18 cm), insika eyindilinga nesikwele, ibhimu |
| Indawo nabantu | Isihlahla, isihlahlana, umuntu (1.75 m), imoto |
| Okuzungezile: izihlahla, abantu nezimoto | Izihlahla ezikhahlayo (4, 6 no-10 m), isihlahla sekhoniferi, isihlahla esinde, izihlahlana ezindilinga nezincane, ugebe, isitshalo esisesitsheni, abantu abamile nabahambayo, ingane, imoto yesedan neye-SUV: izimo ezinamanani ngemibala yazo, za [imifanekiso enikezwe](topic:render) nemipulani yezayithi |

Zimo elula, zakhiwe ngezinto eziqinile ongazipenda, ongazishintshela usayizi, ongazenza [eziyingqayizivele](topic:groups-components) futhi ongazishintsha. Ikhabethe lodonga liphakama ngo-1.45 m ngaphezulu kwalapho uchofoza khona.

Okuzungezile kudwetshwe yi-CadCraft futhi kukhululekile ukusetshenziswa noma yini (CC0); izihlahla, abantu nezimoto ezifanayo zitholakala ku-umtapo we-Library wa-BimCraft ne-CadCraft. Amawindi emoto apenteka ngempahla ethi {{ui:Ingilazi enemibala}}, ngakho avumela ukubona ngaphakathi ezithombeni.

## Hlobisa igumbi lokurenda

Khetha ifloor yegumbi (ubuso obubodwa obubheka phezulu, kumodeli noma ngaphakathi kweqembu) bese usebenzisa **{{ui:Lungisa}} › Hlobisa igumbi…** (inkinobho **Hlobisa**). Khetha ukuthi igumbi ngani — igumbi lokulala, igumbi lokuhlala, igumbi lokudlela, ikhishi, indlu yokugezela noma ihhovisi — noma vumela kuqagele ngosayizi. Izinsiza zokuqala zimi ngokumelene nezindonga kusuka kudonga elide kakhulu kuzungeze, akukho okuhlanganayo, futhi ukugoqeka kwanoma yimuphi umnyango wokuqala obekwe ezindongeni zegumbi (nobubanzi bamawindi ayo, phambi kwezinto ezinde) kuhlala kukhululekile. Yisakhiwo sokuzenzakalela ngemithetho ongayihlola, hhayi idizayini: igumbi elifanayo lihlala linesakhiwo esifanayo.

Konke kungena **eqenjini elilodwa** ngethegi **Izinsiza**, njengesinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa: Hlehlisa, susa iqembu noma fihla ithegi ukuze ukususe. Into engalingani ibizwa kumugqa wesimo futhi ibhalwa ku-**Izinkinga** njengesaziso. I-**Hlobisa igumbi** ye-BimCraft isebenzisa imithetho efanayo.
