Lapho ishape eyodwa idlula kwelinye, i-ModelCraft ayidwebi umugqa lapho izinhlangothi zabo zihlangana: zihlangana nje. I-**Hlanganisa** idweba leyo migqa, njengemiphetho, ukuze izinhlangothi zihlukaniswe khona. Bese ungaphusha/hudula, usule noma upende izingxenye.

Zombili izimiyalo zisesikhundleni se-{{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}}.

## Hlanganisa nemodeli

Isika okukhethiwe ngokumelene **nazo zonke izinhlangothi ezisesikrinini**.

Isibonelo, umthambo (iqembu) udlula odongeni (elinye iqembu), futhi ufuna ukuvula udonga:

1. Chofoza kabili udonga ukuze uvule, bese ukhetha zonke izinhlangothi zalo ({{key:Ctrl+A}}).
2. {{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Intersect with model}}.

Umugqa wembonakalo yomthambo udweba kuzo zombili izinhlangothi zodonga, njengezinhlangothi ezintsha ezimbili. Phusha enye yazo idlule odongeni ukuze wenze ukuvuleka, bese uvala udonga.

## Hlanganisa nokukhethiwe

Isika izinhlangothi, amaqembu nezakhi ezikhethiwe **ngokumelene nazo zodwa**. Khetha okungenani ezimbili.

## Lapho imigqa iya khona

Imigqa ihlale idwetshwa **ngaphakathi kwesimo esivulekile**: emodelini uma kungavulwa iqembu, noma eqenjini ohlela kulo. Amaqembu nezakhi ezingavulwanga azishintshi. Ngakho ukusika izinhlangothi zeqembu, vula kuqala, njengesibonelo esingenhla.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Izinhlangothi ezilele endizeni efanayo azihlanganiswa.
- Imigqa evala ilupu ohlangothini yenza uhlangothi olusha, njengoba imigqa edweshiwe yenza.
- Ukuhlanganisa ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokulanda. Uma akukho okusikayo, akukho okushintshayo.
- Ngezimo ezivaliwe, [amathuluzi amandla](topic:solids) (Union, Subtract) yenza umsebenzi wonke ngesinyathelo esisodwa.
