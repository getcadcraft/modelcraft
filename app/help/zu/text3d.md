{{ui:Umbhalo we-3D}} kweqembu {{ui:Dweba}} wenza izinhlamvu zibe ngobuso, ongazipenda, ungazisunduza/uhudule noma uzinqume zingene esimweni esiqinile.

## Engeza umbhalo

1. Chofoza {{ui:Umbhalo we-3D}}.
2. Thayipha umbhalo. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} kubhokisi ukuze uthole umugqa wesibili.
3. Setha {{ui:Ukuphakama kwezinhlamvu}} (ukuphakama kwesinhlamvu esinkulu, njengo `30cm` noma `1'`) kanye {{ui:Ukujula}}:
   - `0` yenza **izinhlamvu ezicaba**: ubuso ebhekisa phezu kwalokho okubabeka kukho, ezilungele ukupenda noma ukusunduza/ukuhudula.
   - Ukujula okufana no `5cm` yenza **izinhlamvu eziqinile**, izinqamu ezivaliwe ongazisebenzisa nezinsizakalo [zezinto eziqinile](topic:solids).
4. Chofoza {{ui:Beka}}, bese uchofoza lapho umbhalo okuya khona.

Ebusweni, umbhalo ulala phezu kwalobo buso, ufundeka usuka kwesokunxele uye kwesokudla: odongeni umi uqondile; emhlabeni noma etafuleni ulala usicaba ngomugqa obomvu. Kude nanoma yiluphi ubuso ulala emhlabeni.

Umbhalo uyisakhi **esiqanjwe ngawo**, ngakho wonke amakhophi ashintsha uma uhlela esinye, futhi sibonakala ohlwini {{ui:Izakhi}} ukuze sibekwe futhi.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Izinhlamvu ziyifonti yeModelCraft yomugqa-owodwa, ezinikezwe ububanzi: zicacile futhi zifundeka, azikho ukukhetha kohlobo lwefonti okwamanje.
- Izinhlamvu, izinombolo nezimpawu ezivamile (kuhlanganise nezinhlamvu ezinamakhamba) zidwetshwa; uhlamvu lefonti lingenayo lubonakala njengebhokisi elincane.
- Ukuze ushintshe amagama, susa umbhalo bese uwungeza futhi.
- Ukuze ubhale izinhlamvu ebusweni, yenza izinhlamvu eziqinile, uzibeke zingene ingxenye ngaphakathi kweqembu eliqinile, bese [uzikhipha](topic:solids) kulo.
