**Imbonini** igcina indawo yekhamera, isitayela nalokho okufihliwe: ukuthi ubukela kusuphi, i-perspective noma i-parallel, isitayela sobuso, imiphetho, izilungiselelo zelanga, amaqembu afihliwe, amathegi avaliwe nokusikwa kwesigaba. Izimbonini zigcinwa kufayela le-`.modelcraft`.

## Engeza imbonini

1. Lungisa umbono: zungeza, sondeza, khetha isitayela, setha ilanga.
2. Ephaneli ye-{{ui:Imiboniso}} ngakwesokudla, chofoza {{ui:Engeza umboniso}}.

   Imbonini iveza ohlwini futhi njengethebhu phezu kombono we-3D, iqanjwe ngokuthi "Imbonini 1", "Imbonini 2" njalo. Ibha yesimo ithi "Imbonini 1 ilondoloziwe: le kheshi, isitayela nalokho okufihliwe."

## Yanga embonini

- Chofoza ithebhu yayo phezu kombono we-3D, noma igama layo ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Imiboniso}}.
- Noma cindezela {{key:PageDown}} ukuya embonini elandelayo, no-{{key:PageUp}} ukuya kwendulele. {{ui:Okulandelayo}} no-{{ui:Okwangaphambili}} ngaphansi kohlu benza okufanayo.

Ikhamera iyashelela iye embonini ngaphezu kwesigamu sesekhondi. Uma ikhompyutha yakho isethelwe ukunciphisa ukunyakaza, igxuma kuyo ngokushesha.

## Shintsha, qamba kabusha noma sula imbonini

Imbonini ngayinye ohlwini inamanku amathathu angemva kwegama layo:

- **Buyekeza** (izingalo eziyindilinga): igcina ikhamera yamanje, isitayela nalokho okufihliwe kuleyo mbonini. "Imbonini 2 ibuyekeziwe."
- **Qamba kabusha** (ipensela): ivula ingxoxo ethi "Qamba kabusha imbonini". Thayipha igama elisha bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}.
- **Sula** (ibhini): isusa imbonini. "Imbonini 2 isusiwe. Ctrl+Z iyibuyisela."

Ukungeza, ukubuyekeza, ukuqamba kabusha nokusula izimbonini kuyizinyathelo zokuhlehlisa, njengezinguquko emodelini.

## Khiya ikhamera yembonini

Lapho urenda umbono ofanayo njalo ngenkathi ukuklama kushintsha, khiya ikhamera yembonini ukuze umbono ungahambi ngephutha.

1. Ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Imiboniso}}, chofoza inkinobho **yokukhiya** (ikhiye elivulekile) ngemva kwegama lembonini. Iyavala, bese ithebhu yembonini phezu kombono we-3D ibonisa ikhiye elincane. Ibha yesimo ithi "Imbonini 2: ikhamera ikhiyiwe."
2. Hamba embonini lapho ufuna ukurenda: ikhamera ibuyela ngqo embonweni ogcinwe, ngakho izithombe ezenziwe kuwo zihambisana nezangaphambili.

Lapho ikhamera ikhiyiwe, **Buyekeza** igcina isitayela nalokho okufihliwe embonini, kodwa ikhamera ihlala lapho ibikhona, nakuba ubuye ujikelezile. Chofoza ikhiye futhi ukuze uyivule; bese Buyekeza igcina umbono wamanje futhi. Ukukhiya nokuvula kuyizinyathelo zokuhlehlisa, nokukhiya kugcinwa kufayela le-`.modelcraft`.

## Ethula

1. Engeza izimbonini ezimbili noma ngaphezulu.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Dlala}} ngaphansi kohlu. I-ModelCraft ithuthela embonini elandelayo njalo emasekhondini ambalwa futhi ibuyele kokuqala ngemva kokuwugcina.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Misa}} (inkinobho efanayo) noma cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze umise.

Ukwenza ucwaningo lwelanga, engeza imbonini eyodwa ngehora ngalinye, ushintsha kuphela {{ui:Isikhathi}}, bese udlala. Bona [Izitayela nezithunzi zelanga](topic:styles-shadows#a-sun-study).

Ukuze wethule ngaphandle kwamabha, cindezela {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} kabili: isikrini esigcwele, bese isikrini esigcwele namabha efihliwe. Amathebhu embonini ahlala phezu kombono.

## Okubonakalayo embonini

Imbonini ikhumbula lokho okwakufihliwe lapho uyigcina: amaqembu namakhomponenti owa-[fihla](topic:hide-isolate), [amathegi](topic:tags) owavala, nokuthi yiziphi [izinqwaba zesigaba](topic:sections) ezisika. Ukuya embonini kuyawafihla futhi kuwavule kabusha. Ngakho embonini eyodwa ingabonisa indlu yonke, elandelayo indlu nethegi yophahla ivaliwe, elandelayo ukusika ngayo.

Ubuso nemiphetho ezifihliwe ezingaxhunyiwe akuyona ingxenye yezimbonini. Ukuya embonini akwenzi isinyathelo sokuhlehlisa. Izimbonini ezigcinwe yi-build yangaphambili ye-ModelCraft zishiya okufihliwe njengoba kunjalo; chofoza Buyekeza ukuze ukugcine kuzo.

## Khipha i-animation

{{ui:Export animation}}, ngaphansi kohlu, idlala izimbonini kusukela kokuqala kuya kokugcina, ihambisa ikhamera ngobunono phakathi kwazo, futhi igcina ifilimu njenge-**WebM** ividiyo (ukuhamba kwesekhondi ezimbili nokuma okufushane ngembonini ngayinye). Irekhoda umbono njengoba ubona, ngosayizi wawo esikrinini, ku-[umbono wokwethula](topic:styles-shadows#presentation-view) (akukho ama-axis, imihlahlandlela noma ukukhethwa). Cindezela {{key:Esc}} noma chofoza inkinobho futhi ukuze umise ngaphandle kokugcina. I-WebM idlala kumasiphequluli nase-VLC, nabahleli bevidiyo bayayiguqula.

Uma ufuna uhambo olurenderiwe (isibhakabhaka, ilanga, izithunzi, izinto zokwakha) ngosayizi okhethayo, sebenzisa {{ui:Renda ividiyo…}} esikhundleni: ngaphandle kokhiye be-animation ithatha izimbonini, ngokulandelana, njengendlela yekhamera. Bona [Yenza ikhamera nezinto zibe ne-animation](topic:animation).

## Okufanele wazi

- Ukushintsha isitayela noma ukuhambisa ikhamera ngemva kokuya embonini akushintshi imbonini kuze kuchofolwe Buyekeza.
- Izimbonini azithunyelwa ku-Collada, OBJ, glTF, STL noma IFC.
