{{ui:Move}} ({{key:M}}) ihambisa noma yini okhethiwe isuka endaweni eyodwa iyiyo enye. Ukubamba ukhiye wokuguqula kwenza kube ikhophi, futhi ngemva kokukopisha ungathayipha ukuthi ufuna amanye amaningi kangakanani.

## Hambisa into

1. Khetha okuthile ofuna ukukuhambisa (bona [Khetha izinto](topic:select)). Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, {{ui:Move}} uthatha into ochofoza kuyo.
2. Cindezela {{key:M}}.
3. Chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala lokuhambisa. Khetha into ebalulekile, njengekhona: leliphoyinti liyofika ngqo lapho uchofoza khona okulandelayo.
4. Hambisa isikhombi. Okukhethiwe kuyalandela. Kubamba amaphoyinti, imiphetho nezinqondo ezibomvu, eziluhlaza nezizuri njengoba umugqa wenza.
5. Chofoza lapho iphoyinti liyofika, noma thayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

   Ithiphu yebha yesimo ibuyela ku-"Chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala lokuhambisa…" ukuze kube yokuhambisa okulandelayo.

Ibanga elithayipha liya endleleni okhomba ngayo. Ungathayipha futhi ukushintshwa okunembile, `<2m,0,0>` (2 m ngomugqa obomvu), noma indawo engenzeka ngokunembile yephoyinti oliyikhethile, `[0,0,0]` (i-origin). Bona [Thayipha izilinganiso ezinembile](topic:value-box).

Ukuhambisa ngomugqa owodwa kuphela, cindezela inkinobho yomcibisholo ngemva kochofozo lokuqala: {{key:Right}} ikhiya kubomvu, {{key:Left}} kuluhlaza, {{key:Up}} kuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka. Cindezela umcibisholo ofanayo ukuze uvule.

## Ukuhambisa ingxenye yesakhiwo kuyisoleka

Okungahlanganisiwe kuyaxhunyiwe. Uma ukhetha uhlangothi olulodwa noma imiphetho embalwa uyihambise, izinhlangothi ezixhunyiwe kuyo ziyasoleka ukuze zilandele. Hambisa uhlangothi oluphezulu bhokisi ngasohlangothini, bhokisi liyakhengama; hambisa omunye umphetho wophahla, uphahla luyashintsha ukuthambeka. Izinhlangothi ezingasasali zigoqeke zibe izinxantathu.

Ukuze uhambise into yonke ngaphandle kokuyisoleka, yikhethe yonke kuqala (chofoza kathathu), noma kangcono, yenze iqembu noma ikhomponenti (bona [Amaqembu nezikhomponenti](topic:groups-components)).

## Kopisha into

1. Khetha okuthile ofuna ukukopisha.
2. Cindezela {{key:M}}.
3. Bamba {{key:Ctrl}} (noma {{key:Alt}}) bese uchofoza iphoyinti lokuqala lokuhambisa. Ku-Mac, {{key:Mod}} noma {{key:Alt}} zenza okufanayo. Ithiphu ithi "Ctrl ihambisa kunalokho", okusho ukuthi uyakopisha.

   Ungaphinda ucindezele bese ukhulula {{key:Ctrl}} noma {{key:Alt}} ngemva kochofozo lokuqala ukushintsha phakathi kokuhambisa nokukopisha.

4. Chofoza lapho ikhophi iyofika, noma thayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

   Okokuqala kuhlala lapho kwakukhona, futhi ikhophi iyakhethwa.

## Kopisha, sika, namathisela bese uphinda

- {{key:Mod+C}} ikopisha okukhethiwe, {{key:Mod+X}} ikusika (ikopisha, bese iyasusa), futhi {{ui:Copy}} no {{ui:Paste}} nazo ziyizinkinobho kweqembu {{ui:Edit}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi, kanti {{ui:More}} eseduze kwazo inokusika, namathisela endaweni efanayo nokuphinda.
- {{key:Mod+V}} inamathisela: umumo wekhophi uyalandela isikhombi, uhamba njengamanye amathuluzi, futhi ukuchofoza kuyawubeka ngophansi kwawo okungcono kakhulu lapho. {{key:Esc}} iyakhansela.
- {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} inamathisela endaweni efanayo, lapho ikhophi yavela khona: kuwusizo ukuthutha okuthile kuqembu elivuliwe.
- {{key:Mod+D}} ({{key:Shift+D}} ku-style ye-Modeler) iphinda: ikhophi iyenziwa futhi ihamba nesikhombi ngokushesha; chofoza ukuyibeka, thayipha ibanga, noma cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuyishiya. Ngemva kwalokho, thayipha `3x` ukuthola amanye amakhophi ngokulandelana.

Iqembu elinamathiselwe liba iqembu elisha. Ikhomponenti enamathiselwe iwukubekwa okunye kwale khomponenti, ngakho ukuhlela eyodwa kushintsha zonke. Amakhophi nawo angamathiselwa kwenye ithebhu ye-ModelCraft kulesi sisekeli (lapho angena njengezincazelo ezintsha).

## Umugqa wamakhophi

Ngemva nje kokukopisha, ngenkathi {{ui:Move}} isasethuluzi:

- Thayipha `3x` (noma `*3`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuthola amakhophi amathathu ngokulandelana, ngalinye ebangeni elifanayo nekhophi lokuqala. Uthola amakhophi amathathu amasha ngaphandle kokuqala.
- Thayipha `/3` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuhlukanisa: amakhophi amathathu asakazeka ngokulinganayo kusuka kokuqala kuya endaweni lapho ubeke khona ikhophi yokuqala, eyokugcina ilapho. Sebenzisa lokhu ukugcwalisa ibanga: beka ikhophi eyodwa ekugcineni elikude, bese uhlukanisa.

Thayipha inombolo ehlukile ngemva kwalokho: `5x` ishintsha umugqa we-`3x`. Ithiphu iyakukhumbuza: "Kukopishiwe. Thayipha 3x ukuthola amakhophi amaningi ngokulandelana, noma /3 ukuhlukanisa ibanga."

### Isibonelo: amakholomu njalo nge-6 meter

1. Yenza ikholomu bese uyenza ikhomponenti ({{key:G}}), ukuze wonke amakhophi ashintshe ndawonye kamuva.
2. Cindezela {{key:M}}, bamba {{key:Ctrl}} bese uchofoza ikhona lesisekelo sekholomu.
3. Hambisa ngomugqa obomvu bese uthayipha `6m`, bese {{key:Enter}}.
4. Thayipha `4x` bese {{key:Enter}}. Uzoba namakholomu amahlanu, ayi-6 m ngokuhlukana.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ngaphambi kochofozo lwesibili ukukhansela; konke kubuyela lapho kwakukhona.
- {{ui:Move}} isebenza ngaphakathi eqenjini oliliguqulayo. Ukuze uhambise iqembu lonke, liyeke kuqala bese ulikhetha kusuka ngaphandle.
- Uma **Collide** ivulekile (ngaphansi kwesokudla, nakuma-[ModelCraft settings](topic:settings#solids-stop-where-they-touch)), ukuhamba kuyayeka lapho okukhethiwe kuthinta esinye isolid kunokudlula kuso. Chofoza {{ui:Collide}} ukuyivala ukuhamba okufanele kudlule.
- Ukubeka ikhophi ngaphakathi kwenye iqembu: yikopishe, vula elinye iqembu, bese unamathisela ({{key:Mod+Shift+V}} igcina endaweni efanayo).
