Haddii aad ku qaabeysay SketchUp, ModelCraft wuu ku noqon doonaa mid aad ku barataa: isla fikradda wejiyada iyo push/pull, isla xarfaha qalabka, sanduuq qiime ah oo aad ku qorto adigoo aan riixin, cabbir la yaqaan xagal oo casaan, cagaar iyo buluug ah, kooxaha, qaybaha, Outliner, Entity Info iyo goobaha. Bogganu wuxuu liistaa waxa si toos ah u wada socda, iyo waxa ka sii muhiimsan oo aan wada socon.

## Farqiyo ku qaldiya dadka

### Kaydintu waa soo dejin

Ma jiro Save oo dib u qoraysa faylka aad furtay, ma jiro Save As oo leh magac, ma jiro autosave, ma jiro fayl backup ah. {{ui:Save}} ({{key:Mod+S}}) wuxuu mar kasta soo dejiyaa fayl `.modelcraft` cusub, badanaa folder-ka downloads-kaaga. Badanaa kaydi, oo magacee ama u rarr faylalka adigu. Eeg [Fur, kaydi, soo dhoofi iyo soo saar](topic:files).

### Wuu soo dhoofiyaa faylalka .skp, laakiin ma kaydiyo

{{ui:Import}} wuxuu akhrinayaa faylalka SketchUp adigoo isticmaalaya akhriyaha ModelCraft ee bogga: kooxaha, qaybaha, midabka, texture-yada wejiyada, tags iyo geesaha jilicsan ayaa soo gala. Eeg [Qaababka faylka si faahfaahsan](topic:formats#skp-files). ModelCraft ma kaydin karo `.skp`: si aad dib ugu noqoto, ModelCraft ka soo saar Collada oo ku soo dhoofi SketchUp.

### Ma jiro menu-ka midig-riix

Midig-jiid wuxuu pan-gareeyaa; midig-guji ma sameeyo wax. Amarrada aad ku raadin lahayd menu-ka guji-midig waxay ku jiraan {{ui:Entity info}} ({{ui:Reverse face}}, {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:Explode}}, {{ui:Make unique}}, {{ui:Make component}}) iyo kooxda {{ui:Organize}} ee toolbar-ka.

### Cutubyada waxay ku jiraan status bar-ka

Ma jiro Model Info ama doorashada template. Unit picker-ku wuxuu ku yaal hoose midig. Moodeel cusub wuxuu ku bilaabmaa feet iyo inches Mareykanka, mm meelaha kale, iyadoo laga bilaabayo luuqadda browser-kaaga. Tobanaan jibbaaran had iyo jeer waxay leedahay dhibic, `2.5m`, halkii `2,5m`, sababtoo ah koomaku had iyo jeer kala saaraa qiimayaasha.

### Rinjiga wuxuu leeyahay alaabtiisa

{{ui:Paint}} ({{key:B}}) wuxuu rinjiyaa midabyo adag iyo alaabta qaybta {{ui:Materials}}: dhagax-dhiig, siding, saqaf, alwaax, dhagax iyo wax ka badan, oo ModelCraft ayaa sawira. Weli ma soo rari kartid sawir aad leedahay sida alaab; tixraacyada ku imanaya faylalka la soo dhoofiyay way ilaalinayaan. Eeg [Rinji midabbo iyo alaabta](topic:paint).

### Miisaanku waa siman yahay, ma leh qabasho

Miisaanku ma muujiyo sanduuq leh qabasho. Guji barta ku sii taagan, oo ah barta tixraaca, kadibna cabbirka cusub ama factor aad qortay. Had iyo jeer wuu si siman u miisaaniyaa dhinacyada oo dhan. Si aad hal dhinac u fidiso, push/pull ama guuri wejiga, ama dib u qor ballaca, qotada ama joogta kooxda gudaha {{ui:Entity info}}, ama [dib u cabbir cabbir](topic:dimensions).

### Wareeg wuxuu doortaa diyaaradiisa meesha aad guuto

Ma jiro protractor ku dhegta wejiyada markaad fallaadhaha ku socodsiiso si aad diyaarad u doorato. Guji dhexda wejiga si aad u wareegto diyaaradda wejigaas, ama dhulka si aad u wareegto toosan. Fallaadhaha aragga ayaa wareegaya ka hor guji-ga koowaad.

### Ku-saabsanaanta barta aad marisay

ModelCraft wuxuu ku dhegaa jihooyinka tiirka laga bilaabo **barta bilowga**, iyo barta, geesaha iyo wejiyada ku hoos mouse-ka. Ma raadiyo jihada barta aad kaliya ku dul marisay (khadka dhibcaha ah ee "from point"). Si aad u toosiso geesha fog, ku xidh tiirka fallaadhaha, oo ku dul geeskaas: barta xidhan ayaa u dhaqaaqda si ay u toosanto. Ama dhig [hage](topic:guides-measure).

### Parallel wuxuu isticmaalaa geesta aad marisay ka hor bilowga

Parallel iyo perpendicular waxay isticmaalaan geesta ugu dambeysay ee mouse-ku gudbay **ka hor guji-ga koowaad**, ma aha geesaha aad ku dul marayso intaad sawirayso. Eeg [Ku-saabsanaanta iyo dhegaysiga](topic:inference#parallel-and-perpendicular).

### Nuqullada kooxda waa isku xidhan yihiin

Koobiyeynta kooxda adigoo isticmaalaya {{ui:Move}} waxay samaysaa meel kale oo isla kooxda, sidaas darteed tafatirka mid wuxuu beddelaa labadaba, sida qaybaha. SketchUp kooxaha wuu madax-bannaan yahay. Eeg [Xallinta dhibaatada](topic:troubleshooting#editing-one-group-changed-another).

### Space ma pan-garayso

{{key:Space}} wuxuu doortaa {{ui:Select}}, sida SketchUp. Web apps kale qaar Space la hayo way pan-garayaan; halkan ma sameeyo. Midig-jiid ayaa beddelkeeda pan-garaya.

### Scroll labo-farood wuu zoom-garayaa

Trackpad-ka, deji {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › Scrolling oo ah Trackpad: labo farood wuu orbit-garaya, {{key:Shift}} iyo labo farood wuu pan-garayaa, pinch wuu zoom-garayaa. Eeg [Orbit, pan iyo zoom](topic:orbit-pan-zoom#on-a-trackpad).

Haddii gacmahaagu furayaasha Blender-ka yaqaanaan halkii SketchUp, dooro habka menu-ka {{ui:Modeler}}: {{key:G}}, {{key:R}} iyo {{key:S}} waxay ku shaqeeyaan doorashada hal mar, {{key:E}} wuu soo saaraa (push/pull), {{key:Shift+D}} wuu nuqulaa, oo menu-ka sare (File, Edit, View, Select, Add, Object, Mesh) wuu bedelaa toolbar-ka. Haddii aad ka timid SketchUp, habka {{ui:Push-pull}} wuxuu siinayaa menu-bar la mid ah isla xarfaha qalabka. Eeg [Menus, keys iyo mouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).

### Jidhadka isku riixan ma gooyaan

Push/pull wuxuu ku gooyaa wejiga ka dambeeya isla shay gudahiisa, laakiin laba jidh oo kala duwan oo isku riixan ma isku gudbaan. Ma jiro Intersect Faces ama Solid Tools.

### Waxa maqan

Freehand, Trim, Split iyo Outer Shell, alaabta sawirka aad leedahay, extensions, iyo qalab layout-ka xaashiyaha ma jiraan dhismahan. [Weli lama dhisin](topic:not-built-yet) wuxuu liistaa waxa lagu sameeyo halkeeda.

## Gaagaabyada browser-ku haysto

ModelCraft wuxuu ku shaqeeyaa browser-ka, sidaas darteed gaagaabyo yar oo browser-ka ah ayaa leh, ma aha app-ka. {{key:Mod+N}} wuxuu furaa daaqad browser ah (isticmaal {{ui:New}}), {{key:Mod+W}} wuxuu xiraa tab-ka. Haddii aad isbeddel aan la kaydin ku jirto, browser-ku wuu weydiinayaa ka hor xidhitaanka. Eeg [Furayaasha, mouse-ka iyo trackpad-ka](common:keys#shortcuts-the-browser-keeps).
