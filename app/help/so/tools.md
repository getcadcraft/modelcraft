Talada xaashida xaaladda had iyo jeer waxay sheegaysaa waxa qalabka hadda uu rabo marka xigta. {{key:Esc}} wuxuu joojiyaa wixii qalabku ku jiro dhexdooda.

## Qalabyada sawirka

| Qalab | Furaha | Riix | Qor sanduuqa qiimaha |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Xariiq}} | {{key:L}} | Bilow, kadibna dhibic kasta oo xiga. Wuu joogsadaa marka dusha la xidho, ama markii {{key:Esc}} la riixo | Dherer oo u jeeda tusaha; `[x,y,z]` dhibic; `<dx,dy,dz>` ka fogaan ({{ui:Dherer}}) |
| {{ui:Afar-geesle}} | {{key:R}} | Geeska koowaad (dhulka ama dusha), geeska ka soo horjeeda | Ballac iyo qoto: `4m,3m` ({{ui:Cabbirrada}}) |
| {{ui:Goobo}} | {{key:C}} | Dhexda (dhulka ama dusha), dhibic ku taal geeska | Ka hor riixitaankii koowaad: dhinacyada, `24s` ama `24` ({{ui:Dhinacyada}}). Ka dib: radiuska ({{ui:Radius}}) |
| {{ui:Poligon}} | {{key:N}} | Dhexda, geesta | Sida Goobo; wuxuu ku bilaabmaa 6 dhinac |
| {{ui:Qaanso}} | {{key:A}} | Bilow, dhamaad, kadibna boos | Dhererka xarriiqda labada dhibic ka dib bilowga ({{ui:Dherer}}); boosta ka dib dhamaadka ({{ui:Kor-u-kac}}); `12s` qaybaha |
| {{ui:Protraktor}} | — | Dhexda, dhibcaha 0°, kadibna xagalka | Xagalka digrii ({{ui:Xagal}}) |
| {{ui:Iga raac}} | — | Dooro jid, kadibna riix dusha (ama riix dusha, kadibna geesta jidka) | — |
| {{ui:Rinji}} | {{key:B}} | Dusha, kooxda ama qaybta | — ({{key:Ctrl}} isku xidhan, {{key:Shift}} midabka oo dhan, {{key:Alt}} qaado midab) |
| {{ui:Ka fogaan}} | {{key:F}} | Dusha (ama horey u dooro dusha ama geesaha), kadibna dhinaca iyo masaafada | Masaafada ({{ui:Masaafo}}); gelida dusha waa gudaha |
| {{ui:Qalin}} | — | Dhibcaha uu qalinku maro; {{key:Enter}} ama laba jeer dhufo ayaa dhammaysa | Dherer oo socda tusaha, `[x,y,z]` ama `<dx,dy,dz>` dhibicda xigta |
| {{ui:Qaansada dhexdeeda}} | — | Dhexda, bilowga, kadibna dhamaadka | Radiuska ka dib dhexda; xagalka digrii ka dib bilowga; `12s` qaybaha |

## Qalabyada tafatirka

| Qalab | Furaha | Riix | Qor |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Dooro}} | {{key:Space}} | Riix, laba jeer dhufo, saddex jeer dhufo, jiid sanduuq. Eeg [Dooro waxyaabaha](topic:select) | Marka hal cabbir ama geesi la doortay: dherer cusub, taasoo beddeshaa moodeelka |
| {{ui:Riix/jiid}} | {{key:P}} | Dusha, kadibna masaafada. Dusha laba jeer dhufo si aad masaafadii ugu dambaysay u celiso | Masaafo; taban waa jihada kale ({{ui:Masaafo}}) |
| {{ui:Guuri}} | {{key:M}} | Meel ka, meel u. Ku hay {{key:Ctrl}} ama {{key:Alt}} si aad u koobiyayso | Masaafo oo u jeeda tusaha, `<dx,dy,dz>` ama `[x,y,z]` ({{ui:Masaafo}}). Koobiyee ka dib: `3x` ama `/3` |
| {{ui:Wareeji}} | {{key:Q}} | Dhexda (dusha si aad u leexiso diyaarkeeda), jihada bilowga, jihada dhamaadka | Xagalka digrii ({{ui:Xagal}}) |
| {{ui:Miisaan}} | {{key:S}} | Barta sii jirta, barta tixraaca, cabbirka cusub | Isugeyn: `2`, `0.5` ({{ui:Miisaan}}) |
| {{ui:Tirtiraha}} | {{key:E}} | Riix ama jiid geesaha, kooxaha, hagayaasha iyo cabbirrada | Waxba |
| {{ui:Teef}} | {{key:T}} | Geesta: xariiq hage oo isbarbar ah. Dhibic: cabbir oo dhibic hage ka dhig | Ka fogaanta ({{ui:Ka fogaan}}) ama masaafada ({{ui:Dherer}}) |
| {{ui:Cabbir}} | {{key:D}} | Laba geesood (ama geesta keliya), kadibna meesha xariiqda cabbirku ku dhacayso. Eeg [Cabbir iyo beddel cabbir](topic:dimensions) | Ka fogaanta ({{ui:Ka fogaan}}). Isla markiiba ka dib: dherer cusub, taasoo beddeshaa moodeelka |

{{ui:Guuri}}, {{ui:Wareeji}} iyo {{ui:Miisaan}} waxay ku shaqeeyaan xulashada. Haddii waxba la dooran, waxa aad marka hore dhufato ayay qaadaan (dusha ayaa geeskeeda keenta).

## Qalabyada kamarada

| Qalab | Furaha | Jiid si aad u |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Wareeg}} | {{key:O}} | Ku wareeg qaybta moodeelka ee tusaha hoosteeda ({{key:Shift}} wuxuu u guuraa) |
| {{ui:Muuqaalka guuri}} | {{key:H}} | Muuqaalka u jiid |
| {{ui:Weynee}} | {{key:Z}} | Weynee: sare waa gudaha, hoose waa dibadda |
| {{ui:Xuduudaha}} | {{key:Shift+Z}} | (badhan, ma aha qalab) Moodeelka oo dhan ku buuxi |

Inta badan uma baahnid kuwan iyada oo mouse la isticmaalayo: jiid dhexe wuu wareegaa, jiid midig wuu guurayaa, gurigu qalab kasta weynee. Eeg [Wareeg, guuri iyo weynee](topic:orbit-pan-zoom).

## Dhig qayb

Ma aha toolbar-ka dushiisa: riix {{ui:Dhig}} oo ku xigta qayb ku jirta guddiga {{ui:Qaybaha}}. Hal mar dhufo meesha ay asalka qaybtu tagayso; qalabku markaasuu ku laabanayaa {{ui:Dooro}}.
