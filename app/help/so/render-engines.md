{{ui:Rendar sawir…}} wuxuu leeyahay doorasho **Matoor** oo kor ka taal Mode. Laba matoorradeenna had iyo jeer way jiraan oo meel kasta ayay shaqeeyaan; kordhinno ayaa matoorro dheeraad ah ku dari kara, CadCraft Studio-na liiska ayaa sidoo kale ku jiraan barnaamijyada qaadaya muuqaalka.

| Matoor | Waxa uu sameeyo | Halka |
|---|---|---|
| Dhex ku jira: Dhakhso | Sawirka degdega ah: iftiinka maalinta, hooska jilicsan, muuqaalka aaladda iyo biraha, ilbiriqsiyo gudahood | Browser-ka iyo Studio |
| Dhex ku jira: Path tracer | Iftiinka oo ka dhacaya dusha ilaa dusha kale, kaarka muuqaalkaaga ama processor-ka, safka rendarka iyo kombiyuutarada kale ee shabakaddaada | Browser-ka iyo Studio |
| Matoorka kordhinta | Wuu sawiraa oo sawirka dib ayuu u soo celiyaa: eeg [Sawirka barnaamij kale ku sawir](topic:render-other-program) | Browser-ka (matoor JavaScript ku qoran) ama Studio (matoor barnaamij socodsiiya) |
| Barnaamij muuqaalka la wareega | Studio wuxuu geliyaa muuqaalka folderkiisa oo wuu ku ilaaliyaa wada-socodka | Studio |
| Fur ku … | Muuqaalka wuu kaydiyaa oo barnaamij ayuu ku furaa; waxba ma soo noqdo: eeg [Muuqaalka barnaamij kale ku fur](topic:render-open-in) | Webku wuxuu kaydiyaa faylka |

## Sida doorashadu u shaqeeyo

- Doorashada Dhakhso ama Path tracer waxay beddeshaa Mode-ka isagoo la socda, iyo sidoo kale dhinaca kale. Sanduuqa waxba kale kama beddelo dadka aan waligood matoor kale isticmaalin.
- Matoor aan la heli karin ayaa liiska ku sii jira sababta ("not found", "needs a newer version"); ma dooran kartid. Browser-ka matoor ka shaqeeya Studio kaliya wuxuu muujiyaa "saves a file": {{ui:Rendar sawir…}} markaas wuxuu kaydiyaa muuqaalka iyo dejintiisa fayl aad u qaadato barnaamijkaas.
- Doorashada waa la kaydiyaa dejinta rendarka faylka, matoorka aad ugu dambaysay dooratay wuxuu ahaadaa matoorka bilowga ah faylasha aan doorasho u gaar ah lahayn.
- Sawir ka yimid barnaamij kale wuxuu sheegaa barnaamijka uu sawirka sameeyay iyo nooca, sanduuqa iyo safka rendarka. Ballanqaadyada bogga [Rendar](topic:render) waxay daboolaan **matooradeenna** keliya: barnaamij kale wuxuu iftiimiyaa, midabeeyaa oo tusaaleeyaa si u gaar ah, sidaas darteed laba sawir oo muuqaal isku mid ah ayaa isku eg kamarada, qorraxda, laydhadhka iyo iftiinka, laakiin ma aha pixel ka pixel.

## Waxa matoor kasta la siiyo

Muuqaalka wuxuu u gudbaa barnaamij kale sida **pakaj**: moodeelka sida fayl USD (isla faylka [Kaydi sida USD](topic:usd) qorayo), iyo fayl dejin oo wax ka mid ah u hayaa wixii USD importer aan lagu kalsoonaan karin: qiyamka saxda ah ee agabka, laydhadhka shidan ee awoodooda iyo profaylka iftiinka, qorraxda, cirka, iftiinka iyo kamarada sida sanduuqu u sawiri lahaa. Waxba halkii lagu soo rarin.

## Safka rendarka

Sawir ama animation loo qabanayo barnaamij kale wuxuu sugi karaa [safka rendarka](topic:render#the-render-queue) sida kan aynu leenahay; shaqadiisu xasuusanaysaa matoorka ay u tahay. Animation-ku wuxuu barnaamijka u gaadhaa hal socod.
