    Groups and components you make show here. Select geometry, then G makes a component, Shift+G a group.

    Components

      No components yet. Select geometry and press G: every copy of a component changes when you edit one.

      Start modeling

      • R rectangle: click two corners on the ground, or click one and type 4m,3m and Enter.
      • P push/pull: click the face, move up, click — or type a height.
      • L line, C circle, M move (Ctrl copies), T tape measure for guides.
      • G makes the selection a component, Shift+G a group.
      • Middle-drag orbits, Shift+middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms, a right click opens the menu. Arrow keys lock red, green and blue.
      • Models open and save on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

      Colors

      Pick a color, then click faces, groups or components with Paint (B). With something selected, a click on a color paints the selection.

      Materials

      Paint faces with brick, siding, roofing, wood, stone and more, like a color: pick one, then click faces. Textures show in the Shaded style and in rendered pictures; exports get each material’s color.

      Lights

        Lamps light rendered pictures (Export › Render a picture…), most of all at night. Click Lamp, Spotlight or Light panel, then click where it goes; click a lamp’s mark in the view to change it.

        Tags

          Give groups and components a tag in Entity info, then hide or show everything with that tag here.

              Controls

              Scrolling

              Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to orbit, hold Shift and swipe to pan, pinch to zoom — no camera buttons needed. Mouse wheel: the wheel zooms. "Work it out" treats sideways or fine-grained scrolling as a trackpad.

              Touch screen

              Two fingers also pinch to zoom; three fingers do the other one. Hold one finger still on empty space, then drag, to orbit with one finger. While you draw, a loupe above your finger shows the points it snaps to.

              Snapping and solids

              Both are also buttons at the bottom right of the view, so you can turn them off for one move and back on.

              Corner indicator

              The view cube sits at the top right of the view: click a face, edge or corner to look from there, drag it to orbit, drag the compass ring to turn the model. The axis icon is a small X, Y, Z marker at the bottom left.

              Orbit and pan

              On: orbit turns about the point of the model under the cursor, and pan keeps that point under the cursor; over empty space both use the middle of the view. Off: always the middle of the view. Hold Shift while you orbit to pan, and let it go to orbit again. The speeds apply to the mouse, trackpad, touch and arrow keys.

              3D printer bed

              Check for 3D printing says when a model is larger than this. Pick the size closest to your printer's build volume.

              New models

              New models (New, and the page when it opens) then start with the units, display style and location of the model open now. Kept in this browser only; a file keeps its own.

              First-model tour

              Four short steps: a rectangle, a box, a group, and a check for 3D printing. It starts by itself only the first time.

              From add-ons and files

              Ticked: the layout you pick above is kept for this app alone, and the other apps keep theirs. Unticked: it follows the choice for every app. In SuperCad Studio the layout follows the app you are working in.

              Every app that has the workspace you pick opens in it; the others keep their toolbar and panels. Your toolbar style, menus and keys stay as they are.

              Your own layouts

              A layout is a .ccworkspace.json file: data only, read as untrusted. It arranges the same building blocks in every app; each app fills them with its own tools. Drop a .ccworkspace.json file here to import it.

              Work environments

              Work environments. Save your whole setup under a name (the layout, menu style and keys, toolbar, panels and My tools, for every app) and switch between setups. The theme and the language stay as they are.

              If something went missing

              Lost a toolbar, a menu or a panel?

              Puts the toolbar (order, lock and buttons), the panels, the screen mode and the menu style back to how ModelCraft starts. Your theme, language, sizes and ModelCraft's own settings stay as they are.

              Units

              Global settings: each app can have its own measurement settings and defaults too.

              Switch a unit on to see it in the unit lists. The star marks the default for new drawings, measures and items. These choices are the same in every app in this browser.

              Loading the units…

              A file keeps its own unit (a drawing's units, a project's feet or meters), and that unit stays in its list even when it is turned off here.

              Hardware specs

              Sharing your hardware specs allows us to build more efficient engines. The information is aggregated and not associated with your account: the graphics card, processor cores, memory and screen size, the browser and system family, and the app — no files, no name, no address.

              Full-screen crosshair

              Two thin lines through the pointer across the whole drawing or view, to check that things line up (CURSORSIZE 100). Not with a finger.

              First-time tours

              Off: no app starts its tour by itself. Each app's own settings can still turn its tour off, or take it again.

              Screen

              Sizes and panels

              Text and spacing

              The bars, toolbars, panels and dialogs of ModelCraft, bigger or smaller; the drawing keeps its own zoom. Panel text size works on top of it.

              Compact tightens the row heights, panel padding, toolbar buttons and panel text of ModelCraft, so more fits on the screen. A touch screen keeps its large targets either way.

              Panels

              You can also drag a panel's inner edge. Double-click the edge to put it back.

              Panel widths apply when the panels sit beside the drawing (wider windows).

              Panels

              Drag a panel by its name to dock it left, right or at the bottom, or drop it over the drawing to float it. A new window can go on your other screen. Reset layout puts every panel back where it started.

              Behaviour

              In a panel with several sections, opening one (by clicking its heading or by choosing a tool in the menu) closes the others so the panel stays on what you are doing. The lock beside a heading keeps that section open. Turn this off to open and close sections yourself.

              Add-ons

                Add-ons add features to ModelCraft. Switching one off takes effect at once; some need a reload to go completely.

                Developer mode is on: add-ons that are not signed can load. Only use add-ons you trust.
